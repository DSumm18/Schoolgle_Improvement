Broad Curriculum

A rich, knowledge-based curriculum from EYFS to Year 6, with specialist teaching in music, PE, and modern languages.

❤

Christian Values

Our school is rooted in Christian values of love, respect, and service. We welcome children of all faiths and none.

🎨

Creative Arts

Every child performs in our annual productions, learns an instrument, and has access to our dedicated art studio.

🌱

Outdoor Learning

Our forest school, allotment garden, and nature trail provide hands-on learning in every season.

🤝

Pastoral Care

Small class sizes, dedicated teaching assistants, and our trained ELSA ensure every child is supported.

🏆

Beyond the Classroom

Over 20 after-school clubs including coding, chess, netball, football, gardening, and choir.
