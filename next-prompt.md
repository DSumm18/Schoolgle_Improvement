Create a premium, high-converting landing page for "Schoolgle", the unified platform for school management. 

**VISUAL THEME:** 
Dark Mode Cosmic. Deep midnight backgrounds with subtle vibrant accents and glassmorphism.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Background:** Midnight Space (#0a0a12)
- **Secondary BG:** Deep Slate (#111118)
- **Primary Accent:** Primary Blue (#3b82f6)
- **Secondary Accent:** Amber Glow (#f59e0b)
- **Text:** Off-White (#f8fafc)
- **Secondary Text:** Muted Slate (#94a3b8)
- **Roundness:** Generously rounded corners (radius-2xl)
- **Effects:** Whisper-soft diffused shadows and 12px blur on glass cards.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Hero Section:** High-impact "Mission Control" visualization with a clear "Request Early Access" CTA.
2. **Task Strip:** A horizontal scrolling strip showing real-time school management tasks.
3. **Problem Statement:** Concise section explaining the fragmentation in current school systems.
4. **Value Proposition:** Interactive grid showcasing Schoolgle's unified approach.
5. **Product Showcase:** Visual overview of HR, Finance, and Estates modules.
6. **Trust Section:** GDPR and security reassurance with social proof.
7. **Footer:** Simple links and contact info.
