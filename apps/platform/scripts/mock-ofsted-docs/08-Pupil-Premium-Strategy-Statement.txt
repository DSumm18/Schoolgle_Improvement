STRATEGY STATEMENT

Our Pupil Premium strategy is evidence-based. We draw on the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF) Teaching and Learning Toolkit and the EEF's Pupil Premium guidance (2021) to select interventions. We prioritise approaches with strong evidence and high average impact.

The EEF's three-tiered model underpins our strategy:
1. HIGH-QUALITY TEACHING for all (greatest impact on PP pupils)
2. TARGETED ACADEMIC SUPPORT (structured interventions)
3. WIDER STRATEGIES (attendance, wellbeing, enrichment)

PART B: REVIEW OF PREVIOUS YEAR'S EXPENDITURE (2023-24)

PP allocation 2023-24: £176,400

| Priority                  | Investment | Outcome                                          |
|---------------------------|------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Reading (RWI Fresh Start)  | £4,000    | PP reading ES: 78% → 81%. Gap: -15% → -11%      |

| FLO + breakfast club      | £22,000   | PP attendance: 91.8% → 93.1%. PP PA: 24.2%→22.4%|
| ELSA                      | £4,800    | 94% of ELSA pupils showed improved wellbeing score|

Key learning: Writing gap has not responded sufficiently to the strategies used in 2023-24. In 2024-25 we are adopting the 'Sentence First' programme with stronger structural support for PP pupils. EEF evidence for structured writing approaches: +5 months.
