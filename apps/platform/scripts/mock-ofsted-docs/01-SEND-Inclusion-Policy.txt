1. VISION AND COMMITMENT TO INCLUSION

Grove House Primary School is committed to creating an inclusive environment where every child can achieve their potential. We believe that all pupils, regardless of their needs, have the right to quality education alongside their peers. Our SEND provision is embedded within the school's ethos and is not treated as an add-on.

We follow the SEND Code of Practice (2015) and the Children and Families Act 2014. All staff share responsibility for the progress and wellbeing of pupils with SEND.

---

2. IDENTIFICATION AND ASSESSMENT

We identify pupils with SEND through:
- Entry assessment data (baseline, EYFS Profile, phonics screening)
- Teacher observation and professional judgement
- Parental concerns raised at review meetings
- External referrals (CAMHS, Educational Psychology, Speech and Language Therapy)
- Analysis of in-school progress data each half-term

All pupils with SEND are placed on our SEND register and receive a One Page Profile reviewed termly.

---

3. GRADUATED APPROACH — ASSESS, PLAN, DO, REVIEW

Our graduated approach follows the four-stage cycle mandated by the SEND Code of Practice:

ASSESS: Gather data from class teachers, teaching assistants, parents and the pupil themselves.
PLAN: Create a personalised SEND Support Plan with SMART targets. Parents sign and receive a copy.
DO: Provision is delivered by trained staff. Interventions are timetabled and tracked.
REVIEW: Outcomes are reviewed each term. Progress is discussed with parents at termly reviews.

---

4. EDUCATION, HEALTH AND CARE PLANS (EHCPs)

For pupils with an EHCP, Grove House holds Annual Reviews in line with statutory timescales. Key actions:
- Annual Review meeting held within 12 months of previous review (or last issue/amendment)
- Local Authority notified of review outcomes within two weeks
- Draft EHCP amendments shared with parents within 15 weeks
- EHCPs for Year 6 pupils include transition planning to secondary provision

Current EHCP cohort: 12 pupils (as of September 2024)

6. TRAINING AND CONTINUOUS PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

All teaching staff receive annual SEND awareness training. The SENCO holds the National Award for SEN Coordination. Support staff are trained in:
- Precision Teaching
- Makaton
- PECS (Picture Exchange Communication System)
- Team Teach (de-escalation)

---

7. MONITORING AND EVALUATION

The governing body receives a termly SEND report from the SENCO. Key metrics tracked include:
- Progress of pupils with SEND vs whole-school averages
- Attendance of SEND pupils (target: no more than 2% below school average)
- Complaints or concerns raised by parents
- Outcomes of EHCP Annual Reviews
