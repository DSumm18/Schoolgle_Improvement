PUPIL PREMIUM GAP ANALYSIS

| Subject  | PP ES% | Non-PP ES% | Gap  | National PP Gap |
|----------|--------|------------|------|-----------------|
| Reading  | 81%    | 92%        | -11% | -17%            |
| Writing  | 76%    | 92%        | -16% | -20%            |
| Maths    | 76%    | 88%        | -12% | -18%            |
| RWM Comb | 71%    | 85%        | -14% | -22%            |

The PP gap at Grove House is narrower than the national PP gap in all subjects. This is attributed to targeted EEF-backed interventions including:
- Tuition Partners Programme (1:1 and small group)
- Homework Club and breakfast provision
- Vocabulary enrichment (Tier 2 focus)

Action for 2025-26: Further close the Writing gap (currently 16%) through structured daily writing sessions using the 'Sentence First' approach trialled in Y4 with strong impact (term 3 data).

ABSENCE AND ATTAINMENT CORRELATION

Of the 6 pupils with persistent absence (PA) in KS2:
- 3 achieved Expected Standard in RWM Combined
- 3 did not meet Expected Standard
- Average scaled score for PA group: 99.2 vs 107.8 for non-PA group

This reinforces our attendance strategy priority for 2025-26. PA pupils are disproportionately represented in the lowest attaining group and predominantly from Pupil Premium backgrounds.

---

AREAS FOR DEVELOPMENT (2025-26 SDP Priorities)

1. Writing at Greater Depth: 21% vs 13% national — aim to reach 27% by end of KS2 in 2026.
2. Reduce PP Writing gap from 16% to below 10%.
3. Maintain positive progress scores above +2.0 in all three subjects.
4. Ensure SEND pupils with EHCPs are accessing appropriate external provision.

Analysis verified against DfE ASP data.
