1. STATEMENT OF INTENT

Grove House Primary School is fully committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of every child in our care. We create a culture of safeguarding where all adults — staff, governors, volunteers and contractors — understand their roles and responsibilities. Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility.

This policy applies to all individuals working in or on behalf of the school, including supply staff, volunteers and contractors.

---

2. SAFER RECRUITMENT

All appointments are subject to:
- Enhanced DBS check with Children's Barred List check
- Right to Work in the UK verification
- Reference checks (minimum two, one from current/most recent employer)
- Identity verification
- Prohibition from teaching check (DfE Teacher Services)
- Overseas check where applicable (KCSIE 2024, Annex F)

The school maintains a Single Central Record (SCR) which is reviewed half-termly by the DSL and annually by the named safeguarding governor. The SCR is stored securely and available for inspection at all times.

---

3. REPORTING CONCERNS — STAFF PROCEDURE

All staff are trained to recognise the signs of abuse and neglect (physical, emotional, sexual, neglect). If a member of staff has a concern about a child, they must:

1. Record the concern immediately using the MyConcern online system
2. Report verbally to the DSL (or Deputy DSL if DSL unavailable) on the same day
3. NOT investigate or question the child beyond asking open questions ("Tell me more about that")
4. NOT promise confidentiality
5. NOT share information with parents if to do so could put the child at risk

Concerns are logged within 24 hours. The DSL determines whether to:
- Monitor (record concern, no referral)
- Make an Early Help referral (Bradford Early Help Hub)
- Make a Section 17 (child in need) or Section 47 (child protection) referral to Bradford Children's Services

Threshold of referral: if a child is at risk of significant harm, or the DSL believes a crime may have been committed, a referral to Children's Services is made immediately. The DSL does not require parental consent to make a Section 47 referral.

4. SPECIFIC SAFEGUARDING ISSUES

The DSL receives annual training on all safeguarding issues. Staff receive training on the following topics at least annually:

| Issue                              | Relevant Guidance                          |
|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Child Sexual Exploitation (CSE)    | KCSIE 2024, Part 1                         |
| Child Criminal Exploitation (CCE)  | KCSIE 2024, Part 1                         |
| Domestic abuse                     | Domestic Abuse Act 2021                    |
| Female Genital Mutilation (FGM)    | Multi-Agency Statutory Guidance (2020)     |
| Forced marriage                    | Home Office Forced Marriage Unit guidance  |
| Radicalisation and Prevent         | Counter-Terrorism & Security Act 2015      |
| Online safety and harmful content  | KCSIE 2024, Part 5                         |
| Looked After Children (LAC)        | DfE Statutory Guidance (2023)              |
| Mental health concerns             | KCSIE 2024 — new 2024 emphasis             |

Prevent: The school's Prevent lead is Mrs J. Smith. Staff complete annual online Prevent training (ACT e-learning platform). Any concerns about radicalisation are referred to the Channel programme via Bradford Council.

FGM: The school has a mandatory duty to report known FGM directly to the police (Section 74, Serious Crime Act 2015). The DSL is responsible for this referral — it cannot be delegated.

5. LOW-LEVEL CONCERNS (KCSIE 2024)

Staff must report low-level concerns about any adult's behaviour — including their own — to the Headteacher. Low-level concerns are not necessarily safeguarding referrals but are recorded and monitored for patterns.

Where the concern relates to the Headteacher, it is reported to the Chair of Governors (Mr R. Hussain) directly.

7. ONLINE SAFETY

The school's filtering and monitoring system is managed by Bradford LA (Smoothwall). It meets the requirements of KCSIE 2024 for appropriate filtering and monitoring. The IT Lead reviews filtering reports monthly. Any alert triggered by a pupil is reported to the DSL within 24 hours.

---

8. RECORD KEEPING AND DATA

All safeguarding records are held on MyConcern (cloud, encrypted, Bradford LA approved). Records are:
- Retained until the pupil's 25th birthday (or 26th if SEND)
- Transferred to receiving school on pupil leaving Grove House
- Not included in standard school records transfer — sent separately to DSL
