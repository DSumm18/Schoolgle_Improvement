STRATEGIC CONTEXT

Grove House Primary School serves a diverse community in Bradford BD2. Free School Meal eligibility is 44% (significantly above national average of 24%). 35% of pupils have English as an Additional Language. The school has a strong track record of above-average attainment and progress, and was judged 'Good' in the last Ofsted inspection (March 2022). Our ambition is to achieve 'Outstanding' in the next inspection cycle.

This SDP is built on four strategic priorities identified from:
- Analysis of KS2 outcomes (2024 data)
- Stakeholder consultation (staff, governors, parents, pupils — June 2024)
- Governor monitoring visits (2023-24)
- National and LA improvement priorities

---

PRIORITY 1: RAISE WRITING ATTAINMENT AND CLOSE THE PP WRITING GAP

Context: Writing GD is 21% (national 13%). However, PP Writing gap is 16%, above our aspirational target of 10%.

Success Criteria by July 2026:
- Writing ES combined: 88% (from 85%)
- Writing GD: 27% (from 21%)
- PP writing gap: below 10% (from 16%)

Key Actions:
| Action                                      | Lead         | Timeline        | Resources        |
|---------------------------------------------|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Implement 'Sentence First' programme Yr1-6  | Miss Okafor  | Oct 2024        | CPD budget £2k  |
| Termly writing moderation across all phases | All teachers | Dec, Mar, Jun   | Cover £800      |
| PP pupil writing surgeries (Y4-Y6)          | Mrs Smith    | Weekly          | Staff time       |
| Author visit programme (3 per year)         | Miss Thornton| Nov, Feb, Jun   | Trips budget £1.5k|
| Book audit — diversify reading spine        | All leads    | Jan 2025        | £1,200 books     |

Monitoring: SLT reviews writing data each half-term; governor book scrutiny visit (February)

PRIORITY 3: STRENGTHEN CURRICULUM DEPTH AND TEACHER EXPERTISE

- Pupil voice surveys show 85%+ can articulate what they are learning and why
