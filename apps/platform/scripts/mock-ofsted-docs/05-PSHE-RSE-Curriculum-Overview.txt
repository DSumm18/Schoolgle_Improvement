---

VISION

At Grove House, PSHE and RSHE sit at the heart of our personal development curriculum. We equip pupils with the knowledge, values and attributes to lead healthy, safe and fulfilling lives — as citizens of Bradford, of Britain, and of the world. Our programme reflects the diversity of our school community and is underpinned by our school values: Respect, Resilience, Kindness, Ambition.

---

STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS

Since September 2020, Relationships Education (primary) and Health Education are statutory. Sex Education is not compulsory at primary level; however, the DfE recommends primary schools consider including it as part of their RSHE provision. After consultation with parents and the governing body (June 2024), Grove House teaches age-appropriate content about reproduction and puberty in Years 5 and 6.

Parents have the right to withdraw their child from the Sex Education elements (not the statutory Relationships or Health Education elements) by written request to the Headteacher.

BRITISH VALUES

All PSHE lessons explicitly connect to at least one of the five British Values, as required by the Prevent duty (Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015):

1. DEMOCRACY — School council, class debates, local democracy projects (Y5/6 Bradford Council link)
2. RULE OF LAW — Class charters, consequences, discussion of laws that protect us
3. INDIVIDUAL LIBERTY — Choice, self-expression, celebrating difference
4. MUTUAL RESPECT AND TOLERANCE — Celebrating our diverse community, anti-discrimination, faith perspectives
5. EQUALITY — Gender, race, disability, belief — taught positively and age-appropriately

ONLINE SAFETY

Online safety is woven throughout the curriculum and taught explicitly in Computing and PSHE:
- Annual Safer Internet Day (February) — whole-school theme
- Year 3-6: annual Childnet International workshop
- Digital citizenship charter displayed in every classroom
- Parent workshop: Keeping Children Safe Online (annually, October)

---

MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING

Grove House holds the Wellbeing Award for Schools (2024). We provide:
- Daily mindfulness (5 minutes, all classes, using Headspace for Schools)
- ELSA (Emotional Literacy Support Assistant) — Mrs K. Ford, trained 2023
- Mental Health Lead: Mrs J. Smith (Anna Freud Centre accredited, 2024)
- Forest School: half-termly for all KS1 classes; termly for KS2
- Pupil Wellbeing Survey: conducted each spring term (Years 2-6)

In the 2024 wellbeing survey (n=187):
- 89% of pupils reported feeling safe at school
- 84% said they had an adult they could talk to if worried
- 73% rated their happiness at school as 4 or 5 out of 5

---

EQUALITY, DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION

Our PSHE curriculum explicitly teaches about protected characteristics under the Equality Act 2010. We use texts from diverse authors and ensure representation across gender, ethnicity, religion and family structure. All year groups study at least one unit that celebrates our multicultural community.
