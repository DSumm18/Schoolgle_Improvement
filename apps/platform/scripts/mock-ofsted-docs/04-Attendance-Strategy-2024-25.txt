CONTEXT AND RATIONALE

Following the DfE's revised Working Together to Improve School Attendance guidance (August 2024), Grove House has refreshed its whole-school attendance strategy. National data shows persistent absence (PA) — defined as missing 10% or more of sessions — is strongly correlated with lower attainment outcomes, particularly in the post-COVID period.

Grove House 2023-24 attendance data:
- Overall attendance: 94.8% (national primary: 94.5%)
- Persistent absence rate: 16.2% (national primary: 19.1%)
- Authorised absence: 3.1%
- Unauthorised absence: 2.1%

While overall attendance is above national average, our PA rate — though below national — affects a disproportionate number of PP and SEND pupils.

TIERED SUPPORT FRAMEWORK

UNIVERSAL (all pupils)
- Attendance celebrated weekly in Celebration Assembly (100% certificates, class competition)
- Daily text alerts to parents for unregistered absence by 9:15am (Arbor MIS)
- Termly attendance newsletters to all families
- Visible attendance data in reception — weekly whole-school percentage displayed

TARGETED (pupils 90-94%)
- First-day calling for unexplained absence (admin team)
- Mrs L. Hughes contacts family within 3 days of unexplained absences
- Attendance Action Plan agreed with family (voluntary, informative tone)
- Referral to school counsellor where anxiety or wellbeing concerns identified

INTENSIVE (pupils below 90% / PA)
- Formal Attendance Meeting with parent/carer and FLO
- Home visit within two weeks if contact not established
- Referral to Bradford Education Welfare Service where appropriate
- Fixed Penalty Notice (FPN) pathway initiated for term-time holidays after warning letter
- Weekly monitoring by Headteacher — named pupils on PA watch list reviewed each Monday

The Pupil Premium Strategy includes funding for the Family Liaison Officer role (0.6 FTE), breakfast club provision (free to PP pupils), and a small transport fund for families facing barriers to regular attendance.

---

HOLIDAYS IN TERM TIME

Grove House follows Bradford LA guidance. Holidays will not be authorised except in exceptional circumstances. All requests are reviewed by the Headteacher individually. FPNs are issued in line with the DfE's national framework (effective August 2024): first offence £80 per parent per child (rising to £160 if unpaid in 21 days); second offence within 3 years £160.

---

MONITORING AND ACCOUNTABILITY

- Class teachers review registers daily; discrepancies reported to admin by 9:00am
- SLT reviews weekly attendance data every Monday via Arbor dashboard
- Half-termly report to Governing Body (Pupil Wellbeing Committee)
- Annual summary to Full Governing Body (July)
- External audit by Bradford Attendance Partnership — annual
