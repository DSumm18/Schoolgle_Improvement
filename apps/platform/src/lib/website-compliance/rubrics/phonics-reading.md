# Phonics & Reading (Primary)

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- National Curriculum in England: English programmes of study (2014)
- Reading Framework (DfE, July 2023)
- Ofsted Education Inspection Framework 2025 (reading focus)

## Current References

Must reference the school's current validated Systematic Synthetic Phonics
(SSP) programme. DfE publishes a list of validated SSP programmes; the
school should be using one from that list. If the school names a programme
not on the validated list, this is a concern.

The DfE Reading Framework (2023) is the current guidance document. Schools
should align their approach with this framework.

## Review Frequency

No specific statutory review cycle, but curriculum information must be
kept current for each academic year. Phonics and reading information
should reflect the school's current practice.

## Required Content

1. Name of the phonics programme used — must be a DfE-validated Systematic
   Synthetic Phonics programme (e.g. Little Wandle Letters and Sounds Revised,
   Read Write Inc, Floppy's Phonics, Twinkl Phonics, Essential Letters and
   Sounds)
2. How phonics is taught — frequency, duration, grouping approach
3. How reading is taught across the school — from early reading through to
   fluency and comprehension
4. Reading spine or core book list — the books children read as part of
   the curriculum
5. How the school supports struggling readers and those who fall behind,
   including intervention approaches
6. How decodable books are matched to the phonics programme (i.e. children
   read books that match the sounds they have been taught)

## Quality Indicators

- Book lists organised by year group or reading stage
- Reading for pleasure strategy — how the school promotes a love of reading
  beyond phonics and decoding
- Named reading lead or phonics lead
- Information about how parents can support reading at home
- Details of the school library provision
- Author visits, reading events, or book-related enrichment activities
- Explanation of how the school monitors reading progress
- Mention of the Phonics Screening Check (Year 1) and how results inform
  teaching
- Details of reading provision in KS2 (whole-class reading, guided reading,
  or similar approach)
- Information about how EAL pupils are supported with reading

## Red Flags

- No mention of phonics or reading approach on the website
- Phonics programme named is not on the DfE validated list
- School describes using multiple phonics schemes simultaneously (suggests
  lack of fidelity to a single programme, contrary to DfE guidance)
- No mention of how struggling readers are supported
- Reading information is limited to EYFS/KS1 with nothing about KS2 reading
- Book lists are clearly outdated or contain no recent titles
- No decodable books mentioned — suggests mismatch between phonics teaching
  and reading practice
- Website describes a "mixed methods" approach to early reading rather than
  systematic synthetic phonics

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Named validated SSP programme clearly stated
- Description of how phonics is taught (frequency, grouping, progression)
- Reading approach across the school is explained
- Book list or reading spine is published
- Support for struggling readers is described
- Decodable book approach is mentioned
- Information is current for the academic year

### Partial (40-79)

- Reading or phonics information exists but one or more of:
  - Phonics programme not named or not from the validated list
  - No book list or reading spine published
  - No mention of how struggling readers are supported
  - Only covers EYFS/KS1 with no KS2 reading information
  - Information is vague ("We love reading!") without specific detail
  - Page exists but is sparse with minimal practical information

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No phonics or reading information found on the website
- Only a generic curriculum page with no specific reading content
- Phonics/reading page exists but is empty or has placeholder text
- Information describes an approach that contradicts DfE guidance
  (e.g. "We use a whole-language approach" with no phonics)

## Notes

- This requirement applies to PRIMARY schools only. Secondary schools do
  not need to publish phonics information, though they should publish their
  approach to reading across the curriculum.
- The DfE validated SSP list is updated periodically. As of 2025, the main
  validated programmes include: Little Wandle Letters and Sounds Revised,
  Read Write Inc (Ruth Miskin), Floppy's Phonics, Twinkl Phonics, Essential
  Letters and Sounds, Sounds-Write, and several others.
- Ofsted places extremely high emphasis on reading during inspections of
  primary schools. The deep dive into reading is almost always selected.
  A well-published reading page signals preparedness.
- Many schools publish this under "Curriculum > English" or "Curriculum >
  Phonics" or as a standalone "Reading" page. All are acceptable.
- Schools that have recently changed phonics programmes should explain the
  transition and timeline.
- "Letters and Sounds" (the original 2007 DfE programme) is NOT on the
  validated list. Schools still referencing it without naming a validated
  replacement are likely non-compliant with DfE expectations.
