# Admissions Arrangements

## Legislation

- School Admissions Code 2021 (statutory, came into force 1 September 2021)
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) Regulations 2012
- Education Act 1996, Section 411
- Equality Act 2010 (non-discrimination in admissions)

## Current References

Must reference the School Admissions Code 2021. This is the current statutory
code and replaced the 2014 version. Admissions arrangements must be determined
by 28 February each year for entry the following September. The published
arrangements should clearly state the academic year they apply to (e.g.
"Admissions Policy 2027/28 — for entry September 2027").

## Review Frequency

Annually. Own-admission-authority schools (academies, free schools, voluntary
aided, foundation) must determine arrangements by 28 February each year.
Community and voluntary controlled schools have arrangements determined by the
local authority, but must still publish them on the school website.

Consultation is required when changes are proposed, or at least once every
7 years even if no changes are made.

## Required Content

1. Published Admission Number (PAN) for each relevant year group
2. Oversubscription criteria listed in priority order
3. Explanation of each criterion (e.g. how "distance" is measured, definition of "sibling")
4. Catchment area or designated area description (if applicable), with map or boundary description
5. How to apply — link to local authority common application form or school's own form
6. Application deadlines (national closing date: 15 January for secondary, 15 January for primary)
7. Appeals process — how to appeal, who to contact, and right of appeal
8. Waiting list policy — how the waiting list operates and how long it is maintained
9. In-year admissions process — how to apply outside normal admissions round
10. Fair Access Protocol reference
11. Special educational needs — children with an EHCP naming the school are admitted
12. Looked-after and previously looked-after children priority (highest oversubscription criterion)
13. Supplementary information form (if used, e.g. for faith-based criteria)
14. The academic year the arrangements apply to
15. Date arrangements were determined and by whom (governing body/trust board)

## Quality Indicators

- Clear, jargon-free language accessible to all parents
- Worked examples showing how oversubscription criteria are applied
- Map of catchment area or defined area (not just a text description)
- Downloadable PDF version alongside HTML page
- Previous years' arrangements also available for reference
- Key dates highlighted in a timeline or table format
- Translated versions or "How to apply" guide for EAL families
- Link to local authority coordinated scheme
- Frequently asked questions (FAQ) section
- Named contact for admissions enquiries with phone and email
- Statistics from previous years (applications received, places offered, distance of last place)
- Cross-reference to appeals timetable on the same page or with clear link

## Red Flags

- No admissions information on the website at all
- Oversubscription criteria not listed or not in priority order
- No PAN (Published Admission Number) stated
- Arrangements are for a previous academic year with no current year published
- Discriminatory criteria (e.g. parental occupation, financial status, interview performance)
- Faith-based criteria without published supplementary form
- No mention of looked-after children priority (statutory first criterion)
- No information on how to appeal
- "[INSERT]" placeholder text or generic template
- Policy is behind a login wall
- Catchment area claimed but no map or boundary description provided
- No reference to in-year admissions process
- Arrangements determined after 28 February deadline

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Current year's admissions arrangements are published and clearly dated
- PAN is stated for each relevant year group
- Oversubscription criteria are listed in priority order with looked-after children first
- How to apply, appeals information, and in-year process are all covered
- Catchment/area information included where applicable
- Arrangements appear to have been determined by 28 February
- All 15 required content areas substantially addressed

### Partial (40-79)

- Admissions information is present but one or more of:
  - PAN is missing or unclear
  - Oversubscription criteria listed but not clearly in priority order
  - Missing appeals information or in-year admissions process
  - Arrangements are for the current year but 2-3 required elements are absent
  - Catchment area referenced but not described or mapped
  - No date of determination stated

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No admissions arrangements published on the website
- Only a link to the local authority with no school-specific information
- Arrangements are more than one year out of date
- Missing more than 5 required content areas
- Oversubscription criteria include discriminatory elements
- Information is behind a login wall or password-protected
- Only a downloadable PDF with no HTML summary

## Notes

- Community and voluntary controlled schools have their arrangements
  determined by the local authority, but the School Information Regulations
  still require these to be published on the school website.
- Own-admission-authority schools (academies, free schools, foundation,
  voluntary aided) determine their own arrangements and must publish them.
- Faith schools may use faith-based oversubscription criteria but must
  publish any supplementary information form (SIF) on their website.
- Grammar schools must explain their selection (11+) process, including
  how tests work, qualifying scores, and how distance or other criteria
  apply after the test stage.
- The first oversubscription criterion must always be looked-after children
  and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from care
  or subject to child arrangements orders). This is a legal requirement.
- Schools in a multi-academy trust may have a trust-wide admissions policy.
  The individual school website should still publish the arrangements that
  apply specifically to that school.
- Between 28 February determination and 15 March, objections can be referred
  to the Schools Adjudicator. Schools should not alter determined
  arrangements except to give effect to mandatory requirements or correct errors.
