# Contact Details

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Paragraph 3
- Education (School Information) (England) Regulations 2012
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025 (for academies — must publish trust contact details)

## Current References

No specific dated references required. Contact details must simply be
accurate and current. Telephone numbers, email addresses, and postal
addresses should be verified regularly.

## Review Frequency

Verified at least annually. Updated immediately upon any change of address,
telephone number, or email address. Academy trusts should also update contact
details when there is a change of registered office.

## Required Content

1. Full school name (as registered with the DfE)
2. Postal address including full postcode
3. Telephone number (landline or main reception number)
4. Email address (general school email or enquiries address)
5. For academies: name and registered address of the trust, plus trust contact details
6. Information must be publicly accessible without login

## Quality Indicators

- Embedded map or link to mapping service (Google Maps, Bing Maps)
- School opening hours and office hours clearly stated
- Named contacts for key enquiry types (admissions, SEND, safeguarding, absence reporting)
- Separate phone numbers or extensions for different departments
- Fax number if applicable (increasingly rare but some schools retain)
- Social media links (Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram) alongside official contacts
- Dedicated "Contact Us" page rather than footer-only placement
- Contact form for online enquiries
- Directions or travel information (nearest bus routes, parking)
- Out-of-hours or emergency contact information
- Email addresses use the school domain (not personal Gmail/Hotmail accounts)

## Red Flags

- No contact details found on the website
- Missing postal address (only email or phone provided)
- Missing telephone number
- Missing email address
- Email address uses a personal domain (e.g. gmail.com, hotmail.com) rather than school domain
- Postal address does not match GIAS records
- Phone number is disconnected or incorrect
- Contact page returns a 404 error
- Contact details only in a downloadable document, not on an HTML page
- Academy trust contact details missing (required for academies)
- "[INSERT ADDRESS]" or template placeholder text

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- School name, postal address, telephone number, and email are all clearly published
- Information is on an HTML page (typically a "Contact Us" page or site footer)
- Details are accurate and match DfE/GIAS records
- For academies: trust name and contact details are also published
- Publicly accessible without login
- Bonus: map, opening hours, named contacts for key areas

### Partial (40-79)

- Contact details are present but one or more of:
  - Missing one element (e.g. no email, or no telephone number)
  - Details only appear in the site footer with no dedicated contact page
  - Postal address is incomplete (e.g. missing postcode)
  - Academy trust details are missing (for academy schools)
  - Contact information appears outdated or inconsistent across pages

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No contact details found on the public website
- Only a contact form with no address, phone, or email visible
- Missing two or more of: school name, postal address, telephone, email
- Contact details are behind a login wall
- Website is a placeholder or under construction with no contact information

## Notes

- Contact details are among the most fundamental statutory requirements.
  Ofsted inspectors routinely check this on Day 1 of inspection.
- Many schools place contact details in the website footer, which means
  they appear on every page. This is acceptable and often preferred, but
  a dedicated "Contact Us" page is also expected.
- Academy trusts must publish the trust's name, registered office address,
  and contact details in addition to the individual school's details.
  This is an Academy Trust Handbook requirement.
- Free schools and UTCs sometimes use temporary addresses during their
  pre-opening phase — once open, the permanent address must be published.
- Schools with multiple sites should list all site addresses, making clear
  which is the main school office for correspondence.
- The school name should match the DfE-registered name. Trading names or
  abbreviated names are acceptable alongside the official name but should
  not replace it entirely.
