# Filtering and Monitoring

## Legislation

- Keeping Children Safe in Education 2025 (Part 2: Management of
  Safeguarding, paragraphs 141-148)
- DfE Meeting Digital and Technology Standards in Schools and Colleges 2023
- UK Safer Internet Centre guidance on appropriate filtering and monitoring

## Current References

Must reference Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE) 2025. Any
reference to KCSIE 2024 or earlier without also referencing 2025
indicates the information has not been updated. KCSIE 2025 strengthened
requirements around filtering and monitoring, requiring schools to
publish their approach.

Should also reference the DfE's "Meeting Digital and Technology Standards
in Schools and Colleges" guidance (2023) which sets out specific
technical standards for filtering and monitoring systems.

## Review Frequency

Annually at minimum, as part of the annual safeguarding policy review
cycle. Should also be reviewed when filtering or monitoring systems
change, after any safeguarding incident involving online activity, or
when KCSIE is updated. The DSL should review effectiveness regularly
(at least termly).

## Required Content

1. What filtering system the school uses (named product/provider, e.g.
   Smoothwall, Securly, Lightspeed, RM SafetyNet, Netsweeper) and
   confirmation it meets DfE filtering standards
2. What monitoring system the school uses (named product/provider, e.g.
   Senso, Securly, Smoothwall Monitor, Microsoft Defender) and how
   it identifies concerning online behaviour
3. Who is responsible for overseeing filtering and monitoring (named
   DSL or senior leader, not just a job title)
4. How the effectiveness of filtering and monitoring is reviewed,
   including frequency and method of review
5. How staff are trained to understand and support the filtering and
   monitoring approach, including how to escalate concerns flagged
   by monitoring systems
6. What the school does when filtering or monitoring identifies a
   concern (escalation process)
7. How the school manages filtering for different user groups (staff,
   pupils, guests) if applicable

## Quality Indicators

- Named filtering and monitoring products with version/tier information
- Confirmation that the filtering system meets the DfE Filtering
  Standards (or explanation of how it is being brought into compliance)
- Review log or summary of recent effectiveness reviews
- Staff training schedule and evidence of completion
- Description of how monitoring alerts are triaged and by whom
- Explanation of how the school balances safeguarding with privacy
- Reference to the UK Safer Internet Centre checklist
- Bypass procedures documented (who can override filtering and under
  what circumstances)
- Information about how BYOD (bring your own device) is managed
- Specific categories of content that are filtered
- Process for staff to request unblocking of legitimate educational sites

## Red Flags

- No mention of filtering or monitoring on the school website
- School names a filtering product that is known to be discontinued
  or non-compliant with DfE standards
- No named person responsible for oversight
- No reference to KCSIE or safeguarding context
- States that filtering/monitoring is "managed by the LA" or "managed
  by our IT provider" with no school-level oversight described
- No evidence of effectiveness review
- Only mentions filtering, with no reference to monitoring (both are
  required under KCSIE 2025)
- Information predates KCSIE 2023 strengthened requirements

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Both filtering and monitoring systems named
- Named responsible person (DSL or senior leader)
- Review process for effectiveness described
- Staff training approach outlined
- Escalation process for concerns included
- References KCSIE 2025 or DfE filtering standards
- Current and school-specific content

### Partial (40-79)

- Filtering mentioned but monitoring not addressed (or vice versa)
- Systems named but no responsible person identified
- No review process described
- Generic statements about online safety without specific system details
- References KCSIE but an older edition
- Staff training mentioned but no detail

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No information about filtering or monitoring on the website
- Only a generic online safety policy with no filtering/monitoring detail
- No systems named, no responsible person, no review process
- Information is clearly outdated (pre-2023)
- School appears to have no filtering or monitoring in place

## Notes

- This is a relatively new publication requirement, strengthened
  significantly in KCSIE 2023 and further in 2025. Many schools are
  still catching up with publishing this information.
- The information is often found within the school's Online Safety
  Policy, Safeguarding Policy, or on a dedicated online safety page.
  It does not need to be a standalone document but must be clearly
  accessible to parents.
- KCSIE 2025 is clear that filtering and monitoring are school
  leadership responsibilities, not just IT responsibilities. The DSL
  should have oversight even if technical management is outsourced.
- Schools should be able to demonstrate that their filtering meets the
  DfE Filtering Standards. The UK Safer Internet Centre provides a
  self-assessment checklist.
- Schools in MATs may use trust-wide systems, but each school should
  still publish their own information about how the systems operate
  in their specific context.
- Ofsted inspectors routinely check filtering and monitoring during
  inspections and may ask to see the published information.
