# Off-Payroll Arrangements Disclosure

## Legislation

- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.4
- Academies Accounts Direction (annual, issued by ESFA)
- IR35 — Intermediaries Legislation (Income Tax (Earnings and
  Pensions) Act 2003, Chapter 8)

## Current References

Must disclose any off-payroll arrangements for staff or senior
leaders engaged through personal service companies or other
intermediaries. The disclosure should cover the most recent financial
year, aligned with the published annual accounts.

## Review Frequency

Annually, updated when new audited accounts are published (by 31
December each year). Must reflect the current financial year's
arrangements.

## Required Content

1. A statement confirming whether any off-payroll arrangements exist
2. If arrangements exist, details including:
   a. Number of individuals engaged via off-payroll arrangements
   b. The roles or functions covered by off-payroll arrangements
   c. Assurance that the trust has considered IR35 status for each
   engagement
   d. How the trust assures itself that arrangements are appropriate
3. If no off-payroll arrangements exist, an explicit "nil" statement
4. The financial period the disclosure covers

## Quality Indicators

- Clear, explicit statement on the trust website (not just buried
  in accounts)
- IR35 assessment process described
- Board oversight of off-payroll arrangements documented
- Named committee or individual responsible for reviewing arrangements
- Duration of any off-payroll arrangements stated
- Justification for why off-payroll engagement is used rather than
  direct employment
- Confirmation that HMRC's Check Employment Status for Tax (CEST)
  tool has been used
- Previous year comparison data

## Red Flags

- No mention of off-payroll arrangements anywhere on website or in
  accounts
- Trust clearly uses consultants or interim staff but makes no
  off-payroll disclosure
- Statement is ambiguous — neither confirms nor denies off-payroll
  arrangements
- Arrangements disclosed but no IR35 consideration mentioned
- Senior leaders (CEO, CFO, Headteacher) engaged off-payroll without
  explicit board approval and disclosure
- Disclosure is more than one year out of date

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Clear statement on off-payroll arrangements published
- If arrangements exist: number, roles, and IR35 status covered
- If no arrangements: explicit "nil" or "none" statement
- Covers the most recent financial year
- Published in accounts and/or on the trust website

### Partial (40-79)

- Disclosure exists but:
  - Is one year out of date
  - Only appears in the accounts notes, not findable on website
  - Vague statement without specifying numbers or roles
  - IR35 consideration not mentioned
  - States arrangements exist but provides no detail

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No off-payroll disclosure found on website or in accounts
- Disclosure is 2+ years out of date
- Trust uses off-payroll arrangements but makes no disclosure
- Only a general statement about employment practices with no
  specific off-payroll information

## Notes

- This requirement applies to ACADEMIES ONLY. Maintained schools'
  off-payroll arrangements are managed through the local authority.
- Off-payroll arrangements include staff engaged through personal
  service companies (PSCs), agencies, or other intermediaries where
  the individual would be an employee if engaged directly.
- The Academy Trust Handbook requires that trusts obtain ESFA
  approval before engaging any individual off-payroll who would
  otherwise be a senior employee.
- ESFA has taken enforcement action against trusts with undisclosed
  off-payroll arrangements, particularly for senior leaders.
- IR35 rules (reformed in the public sector since April 2017)
  require the engaging organisation (the trust) to determine
  whether the off-payroll worker would be an employee if engaged
  directly. The trust is liable for tax and NICs if the
  determination is incorrect.
- Many trusts legitimately report "nil" off-payroll arrangements.
  This is perfectly acceptable — the requirement is for
  transparency either way.
- The disclosure is typically found in the notes to the annual
  accounts alongside the staff costs note.
- Common legitimate off-payroll roles include interim headteachers,
  specialist consultants, and IT contractors. However, ongoing
  arrangements for core roles are scrutinised more closely.
