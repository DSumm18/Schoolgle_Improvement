# Academy Trust Information

## Legislation

- Academy Trust Handbook 2025 (ESFA)
- Academies Act 2010
- Articles of Association (model articles)
- Funding Agreement (between the trust and the Secretary of State)
- Companies Act 2006 (academy trusts are companies limited by guarantee)

## Current References

Must comply with the Academy Trust Handbook 2025 (previously the
Academies Financial Handbook). The Handbook is updated annually and
schools should reference the current edition. Any reference to the
"Academies Financial Handbook" (renamed in 2021) suggests the
information has not been reviewed recently.

Academy trusts must also comply with company law requirements for
disclosure under the Companies Act 2006.

## Review Frequency

Updated whenever governance arrangements change (new members, trustees,
or local governors appointed or departing). Must be reviewed at least
annually. The trust's annual governance statement should be published
within the annual accounts, and the website should reflect the current
position at all times.

## Required Content

1. Names of all members of the academy trust (the individuals or
   corporate entities that are the legal members, equivalent to
   shareholders)
2. Names of all trustees (directors of the trust board)
3. Names of all local governors (members of any local governing bodies
   or academy committees), with the school(s) they are associated with
4. The structure of the trust's governance — clear description or
   diagram showing the relationship between members, trustees, and
   local governing bodies
5. The trust's Articles of Association (published document or link)
6. The Funding Agreement (published document or link to the DfE's
   published version)
7. Scheme of Delegation — showing which governance functions are
   delegated to which level (trust board, committees, local governing
   bodies, executive leaders)

## Quality Indicators

- Governance structure diagram (visual representation of the trust)
- Pen portraits or biographies for members and trustees
- Terms of office and appointment dates for trustees and local governors
- Declaration of business and personal interests for trustees
- Attendance records for board and committee meetings
- Clerk to the board named with contact details
- Trust's annual report and accounts published
- Register of interests published (linked to family-relationships-register)
- Committee structures with terms of reference
- Information about how to contact the trust board (e.g. via the clerk)
- Trustee recruitment information
- Explanation of the role and responsibilities of each governance tier

## Red Flags

- No governance information published on the website
- Members not listed (members are often forgotten — only trustees shown)
- Governance structure unclear or missing
- Articles of Association not published or linked
- Funding Agreement not published or linked
- Scheme of Delegation missing
- Outdated information (lists former trustees who have resigned)
- Trust claims to have no members (all trusts must have members)
- No distinction between members and trustees (different roles)
- Related party interests not disclosed
- Single individual listed as both member and trustee (Academy Trust
  Handbook limits this to one person maximum)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- All members, trustees, and local governors named
- Governance structure clearly described or diagrammed
- Articles of Association published or linked
- Funding Agreement published or linked
- Scheme of Delegation published
- Information is current (reflects current governance arrangements)
- Clear distinction between members, trustees, and local governors

### Partial (40-79)

- Trustees listed but members omitted
- Governance structure vague or partially described
- Some key documents missing (e.g. Scheme of Delegation absent)
- Local governors listed but not linked to specific schools
- Information appears outdated but not significantly so
- Articles or Funding Agreement referenced but not published/linked

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No trust governance information on the website
- No members or trustees listed
- No governance documents published (no articles, no scheme of delegation)
- Information is clearly outdated (names individuals who left 2+ years ago)
- School website makes no reference to the trust structure at all

## Notes

- This requirement applies to ACADEMIES AND FREE SCHOOLS ONLY.
  Maintained schools have different governance publication requirements
  under the School Governance (Roles, Procedures and Allowances)
  Regulations 2013.
- Members and trustees are different roles with different responsibilities.
  Members are the "owners" of the trust (appoint/remove trustees, amend
  articles). Trustees are the directors who run the trust. The Academy
  Trust Handbook limits member-trustee overlap.
- Single academy trusts (SATs) still need to publish this information.
  The trust is the legal entity, even if it only runs one school.
- Multi-academy trusts (MATs) should publish trust-level information
  on the trust website, but each school website should also provide
  governance information relevant to that school (including local
  governing body membership).
- The Scheme of Delegation is a key document that shows how decision-
  making is distributed. It is frequently missing from school websites.
- Academy trusts are companies limited by guarantee registered at
  Companies House. Basic governance information is also available
  there, but the trust must publish it on its own website.
- The Governance Handbook (DfE) provides additional guidance on
  publication requirements for all school types.
