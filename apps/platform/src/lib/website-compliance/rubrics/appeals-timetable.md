# Appeals Timetable

## Legislation

- School Admissions Code 2021, Paragraphs 2.25–2.29
- School Admission Appeals Code 2012
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- Education Act 1998, Section 94 (right of appeal)

## Current References

Must reference the School Admissions Code 2021 and/or the School Admission
Appeals Code 2012 (which remains the current appeals code). The timetable
must relate to the current or upcoming admissions round. An appeals timetable
for a past year without a current one indicates non-compliance.

## Review Frequency

Annually. The appeals timetable must be published alongside the determined
admissions arrangements. It should be updated each year to reflect the
specific dates for that admissions cycle. Key dates should be published
well in advance of the appeals deadline.

## Required Content

1. Deadline for lodging an appeal (must allow at least 20 school days from the date of notification of refusal)
2. Date by which appeal hearings will be held (within 40 school days of the deadline for making appeals for normal-round appeals)
3. Who to contact to make an appeal (name, address, email, phone)
4. How to submit an appeal (online form, letter, email)
5. Statement that parents have the right to appeal for every school at which they were refused a place
6. Information about the independent appeal panel process
7. For infant class size appeals: explanation that different grounds apply
8. Clear indication of the admissions year the timetable relates to

## Quality Indicators

- Timeline or visual format showing key dates at a glance
- Step-by-step guide to the appeals process
- What to include in an appeal (guidance on presenting a case)
- What happens at the hearing — description of the panel format
- Links to independent advice (e.g. local authority, Coram Children's Legal Centre, ACE)
- Information about the role of the clerk to the appeal panel
- Guidance on multiple appeals (applying to more than one school)
- Expected timeframe for the decision letter after the hearing
- Information about further recourse (Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman)
- Translated versions or plain English summary for EAL families
- Downloadable appeal form

## Red Flags

- No appeals timetable published
- Timetable relates to a previous year with no current year published
- No deadline date for lodging appeals
- No hearing date or date range
- No contact details for submitting an appeal
- Discouraging language that implies appeals are unlikely to succeed
- Suggestion that parents cannot or should not appeal
- Appeals deadline of fewer than 20 school days from refusal notification
- No reference to infant class size regulations for Reception appeals
- Appeals process described as going to the headteacher or governors directly (should be independent panel)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Appeals timetable is published with clear dates for the current admissions round
- Deadline for lodging appeals and hearing dates are stated
- Contact details for submitting an appeal are provided
- Right of appeal is clearly communicated
- Infant class size appeal differences noted (for primary/infant schools)
- All 8 required content areas substantially addressed

### Partial (40-79)

- Some appeals information is present but one or more of:
  - Timetable dates are vague (e.g. "Spring term" without specific dates)
  - Missing contact details for the appeals body
  - No distinction between normal-round and in-year appeals
  - Information about appeals exists but not in a timetable/dated format
  - Timetable is for the current year but missing 2-3 required elements

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No appeals timetable or appeals information found
- Appeals timetable is more than one year out of date
- Only a vague reference to "parents can appeal" with no process or dates
- Information is behind a login wall
- Appeals process described incorrectly (e.g. appeal to the headteacher)
- Missing more than 4 required content areas

## Notes

- The requirement to publish an appeals timetable applies primarily to
  own-admission-authority schools (academies, free schools, voluntary aided,
  foundation schools). Community and voluntary controlled schools have their
  appeals managed by the local authority, but should still signpost parents
  to the LA appeals process.
- For normal admissions round (Reception and Year 7 entry), appeals must be
  heard within 40 school days of the appeal deadline. For in-year appeals,
  they should be heard within 30 school days of the appeal being lodged.
- Infant class size appeals (Reception, Year 1, Year 2) have more restricted
  grounds — the panel can only uphold the appeal if the admission decision
  was unlawful, unreasonable, or not made in accordance with the published
  arrangements. Schools should explain this distinction.
- Many academy trusts contract their appeals to the local authority or an
  independent appeals service. The school website should still provide the
  timetable and contact details regardless of who administers the appeals.
- The appeals timetable is often published on the same page as the admissions
  arrangements. This is good practice but it should be clearly visible and
  not buried within a lengthy document.
- Schools sometimes confuse internal "reconsideration" processes with formal
  independent appeals. The statutory right is to an independent appeal panel,
  not to a review by the school itself.
