# Governance Information

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 2
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Sections 1.1-1.56 (for academies)
- School Governance (Constitution) (England) Regulations 2012 (for maintained)
- School Governance (Roles, Procedures and Allowances) (England) Regulations 2013
- Academies Financial Handbook / Academy Trust Handbook (for academies)

## Current References

Must reflect the current academic year's governance structure.
For academies, must align with Academy Trust Handbook 2025 requirements.
For maintained schools, must comply with the constitution regulations.

## Review Frequency

Updated when governance membership changes. Full review annually at the
start of each academic year. Pecuniary interests register updated within
28 days of any change.

## Required Content

### All Schools

1. Governance structure — how the governing body/board is organised
2. Names of all governors/trustees (full names, not initials)
3. Category of each governor (parent, staff, co-opted, LA, foundation, etc.)
4. Date of appointment for each governor
5. Term of office for each governor (and end date)
6. Who appointed each governor
7. Any committees and their membership
8. Attendance record at governing body/committee meetings (current academic year)
9. Register of business and pecuniary interests for ALL governors/trustees:
   a. Relevant business and financial interests
   b. Details of any governance roles in other educational institutions
   c. Any material interests arising from relationships between governors/trustees
   or between governors and school staff (including spouses/partners)
10. Chair of governors/trustees name and contact method

### Academies (additional requirements)

11. Names of Members of the academy trust
12. Names of Trustees / Directors
13. Names of any Local Governing Body members
14. Accounting Officer name
15. Chief Financial Officer / Chief Operating Officer name
16. Link to Articles of Association
17. Funding Agreement (or link to DfE register)
18. Scheme of Delegation — showing which decisions are made at which level
    (Members → Trustees → CEO → LGB → Headteacher)

## Quality Indicators

- Photos of governors/trustees
- Brief biography or background for each governor
- Link governor/trustee responsibilities listed (e.g. safeguarding, SEND, PP)
- Governor/trustee training record
- Governor visit reports published
- Minutes of meetings published (or summary)
- Clear visual showing the governance structure (organogram)
- Contact details for the clerk to governors
- DBS check status confirmed
- Skills audit or skills matrix published

## Red Flags

- Governor names not published (privacy is not a valid exemption for governors)
- No pecuniary interests register published
- Pecuniary interests all marked "None" or "N/A" without proper declaration
- Attendance records not published
- Governance page is password-protected
- Governance information is more than one academic year out of date
- Governor has served beyond their term of office with no reappointment recorded
- No chair of governors named
- Academy trust with no scheme of delegation published
- Academy trust with no articles of association accessible
- Governance structure diagram missing or out of date
- Conflicts of interest between governors and staff not declared

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- All governors/trustees named with categories and appointment dates
- Attendance records published for current academic year
- Register of pecuniary interests published and appears current
- Governance structure clearly shown
- For academies: Members, Trustees, LGB all listed; Articles of Association
  and Scheme of Delegation accessible

### Partial (40-79)

- Governor names listed but missing one or more of:
  - Categories/appointment dates
  - Attendance records
  - Pecuniary interests register
  - Committee membership
- Information appears partially outdated
- Trust governance published but school-level LGB not clear
- Pecuniary interests register exists but appears template/generic

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No governance information found on the website
- Only chair of governors named, others missing
- Governance page exists but contains no substantive information
- Pecuniary interests register completely absent
- Information is clearly years out of date

## Notes

- Academy trusts typically publish trust-level governance (Members, Trustees)
  on the trust website and school-level governance (LGB) on the school website.
  Both should be assessed together.
- The register of pecuniary interests is a legal requirement, not optional.
  Schools cannot refuse to publish it on privacy grounds. It must include
  ALL governors/trustees.
- Attendance records should show which governors attended which meetings,
  typically as a matrix. Stating "85% attendance" without the detail is
  insufficient.
- Some schools use GovernorHub which generates a public-facing governance
  page. This is acceptable if it includes all required information.
- Co-opted governors who are also staff should declare their employment
  as a relevant interest.
