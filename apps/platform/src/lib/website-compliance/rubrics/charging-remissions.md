# Charging and Remissions Policy

## Legislation

- Education Act 1996, Sections 449-462
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- DfE "Charging for School Activities" guidance (updated October 2023)
- Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014 (academies)

## Current References

Must align with the DfE "Charging for School Activities" guidance. Should
reference the Education Act 1996. Academies should reference their funding
agreement obligations. Any reference to "free school meals" criteria for
remissions should use the current eligibility criteria (not legacy thresholds).

## Review Frequency

Annually, approved by governing body. Must show a review date or academic year.
Policy should be reviewed whenever charging arrangements change.

## Required Content

1. Statement of the school's commitment to equal access regardless of ability to pay
2. Activities the school does NOT charge for — education during school hours, required materials and equipment, transport to and from school
3. Activities the school CAN charge for — board and lodging on residential trips, individual music tuition (not part of curriculum), activities outside school hours that are optional extras, examination re-sits where no further preparation is provided, breakages and damage caused wilfully
4. Residential trips — breakdown of what constitutes board and lodging vs educational activity
5. Voluntary contributions — statement that these are genuinely voluntary, that no child will be excluded for non-payment, and that activities may be cancelled if insufficient contributions are received
6. Music tuition — whether individual or group, what instruments, whether part of national curriculum requirements
7. Remissions policy — which families are eligible for financial support (must include pupils eligible for FSM/Pupil Premium as a minimum)
8. How remissions are applied (e.g. full or partial waiver of charges, payment plans)
9. How parents can request financial assistance and who to contact
10. Lettings and hire of school facilities (if applicable)

## Quality Indicators

- Written in plain language accessible to all parents
- Specific examples of what is and is not charged for at the school (not just quoting legislation)
- Clear statement about voluntary contributions being truly voluntary
- Named contact for financial assistance requests
- Sensitive approach to remissions — does not require parents to identify themselves publicly
- Includes practical details (e.g. how payments are collected, payment plan availability)
- Links to Pupil Premium or other relevant financial support information
- Published as a standalone page or clearly labelled document

## Red Flags

- No charging and remissions policy found on the website
- Policy states charges for activities during school hours without qualification
- No mention of remissions for disadvantaged families
- Voluntary contributions described in a way that implies they are compulsory
- States children will be excluded from activities if voluntary contributions not made
- No mention of Pupil Premium or FSM eligibility in remissions criteria
- Policy has not been updated for 3+ years
- Charges listed that appear to breach the Education Act (e.g. charging for curriculum materials)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Policy published and accessible on website
- Clearly states what is and is not charged for
- Voluntary contributions described as genuinely voluntary
- Remissions policy included with eligibility criteria
- References current legislation and guidance
- Review date within last 14 months

### Partial (40-79)

- Policy exists but missing remissions detail
- Voluntary contributions section unclear or absent
- Generic template not customised to school's actual charges
- Outdated but broadly covers required content
- Missing 2-3 required elements

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No charging and remissions policy found on website
- Policy only addresses charging with no remissions information
- Brief statement with no substantive content
- Policy is significantly outdated (3+ years)

## Notes

- This is one of the most commonly missing or outdated policies on school websites.
  Schools often publish a generic LA template without customisation.
- The key legal principle is that education during school hours must be free.
  Schools can only charge for optional extras, board and lodging, and specific
  items like individual music tuition or exam re-sits.
- Academies have slightly more flexibility in their charging arrangements but
  must still comply with the Education Act 1996 provisions and publish a policy.
- The voluntary contributions issue is a frequent source of complaints to Ofsted
  and the ESFA. Schools must be very clear that non-payment cannot result in
  exclusion from the activity.
- Some schools combine this with a "Financial Support for Families" page which
  also covers Pupil Premium, free school meals, and uniform grants. This is
  good practice but the specific charging and remissions content must still be present.
