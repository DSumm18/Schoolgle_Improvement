# KS5 Results (Post-16)

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 2
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.5 (for academies)
- Education Act 2005 (school performance information)
- 16 to 19 Accountability Headline Measures (DfE guidance)

## Current References

Must publish the most recent set of A-level/KS5 results. As of 2025-26,
this means the 2025 results (exams sat in summer 2025). Results from 2024
alone without the 2025 results indicate the page has not been updated.

Note: 2020 and 2021 results used Centre Assessed Grades (CAGs) and Teacher
Assessed Grades (TAGs) respectively. Schools may note this context for
historical trend data.

Schools must link to the school's or college's page on Find School and
College Performance Data (DfE).

## Review Frequency

Annually, updated each autumn/winter once results are validated by DfE.
Provisional results are available from August; validated results with
value-added measures are published by DfE in January/February.

## Required Content

1. Average point score per entry for academic qualifications (A-levels)
   — expressed as a grade (e.g. "B-" or numerical score)
2. Average point score per entry for applied general qualifications
   (e.g. BTECs, Cambridge Technicals) — where the school offers these
3. Average point score per entry for tech level qualifications — where
   the school offers these
4. Progress measure (value-added) — how students' results compare to
   what was predicted based on their GCSE starting points
5. Retention rate — percentage of students who complete their programme
   of study (i.e. entered Year 12 and completed Year 13)
6. Destinations data — percentage of students progressing to higher
   education, further education, apprenticeships, or employment
7. Link to the school's or college's page on Find School and College
   Performance Data

## Quality Indicators

- Comparison to national averages for each measure
- Multi-year trend data (3+ years where available)
- Subject-level results showing performance in individual A-level subjects
- Breakdown of higher education destinations (Russell Group, Oxbridge,
  other universities, degree apprenticeships)
- Contextual commentary about the cohort and school's sixth form offer
- Information about the range of courses offered at KS5
- Apprenticeship and employment destinations highlighted (not just
  university-focused)
- Explanation of value-added/progress measures for parents and students
- ALPS (A-Level Performance System) grade or similar quality indicator
  if available
- Celebration of individual student achievements where appropriate

## Red Flags

- No KS5 results published on the website despite having a sixth form
- Results are more than one academic year old
- Only A-level results shown when the school also offers vocational routes
  (selective presentation)
- No progress or value-added measures published
- No retention rate data — may indicate high dropout between Year 12 and 13
- No destinations data when it has been published by DfE
- No link to the DfE performance data website
- School claims results are "not applicable" when it has Year 13 students
- Results appear to be for only a subset of students (e.g. only those
  achieving A\*-B, omitting lower grades)
- Only university destinations mentioned, ignoring apprenticeships and
  employment

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Most recent KS5 results published (or clearly marked as provisional)
- Average point scores per entry shown for academic qualifications
- Average point scores for vocational qualifications shown (where offered)
- Progress/value-added measure shown
- Retention rate published
- Destinations data shown (where available from DfE)
- Link to DfE performance data website provided
- Academic year clearly stated
- Data presented clearly in tabular format

### Partial (40-79)

- Results are published but one or more of:
  - Only A-level grades listed without average point scores
  - No progress or value-added measure shown
  - No retention rate data
  - Vocational qualification results omitted
  - No link to DfE performance data website
  - Results are from the previous year (acceptable August-February;
    concern after February)
  - Data is presented in prose or as a summary rather than full detail
  - No destinations data (less concerning due to DfE time lag)

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No KS5 results found on the website despite having a sixth form
- Results are 2+ years old
- Only a link to the DfE website with no results on the school's own site
- Page exists with a heading but no data
- Only headline grades mentioned (e.g. "Our students achieved excellent
  results") without any specific data

## Notes

- This requirement applies only to schools and colleges with POST-16
  provision (sixth forms, sixth form colleges, FE colleges with 16-19
  provision). Schools without a sixth form are not required to publish
  KS5 results.
- The 16-19 accountability measures use a different framework from KS4.
  The headline measures are: progress (value-added), attainment (APS per
  entry), retention, and destinations.
- Results are published separately for academic (A-levels), applied general
  (BTECs etc.), and tech levels. Schools should report whichever categories
  they offer.
- Retention rates can be sensitive — a school with high dropout rates may
  still show strong results for those who remain. Publishing retention data
  provides important context.
- Destinations data has a significant time lag (typically 2 years). Schools
  should publish whatever is most recently available from DfE.
- Some sixth forms are part of multi-academy trusts with centralised sixth
  form provision. Each individual school site that teaches sixth formers
  should publish its own results.
- Schools that have recently opened a sixth form may not yet have published
  results. They should state this clearly rather than having no information.
- L3 value-added (level 3 value-added) is the technical name for the KS5
  progress measure. It compares actual results to expected results based
  on GCSE attainment.
