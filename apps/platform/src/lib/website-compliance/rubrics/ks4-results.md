# KS4 Results (Secondary)

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 2
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.5 (for academies)
- Education Act 2005 (school performance information)

## Current References

Must publish the most recent set of GCSE/KS4 results. As of 2025-26,
this means the 2025 results (exams sat in summer 2025). Results from
2024 alone without the 2025 results indicate the page has not been updated.

Note: 2020 and 2021 used Centre Assessed Grades (CAGs) and Teacher
Assessed Grades (TAGs) respectively. 2022 saw a return to exams with
some grading adjustments. Schools may note this context when showing
historical trends.

Schools must also link to the school's page on Find School and College
Performance Data (DfE).

## Review Frequency

Annually, updated each autumn/winter once results are validated by DfE
(typically January). Provisional results are available from August;
validated results with Progress 8 and Attainment 8 are published by
DfE in January/February.

## Required Content

1. Progress 8 score — the school's headline value-added measure
2. Progress 8 confidence interval — whether the score is significantly
   above, below, or in line with the national average
3. Attainment 8 score — average achievement across 8 qualifications
4. Percentage of pupils achieving grade 5 or above in English and maths
5. Percentage of pupils achieving grade 4 or above in English and maths
6. English Baccalaureate (EBacc) entry rate — percentage of pupils
   entered for the EBacc combination
7. EBacc average point score (APS) — average point score across EBacc
   subjects
8. Destinations data — percentage of pupils staying in education or
   entering employment after KS4 (published by DfE with a time lag)
9. Link to the school's page on Find School and College Performance Data

## Quality Indicators

- Comparison to national averages alongside school figures
- Multi-year trend data (3+ years where available)
- Contextual commentary explaining the school's cohort and circumstances
- Breakdown by pupil group (disadvantaged vs other) showing the school's
  efforts to close gaps — not required but demonstrates transparency
- Clear explanation of what Progress 8 and Attainment 8 mean for parents
- Subject-level results or analysis (e.g. strongest and weakest subjects)
- Information about the school's curriculum offer and how it relates to
  EBacc entry
- Celebration of non-academic achievements alongside exam results
- Details of vocational and technical qualification results where applicable
- Mention of how the school addresses areas for improvement identified
  by the data

## Red Flags

- No KS4 results published on the website
- Results are more than one academic year old
- Only some headline measures published (e.g. Attainment 8 but no
  Progress 8, or grades 4+ but not grades 5+)
- EBacc data omitted entirely
- No link to the DfE performance data website
- Results appear to have been selectively presented to hide poor performance
  (e.g. only showing subjects where results were strong)
- Progress 8 score omitted when it is negative
- Destinations data omitted when published by DfE
- School claims results are "not applicable" for a secondary school with
  Year 11 pupils
- Only a link to DfE with no results published on the school's own website

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Most recent KS4 results published (or clearly marked as provisional)
- Progress 8 score and confidence interval shown
- Attainment 8 score shown
- Percentage achieving grades 5+ and 4+ in English and maths shown
- EBacc entry rate and APS shown
- Destinations data shown (where available from DfE)
- Link to DfE performance data website provided
- Academic year clearly stated
- Data presented clearly in tabular format

### Partial (40-79)

- Results are published but one or more of:
  - Progress 8 or Attainment 8 missing
  - EBacc data not published
  - Only grade 4+ shown without grade 5+
  - No link to DfE performance data website
  - Results are from the previous year (acceptable August-January;
    concern after February when validated data is released)
  - Data is presented in prose form making it hard to find specific figures
  - No destinations data (less concerning due to DfE time lag)

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No KS4 results found on the website
- Results are 2+ years old
- Only a link to the DfE website with no results on the school's own site
- Page exists with a heading but no data
- Only one or two measures published out of the full set

## Notes

- This requirement applies to SECONDARY schools with Year 11 pupils only.
  Schools without Year 11 (e.g. Year 7-9 middle schools) are not required
  to publish KS4 results.
- Progress 8 is calculated by DfE and is not available until January/February
  following the exam year. Schools should publish provisional results
  (Attainment 8, grade percentages) from August and add Progress 8 when
  available.
- EBacc consists of English, maths, sciences, a language, and either
  history or geography. Not all schools enter high proportions of pupils
  for EBacc — this is a school choice, but the entry rate must be published.
- Destinations data has a significant time lag (typically 2 years behind
  current results). Schools should publish whatever is most recently
  available from DfE.
- Some schools also publish vocational qualification results (BTECs, etc.)
  alongside GCSEs. While not strictly required, it gives a more complete
  picture for schools with significant vocational pathways.
- UTC, studio schools, and free schools with Year 11 have the same
  publication requirements as mainstream secondary schools.
- The DfE performance tables website was rebranded to "Find School and
  College Performance Data" — older links to "Compare School Performance"
  may still work but should be updated.
