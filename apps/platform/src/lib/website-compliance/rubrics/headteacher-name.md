# Headteacher Name

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Paragraph 3
- Education (School Information) (England) Regulations 2012
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025 (for academies)

## Current References

No specific dated references required. The headteacher name must simply
reflect the current postholder. If the school has recently appointed a
new headteacher, the website should be updated promptly.

## Review Frequency

Should be updated immediately upon any change of headteacher, including
when an acting or interim headteacher is appointed. As a minimum, verified
annually as part of the website compliance review.

## Required Content

1. Full name of the headteacher (or equivalent: head of school, executive headteacher, principal)
2. Correct title and role designation (e.g. "Headteacher", "Head of School", "Executive Principal")
3. Must be publicly visible without login or registration

## Quality Indicators

- Professional photograph of the headteacher
- Welcome message or introductory letter from the headteacher
- Contact route to reach the headteacher (email, phone, or "contact via school office")
- Brief biography or professional background
- Headteacher's educational philosophy or vision statement
- Named on a dedicated "About Us", "Our Team", or "Leadership" page
- Also referenced on the homepage or a prominent landing page
- Qualifications listed (e.g. NPQH, MA, BA)
- Clear distinction between headteacher and other senior leaders

## Red Flags

- No headteacher name found anywhere on the website
- Name only appears in a downloadable PDF but not on any HTML page
- "[INSERT NAME]" or template placeholder text
- Name appears to be outdated (e.g. contradicted by Ofsted report or GIAS data)
- Headteacher name only accessible behind a login wall
- Multiple conflicting names across different pages (e.g. old name on one page, new name on another)
- Role title is ambiguous or missing entirely (just a name with no designation)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Headteacher's full name is clearly published on the website
- Correct role title is stated (headteacher, principal, head of school, etc.)
- Name is on an HTML page (not only in a downloadable document)
- Information is publicly accessible without login
- Bonus: accompanied by photo, welcome message, or contact route

### Partial (40-79)

- Headteacher name is present but one or more of:
  - Only appears in a downloadable PDF (e.g. a policy document) not on an HTML page
  - Name is present but role title is missing or unclear
  - Name may be outdated (e.g. previous headteacher still listed alongside new one)
  - Found only in a hard-to-find location (e.g. buried in meeting minutes)

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No headteacher name found anywhere on the public website
- Only placeholder text or generic "The Headteacher" without a name
- Name is behind a login wall or password-protected area
- Website only shows governors/trustees with no reference to the headteacher

## Notes

- In academy trusts, the school leader may hold the title "Head of School"
  or "Principal" rather than "Headteacher". The executive headteacher or
  CEO may also be named but is not a substitute for the school-level leader.
- Multi-academy trusts should name the headteacher/principal for each
  individual school, not just the CEO or executive headteacher.
- The headteacher name is one of the most basic statutory requirements and
  is frequently checked by Ofsted inspectors on Day 1 of an inspection.
- GIAS (Get Information About Schools) data can be used to cross-reference
  the published headteacher name for accuracy.
- Some schools publish the headteacher name on a "Meet the Team" or "Staff"
  page rather than a dedicated leadership page — this is acceptable as long
  as the name is clearly identified with the headteacher role.
