# PE & Sport Premium

## Legislation

- PE and Sport Premium Conditions of Grant (updated annually by DfE)
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 3
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.5 (for academies)

## Current References

Must reference the current academic year's funding allocation.
Reports should clearly state the academic year they cover (e.g. 2025-26).
A report from a previous academic year alone (e.g. 2023-24) without the
current year's report means the school is non-compliant.

DfE requires schools to publish the report by 31 July each year for the
previous academic year's spending, and to publish a forward-looking plan
for the current year.

## Review Frequency

Annually by 31 July. Must cover the previous academic year's actual
spending AND the current year's planned spending.

## Required Content

1. Total PE and sport premium funding received (exact £ amount)
2. A breakdown of how the funding has been or will be spent — not just
   categories but specific activities, programmes, and costs
3. The impact of the spending on pupils' PE and sport participation
   and attainment — measurable outcomes, not just activities delivered
4. How the improvements will be sustained after the funding ends
5. Year 6 swimming and water safety data (primary schools only):
   a. Percentage meeting the national curriculum requirement to swim
   competently, confidently and proficiently over at least 25 metres
   b. Percentage who can use a range of strokes effectively
   c. Percentage who can perform safe self-rescue in different water-based
   situations
6. If the school does not have Year 6 pupils (e.g. infant school), this
   should be stated explicitly

## Quality Indicators

- Specific activities and programmes named (not just "sports coaching")
- Before/after participation data (e.g. "62% → 78% attending clubs")
- Competition results or inter-school competition participation rates
- Staff CPD in PE mentioned (e.g. coaches upskilling teachers)
- Published as HTML on the website (DfE preference, not just PDF download)
- Covers both actual spending (previous year) and planned spending (current year)
- Named lead for PE and sport premium spending
- Links to the school's broader curriculum intent for PE
- Inclusion: how the premium supports disadvantaged or less active pupils

## Red Flags

- No PE sport premium information found anywhere on the website
- Report is from a previous academic year only (e.g. 2023-24 with no 2024-25)
- No spending breakdown — just the total amount without detail
- No impact data — describes activities but not outcomes
- No swimming data for a primary school with Year 6
- Swimming data shows less than 25% meeting the standard (may be accurate
  but should trigger a question about improvement strategies)
- Report is identical to the previous year's (copy-paste without updating)
- Total spending stated does not match the known DfE allocation for the school
  (typical primary allocation is approx. £16,000-£20,000 per year)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Current year report published (or previous year if before 31 July deadline)
- Total funding amount clearly stated
- Spending breakdown with specific activities and costs
- Impact on pupils described with measurable outcomes
- Sustainability plan explaining how improvements continue
- Year 6 swimming data with all 3 metrics (primary schools)
- Review date or academic year clearly stated

### Partial (40-79)

- Report exists but one or more of:
  - No spending breakdown (just total amount)
  - No impact evidence (activities listed but no outcomes)
  - No swimming data
  - Report is from previous year and current year not yet published (between
    August and October this is acceptable; after October it's a concern)
  - Report exists but lacks specificity (vague categories, no costs)

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No PE sport premium report found on the website
- Report is 2+ academic years old
- Only a brief mention of PE sport premium without any spending or impact data
- Page exists with title but no content

## Notes

- Many schools publish this as a PDF download rather than an HTML page.
  DfE guidance recommends HTML but PDF is acceptable if accessible.
- Some schools use the DfE template (a standard table format). The template
  is acceptable but should be fully completed, not partially filled.
- For academies, this is a school-level requirement, not trust-level.
  Each school in a MAT must publish its own PE sport premium report.
- The DfE allocation is calculated as a lump sum of £16,000 plus £10 per
  pupil in Years 1-6. This can be used to verify the stated amount.
- Schools sometimes call this "Sports Grant" or "PE Grant" rather than
  "PE and Sport Premium" — all are acceptable terms.
- Infant schools (no Year 6) should state they do not have Y6 pupils
  rather than omitting swimming data without explanation.
