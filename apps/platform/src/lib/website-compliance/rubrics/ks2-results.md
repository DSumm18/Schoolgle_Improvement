# KS2 Results (Primary)

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 2
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.5 (for academies)
- Education Act 2005 (school performance information)

## Current References

Must publish the most recent set of KS2 SATs results. As of 2025-26,
this means the 2025 results (tests sat in May 2025). Results from 2024
alone without the 2025 results indicate the page has not been updated.

Note: 2020 and 2021 results do not exist due to COVID cancellations.
2022 results were the first post-COVID set. Schools should not be penalised
for gaps in those years.

Schools must also link to the school's performance data on the DfE
Compare School Performance website (now called Find School and College
Performance Data).

## Review Frequency

Annually, updated each autumn once results are confirmed (typically by
December). Provisional results are available from July; validated and
final results are published by DfE in December.

## Required Content

1. Percentage of pupils meeting the expected standard in reading
2. Percentage of pupils meeting the expected standard in writing
   (teacher assessed)
3. Percentage of pupils meeting the expected standard in maths
4. Percentage of pupils meeting the expected standard in reading,
   writing, and maths combined
5. Percentage of pupils achieving the higher standard in reading
6. Percentage of pupils achieving the higher standard in writing
7. Percentage of pupils achieving the higher standard in maths
8. Average scaled score in reading (out of 120, expected = 100+)
9. Average scaled score in maths (out of 120, expected = 100+)
10. Average progress score in reading (value-added from KS1 to KS2)
11. Average progress score in writing (value-added from KS1 to KS2)
12. Average progress score in maths (value-added from KS1 to KS2)
13. Link to the school's page on Find School and College Performance Data

## Quality Indicators

- Comparison to national averages alongside school results
- Multi-year trend data (e.g. 3 years of results where available)
- Contextual commentary explaining results in the school's context
  (e.g. cohort characteristics, SEND proportion, mobility)
- Breakdown by pupil group where appropriate (disadvantaged vs other,
  SEND, EAL) — though this is not required on the website
- Clear data presentation (tables preferred over prose)
- Explanation of what the measures mean for parents who may not understand
  scaled scores or progress measures
- Celebration of achievements alongside honest acknowledgement of areas
  for improvement
- Information about how the school is addressing any areas below national

## Red Flags

- No KS2 results published on the website
- Results are more than one academic year old (e.g. only 2023 results
  published when 2024/2025 results are available)
- Only some measures published (e.g. expected standard but no progress
  scores or scaled scores)
- Results are presented without the academic year being stated
- No link to the DfE performance data website
- Results appear to be fabricated or do not match publicly available DfE data
- School claims results are "not available" when DfE has published them
- Only reading and maths shown with writing omitted (sometimes done when
  writing results are poor)
- Progress scores omitted entirely (sometimes done when progress is negative)

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Most recent KS2 results published (or clearly marked as provisional)
- Expected standard percentages for reading, writing, and maths shown
- Higher standard percentages shown
- Average scaled scores for reading and maths shown
- Progress scores for reading, writing, and maths shown
- Link to DfE performance data website provided
- Academic year clearly stated
- Data is presented clearly (table format preferred)

### Partial (40-79)

- Results are published but one or more of:
  - Only some measures shown (e.g. expected standard but no progress)
  - No scaled scores published
  - Results are from the previous year and current year not yet published
    (acceptable between July-December; concern after December)
  - No link to DfE performance data website
  - Data is difficult to read or buried in prose
  - Writing results omitted

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No KS2 results found on the website
- Results are 2+ years old
- Only a link to the DfE website with no results published on the school's
  own site (the school must publish results itself, not just link)
- Page exists with a heading but no data
- Results cover only one subject

## Notes

- This requirement applies to PRIMARY schools with Year 6 pupils only.
  Infant schools and junior schools joining at Year 3 have different
  considerations.
- Schools with very small cohorts (fewer than 11 pupils) may suppress
  data to prevent identification of individual pupils. They should state
  this explicitly rather than simply omitting results.
- Progress scores are value-added measures comparing pupils' KS2 results
  to their KS1 starting points. A score of 0 is average; positive means
  above average progress; negative means below average. Many parents do
  not understand this — good websites explain it.
- The DfE performance data website was previously called "Compare School
  Performance" and before that "School Performance Tables." Schools may
  use any of these names when linking to it.
- Some schools publish results in their annual report to parents or in
  a governors' report. The statutory requirement is for the results to
  be on the school website specifically.
- Schools that have converted to academy status mid-year should publish
  results under whatever name and status they held when the tests were sat.
