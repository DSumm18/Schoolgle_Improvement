# Ofsted Report

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- Education Act 2005 (inspection framework)

## Current References

Must link to the school's most recent Ofsted inspection report. The
report is published on the Ofsted reports website (reports.ofsted.gov.uk).
Schools should link to their specific report page, not just the Ofsted
homepage. If the school has been re-brokered or changed status (e.g.
converter academy), the most recent report for the current school should
be linked.

From September 2024, Ofsted no longer issues single headline grades for
state-funded schools. Schools inspected under the new framework will
receive individual grades for each area of judgement. Schools should
display the current rating information accurately.

## Review Frequency

Updated whenever a new inspection report is published. Schools should
add the link promptly after the report is published (typically within
30 working days of the inspection). The website should be checked at
least annually to ensure the link remains active.

## Required Content

1. A direct link to the school's Ofsted report page on the official
   Ofsted reports website (reports.ofsted.gov.uk)
2. The school's current overall Ofsted rating clearly displayed
   (Outstanding, Good, Requires Improvement, or Inadequate) — or
   individual judgement grades for inspections from September 2024
   onwards
3. The date of the most recent inspection
4. The Ofsted logo or "Good Provider" / "Outstanding Provider" badge
   if used (must be the current version and used in accordance with
   Ofsted brand guidelines)

## Quality Indicators

- Summary of key findings from the inspection report
- Quotes from the report highlighting strengths
- School's response to the inspection or improvement actions taken
- Link is prominent and easy to find (not buried deep in the site)
- Previous inspection reports also accessible
- Clear context if the school has changed status since last inspection
  (e.g. "This report was for our predecessor school")
- Date formatted clearly (e.g. "Inspected: March 2024")
- Any monitoring visit letters also published
- Parent View link included alongside the Ofsted report

## Red Flags

- No Ofsted report link on the website
- Link goes to the Ofsted homepage rather than the school's specific page
- Link is broken or dead
- Outdated rating displayed that does not match the current report
- School displays a rating badge that does not match their actual rating
- Report link goes to a very old inspection (pre-academy conversion)
  with no explanation
- School claims a rating they do not hold
- Ofsted logo used incorrectly or an outdated version of the logo

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Direct link to school's page on Ofsted reports website
- Current rating clearly displayed and accurate
- Date of most recent inspection stated
- Link is working and easily findable on the website
- Ofsted branding used correctly (if used at all)

### Partial (40-79)

- Ofsted mentioned but link goes to homepage rather than school page
- Rating displayed but date of inspection not stated
- Report linked but rating shown is from a previous inspection
- Link exists but is difficult to find (buried in policies or documents)
- Information is present but partially inaccurate

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No Ofsted report link found on the website
- Link is broken or dead
- School displays an incorrect rating
- No reference to Ofsted inspection at all
- Information is clearly fraudulent or misleading

## Notes

- Schools that have recently converted to academy status may not yet
  have an inspection under their new name. They should link to the
  predecessor school's report and explain the context.
- New schools (open less than 3 years) may not yet have been inspected.
  They should state this clearly rather than having no mention of Ofsted.
- Schools in special measures or with serious weaknesses should still
  link to their report. Attempting to hide the rating is a significant
  red flag.
- The Ofsted "Good Provider" and "Outstanding Provider" logos have
  specific usage guidelines. Schools should only display the badge
  that matches their current rating.
- From September 2024, overall effectiveness grades are removed for
  state-funded schools. Schools inspected under this new approach
  should display the individual area judgements rather than a single
  headline grade.
- Independent schools and early years settings may still receive
  overall effectiveness judgements.
- Schools should also consider linking to Parent View
  (parentview.ofsted.gov.uk) to encourage parent participation.
