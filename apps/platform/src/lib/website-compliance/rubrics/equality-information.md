# Equality Information

## Legislation

- Equality Act 2010, Section 149 (Public Sector Equality Duty)
- Equality Act 2010 (Specific Duties) Regulations 2011, Regulation 2

## Current References

Must publish information annually to demonstrate compliance with the
Public Sector Equality Duty. Information should be current — relating
to the most recent academic or calendar year. Information from more
than two years ago without an update is likely non-compliant.

## Review Frequency

Annually. Schools must publish information at least once a year
showing how they are meeting the Equality Duty. This is typically
updated at the start of the academic year or when new data becomes
available.

## Required Content

1. Information about the school's workforce diversity, including:
   a. Breakdown of staff by protected characteristics where data
   is held (gender, ethnicity, disability, age profile)
   b. How the school promotes equality in recruitment and employment
2. Information about the school's pupil population, including:
   a. Pupil demographics by relevant protected characteristics
   (gender, ethnicity, SEND, FSM as a proxy for socioeconomic
   status)
   b. Any attainment or progress gaps between different groups
3. How the school is meeting each of the three aims of the Equality
   Duty:
   a. Eliminating discrimination, harassment, and victimisation
   b. Advancing equality of opportunity
   c. Fostering good relations between people with different
   protected characteristics
4. Evidence of how equality considerations are embedded in school
   policies and practices
5. The date the information was last updated

## Quality Indicators

- Specific data and statistics (not just narrative statements)
- Analysis of attainment gaps by pupil group with trend data
- Staff diversity data compared to local population demographics
- Evidence of equality impact assessments on school policies
- Pupil voice and stakeholder engagement on equality matters
- Actions taken in response to identified inequalities
- Links to the school's equality objectives and progress updates
- Accessibility audit of the school site and website
- Training provided to staff on equality and diversity
- Curriculum review for diversity and representation

## Red Flags

- No equality information published on the website
- Information is more than two years old
- Only a generic equality policy with no school-specific data
- No workforce diversity data at all
- No pupil demographic data
- Claims "we treat everyone equally" without evidence
- Protected characteristics not mentioned
- Information is identical to template text with no local data
- SEND or disadvantaged pupils not mentioned

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Current equality information published (within the last year)
- Workforce diversity data included
- Pupil demographic data included
- How the school meets the three Equality Duty aims is addressed
- Date of publication/update is stated
- School-specific data and evidence provided

### Partial (40-79)

- Information exists but:
  - Is more than one year old but less than two
  - Missing workforce or pupil data (has one but not the other)
  - Generic statements without school-specific data
  - Only covers one or two of the three Equality Duty aims
  - Buried in an equality policy without being clearly identified
    as the annual equality information publication

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No equality information found on the website
- Information is more than two years old
- Only a generic equality policy with no data or evidence
- Website states "equality information available on request"
- Brief mention of equality in another policy but no substantive data

## Notes

- The specific duties under the Equality Act apply to all
  state-funded schools — both maintained and academies.
- Equality information is different from equality objectives.
  Information is published annually to show how the school is
  meeting the Equality Duty. Objectives are specific goals set
  at least every four years (see equality-objectives rubric).
- Schools often combine equality information with their equality
  policy. This is acceptable, provided the policy includes
  school-specific data and is updated annually.
- The nine protected characteristics are: age, disability, gender
  reassignment, marriage/civil partnership, pregnancy/maternity,
  race, religion/belief, sex, sexual orientation. Not all will
  be relevant to pupil data (e.g. marriage), but schools should
  consider each.
- Schools are not required to publish information that would
  identify individual pupils or staff. Data should be aggregated.
  Where numbers are very small, schools may withhold data to
  prevent identification.
- The EHRC Technical Guidance for Schools (2014) provides detailed
  advice on meeting the PSED, including what information to publish.
- Many schools use their annual self-evaluation or school
  improvement plan data to populate this requirement.
