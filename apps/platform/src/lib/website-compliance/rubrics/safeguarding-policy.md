# Safeguarding / Child Protection Policy

## Legislation

- Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE) 2025
- Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- Children Act 1989 and 2004
- Education Act 2002, Section 175 (maintained) / Section 157 (academies)

## Current References

Must reference KCSIE 2025 (published September 2025). Any reference to
KCSIE 2024 or earlier WITHOUT also referencing KCSIE 2025 means the policy
has not been updated for the current academic year.

Must also reference Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023.
References to Working Together 2018 alone indicate an outdated policy.

## Review Frequency

Annually. Must be approved by the governing body/board of trustees.
Review date and next review date should be stated. The policy should be
reviewed at the start of each academic year (September) at minimum.

## Required Content

1. Named Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) with contact details (phone/email)
2. Named Deputy DSL(s) with contact details
3. Named safeguarding governor/trustee
4. Definition of safeguarding and child protection
5. Clear procedures for staff to report concerns about a child
6. Procedures for managing allegations against staff and volunteers (KCSIE Part 4)
7. Role and responsibilities of the DSL (per KCSIE Annex C)
8. Multi-agency working procedures — how to make a referral to Children's Social Care
9. Local Safeguarding Children Partnership (LSCP) contact details
10. Record keeping procedures (what to record, where, who has access)
11. Confidentiality statement — information sharing principles
12. Whistleblowing reference or cross-reference to whistleblowing policy
13. Online safety section including filtering and monitoring approach (KCSIE Part 2)
14. Children missing from education procedures
15. Peer-on-peer / child-on-child abuse section (KCSIE 2025 terminology)
16. Sexual violence and sexual harassment between children (KCSIE Part 5)
17. Prevent duty — radicalisation and extremism
18. FGM, forced marriage, honour-based abuse
19. County lines and criminal exploitation
20. Children with SEND — additional vulnerabilities
21. Early help and early intervention procedures
22. Governing body/trustee board safeguarding responsibilities
23. Staff training requirements — induction + regular updates
24. Safer recruitment reference or cross-reference
25. Use of reasonable force / physical intervention reference
26. Photography and images policy reference
27. Mobile phones and personal devices policy (for staff)

## Quality Indicators

- Flowcharts showing clear reporting procedures
- Local authority / LSCP contact details included (not just "contact the LA")
- Named individuals throughout (not just roles)
- Cross-references to related policies (behaviour, online safety, whistleblowing, RSE, attendance)
- Appendices with key contacts, concern forms, body maps
- Written in accessible language for all staff including support staff
- Version control with approval date, approving body, next review date
- Published as both HTML summary page AND downloadable full policy PDF

## Red Flags

- References KCSIE 2024 or earlier without referencing 2025
- References Working Together 2018 without referencing 2023
- DSL name does not match the person currently in role
- No review date or review date more than 14 months old
- Generic template with "[SCHOOL NAME]" or "[INSERT NAME]" placeholders unfilled
- No governing body approval recorded
- Missing online safety / filtering section (strengthened in KCSIE 2025)
- No reference to child-on-child abuse (terminology updated from peer-on-peer)
- Missing sexual violence and harassment section
- Policy is password-protected or requires login to access
- No contact details for the DSL (just a name without phone/email)
- References to repealed or superseded legislation

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Policy exists, is accessible on the website, and is clearly the current version
- References KCSIE 2025 and Working Together 2023
- DSL and Deputy DSL named with contact details
- All 27 required content areas substantially covered
- Review date within the last 12 months
- Governing body approval evident

### Partial (40-79)

- Policy exists but one or more of:
  - References KCSIE 2024 (one year out of date)
  - Missing 3-5 required content areas
  - No review date but content appears relatively current
  - DSL named but no contact details
  - Template partially customised

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No safeguarding policy found on the website
- Policy references KCSIE 2023 or earlier (2+ years out of date)
- Missing more than 5 required content areas
- Generic uncustomised template
- Policy is behind a login wall and not publicly accessible

## Notes

- Academy trusts often publish a trust-wide policy which the school then
  customises with an addendum naming their DSL and deputy. Both the trust
  policy AND the school addendum should be assessed together.
- Some schools publish a short "safeguarding information for parents" page
  alongside the full policy. The HTML page is NOT a substitute for the full
  policy but IS good practice for accessibility.
- The DSL name should match across the policy, the school website safeguarding
  page, and any staff directory. Inconsistencies are a red flag.
- KCSIE Part 1 (or Annex A condensed version) should be referenced as
  mandatory reading for all staff.
