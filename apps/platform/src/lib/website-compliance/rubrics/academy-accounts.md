# Academy Annual Accounts and Report

## Legislation

- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 3 (Financial Statements)
- Academies Financial Handbook (predecessor, now superseded)
- Companies Act 2006 (as applicable to academy trusts)
- Charities SORP (FRS 102) — Statement of Recommended Practice

## Current References

Must publish the latest audited annual accounts and annual report.
For the 2025-26 academic year, the most recent published accounts
will be for the period ending 31 August 2025. Accounts from before
31 August 2024 alone are out of date.

## Review Frequency

Annually. Audited accounts must be filed with ESFA by 31 December
each year and published on the trust website by the same date.
The annual report covers the same period as the accounts.

## Required Content

1. Latest audited annual accounts (financial statements) for the
   academy trust
2. Annual report (trustees' report) covering:
   a. Structure, governance, and management
   b. Objectives and activities
   c. Strategic report
   d. Achievements and performance
   e. Financial review
3. Accounts must be the full audited version (not draft or
   management accounts)
4. Previous years' accounts should ideally be available (at least
   the prior year)

## Quality Indicators

- Both current and previous years' accounts available for download
- Published as accessible PDF (not scanned images)
- Easy to find from the website navigation (dedicated finance or
  governance page)
- Accompanied by a plain-English summary of the financial position
- Trust-wide and individual academy financial breakdowns where the
  trust operates multiple schools
- Clearly labelled with the financial period covered
- Auditor's report included as part of the published accounts

## Red Flags

- No accounts published on the trust website
- Only draft or unaudited accounts published
- Accounts are more than one year out of date
- Accounts are published but the annual report is missing
- Accounts are only available on Companies House, not on the
  trust's own website
- Published as low-quality scanned PDFs that are not searchable

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Latest audited accounts published and available for download
- Annual report (trustees' report) published alongside accounts
- Documents are clearly labelled with the correct financial period
- Published within the required timeframe (by 31 December)
- Documents are accessible (searchable PDF, not scanned images)

### Partial (40-79)

- Accounts exist but:
  - Are one year out of date (previous year only, not current)
  - Annual report is missing (only the financial statements)
  - Published as poor-quality scans
  - Hard to find on the website
  - Only summary figures published, not the full audited accounts

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No accounts or annual report found on the trust website
- Accounts are 2+ years out of date
- Only management accounts published (not audited)
- Website states "accounts available on request" without publishing

## Notes

- This requirement applies to ACADEMIES ONLY. Maintained schools'
  finances are published through the local authority.
- Academy trusts must file accounts with ESFA by 31 December and
  publish them on their website by the same date.
- Multi-academy trusts (MATs) typically publish trust-wide accounts.
  Individual academy financial data should be identifiable within
  the trust accounts or published separately.
- Accounts must follow Charities SORP (FRS 102) and the Academies
  Accounts Direction issued by ESFA.
- The accounts must include the auditor's report — an independent
  external audit is mandatory for all academy trusts.
- Some trusts also publish a simplified financial summary for
  parents. This is good practice but does not replace the full
  audited accounts.
