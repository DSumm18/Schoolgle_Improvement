# In-Year Admissions

## Legislation

- School Admissions Code 2021, Paragraphs 2.30–2.33
- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4
- Education Act 1996, Section 411
- Equality Act 2010 (non-discrimination in admissions)

## Current References

Must reference the School Admissions Code 2021. The 2021 Code significantly
strengthened requirements around in-year admissions, including requirements
for own-admission-authority schools to participate in local authority
in-year coordination where it exists. Any reference to the 2014 Code alone
indicates the information has not been updated.

## Review Frequency

Reviewed annually alongside the main admissions arrangements. Updated
whenever the school's in-year process changes. Should be checked to ensure
contact details and application routes remain accurate.

## Required Content

1. Clear statement that the school accepts in-year applications (applications outside the normal admissions round)
2. How to apply — application form, local authority portal, or direct contact
3. Where to obtain or download the in-year application form
4. Who processes in-year applications (school directly, or local authority on behalf of school)
5. What information is required from the applicant
6. Expected timeframe for a decision (must notify parents of outcome within 15 school days)
7. What happens if the year group is full — waiting list information
8. Right of appeal if the application is refused, with appeals contact details
9. Fair Access Protocol reference — how the school participates
10. Contact details for in-year admissions enquiries (named person or department, phone, email)

## Quality Indicators

- Downloadable in-year application form on the website
- Online application option (form or link to LA portal)
- Clear explanation of what "in-year" means (moving to the area, changing schools mid-year)
- Information about settling-in support, transition arrangements, or induction for new starters
- Current availability by year group (places available / year group full)
- Named admissions contact with direct phone number and email
- Information in multiple languages for EAL families
- Links to local authority in-year coordination scheme
- Expected timeline presented visually (application > decision > start date)
- Information about documents needed (proof of address, birth certificate, previous school details)
- Guidance for families new to the UK or the area
- Cross-reference to main admissions policy and oversubscription criteria

## Red Flags

- No in-year admissions information on the website
- Website states "we are full" or "not accepting applications" without offering the right to apply and appeal
- No application form or route to apply provided
- No mention of the right to appeal a refusal
- Discouraging language suggesting parents should not apply or will not get a place
- Requirement for an interview or assessment before admission (not permitted under the Admissions Code except for grammar school test/boarding assessment)
- Request for information about immigration status, nationality, or first language as a condition of application (not an admissions criterion)
- No contact details for enquiries
- Information is behind a login wall
- In-year process described only within a lengthy PDF policy with no HTML summary

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Clear in-year admissions process is published on the website
- How to apply and where to get the form is stated
- Decision timeframe referenced (within 15 school days)
- Right of appeal if refused is clearly stated with contact details
- Waiting list information provided
- Contact details for admissions enquiries included
- All 10 required content areas substantially addressed

### Partial (40-79)

- Some in-year admissions information present but one or more of:
  - No application form or download link provided
  - Missing right-of-appeal information
  - No decision timeframe stated
  - Contact details incomplete (e.g. no phone number or email)
  - Process described but buried in the main admissions policy with no standalone section
  - Waiting list process not explained

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No in-year admissions information found on the website
- Website actively discourages in-year applications
- Only a link to the local authority with no school-specific information
- In-year admissions process requires interview, assessment, or meeting before application is considered
- Information is behind a login wall
- Missing more than 5 required content areas

## Notes

- The School Admissions Code 2021 strengthened in-year admissions requirements
  significantly. Own-admission-authority schools must now process in-year
  applications in accordance with their published arrangements and the Code.
- Schools must not refuse to provide an application form or discourage
  parents from applying, even when year groups are believed to be full.
  Parents have a right to apply and a right to appeal any refusal.
- The 15 school day decision timeframe is a requirement of the 2021 Code
  (paragraph 2.32). Schools must notify the local authority of the
  application and its outcome.
- Many local authorities now operate in-year coordination schemes. Where
  this exists, own-admission-authority schools should participate and
  direct parents to the LA portal, while also accepting direct applications.
- Community and voluntary controlled schools have their in-year admissions
  managed by the local authority. The school website should still explain
  how parents can apply and link to the LA process.
- Schools subject to a direction under the Fair Access Protocol must admit
  children even if the year group is full. Reference to the Fair Access
  Protocol demonstrates the school understands this obligation.
- Infant class size regulations (maximum 30 per class in Reception, Year 1,
  Year 2) apply to in-year admissions as well as normal-round admissions.
  Schools should explain this where relevant.
- Children with an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) naming the school
  must be admitted. In-year admissions pages should note that EHCP
  placements are managed through the SEND team, not the standard
  admissions process.
