# Pupil Premium Strategy Statement

## Legislation

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008, Schedule 4, Part 3
- Pupil Premium Conditions of Grant (updated annually by DfE)
- Academy Trust Handbook 2025, Section 2.5 (for academies)

## Current References

Must reference the current academic year (2025-26). DfE publishes an
updated strategy template each year. Schools using the DfE template
should use the current version. A strategy from 2023-24 or earlier
alone is non-compliant.

Schools should use the DfE's recommended 3-year strategy template,
updated annually with a review of the previous year's impact.

## Review Frequency

Annually. The strategy should be reviewed at the start of each
academic year with updated data on eligible pupils, spending plans,
and previous year's impact assessment.

## Required Content

1. The school's pupil premium allocation for the current academic year (exact £ amount)
2. Number of pupils eligible for pupil premium (including Ever 6 FSM,
   looked-after children, previously looked-after children, service children)
3. A breakdown of how the pupil premium is being spent — specific
   strategies, interventions, and costs per strategy
4. The rationale for each spending decision — why this strategy was chosen
   and what evidence supports it (e.g. EEF Toolkit references)
5. How the school will measure the impact of each strategy
6. Review of the previous year's pupil premium spending:
   a. What was spent and on what
   b. The impact on disadvantaged pupils' outcomes
   c. Whether intended outcomes were achieved
7. Barriers to learning for disadvantaged pupils at this school
8. Desired outcomes linked to the barriers identified
9. How the school ensures disadvantaged pupils benefit from the strategies
   (not just whole-class approaches that happen to include PP pupils)

## Quality Indicators

- Uses the DfE's recommended 3-year strategy template
- References EEF Teaching and Learning Toolkit or other research evidence
- Specific outcome data (attendance, attainment, progress) for disadvantaged
  vs non-disadvantaged pupils
- Named pupil premium lead/champion
- Links to wider school improvement plan
- Tiered approach: teaching, targeted academic support, wider strategies
- Service children identified separately where applicable
- Published as HTML (DfE preference) with downloadable PDF option
- Previous year review includes specific data, not just narrative

## Red Flags

- No pupil premium information found on the website
- Strategy is more than one academic year old
- No spending breakdown — just the total allocation stated
- No impact data from previous year's spending
- Strategies are vague ("additional support" without specifying what)
- No link between identified barriers and chosen strategies
- Total stated does not match expected allocation for school size
  (£1,455 per eligible primary pupil, £1,035 per secondary pupil 2025-26)
- Identical strategy to previous year with no review or update
- No mention of EEF or research evidence to support spending decisions

## Rating Descriptors

### Compliant (80-100)

- Current academic year strategy published
- Allocation amount stated
- Number of eligible pupils stated
- Spending breakdown with specific strategies and costs
- Impact review of previous year's spending with data
- Barriers to learning identified
- Rationale for spending decisions linked to evidence
- Measurable desired outcomes stated

### Partial (40-79)

- Strategy exists but:
  - Missing spending breakdown (just total amount)
  - No previous year impact review
  - Vague strategies without costs
  - No barriers identified
  - Previous academic year strategy without current year update
  - Research evidence not referenced

### Not Found / Non-Compliant (0-39)

- No pupil premium strategy found on the website
- Strategy is 2+ academic years old
- Brief mention of pupil premium without any strategy detail
- Only the allocation amount stated with no strategy

## Notes

- DfE provides a standard template that most schools now use. The template
  has a 3-year structure with annual review sections.
- Some academy trusts publish a trust-wide pupil premium strategy. Individual
  school data should still be published at school level.
- The pupil premium and the PE sport premium are different funding streams
  with different requirements. They should not be combined into one report.
- Looked-after children (LAC) funding is managed differently — £2,530 per
  pupil, managed by the Virtual School Head. Schools may or may not include
  LAC pupil premium in their published strategy.
- Service children premium is £340 per pupil (2025-26). Schools with service
  children should identify this separately.
