Finance: SFVS and Budget Monitoring

Rawdon St Peter's is a maintained voluntary controlled school, so the Schools Financial Value Standard (SFVS) is the relevant DfE financial management framework.

DfE SFVS guidance asks whether the governing body receives clear and concise monitoring reports of the school's budget position at least six times a year. The guidance also says monitoring reports should show income and expenditure against budget, identify variances, explain them and set out what will be done to rebalance the budget.

The practical governance point is that governors need timely and understandable financial information so they can challenge, support and take corrective action while there is still time to affect the budget.

Schoolgle's finance dashboard is designed to support that duty by turning existing exports and reports into clearer governor-ready monitoring: budget position, transactions, variances, projected position, anomalies and narrative explanations.

Source: DfE Schools Financial Value Standard checklist guidance, updated 16 December 2025.

Estates: DfE Good Estate Management for Schools

DfE Good Estate Management for Schools (GEMS) sets out that schools should have governance processes that ensure statutory and necessary estate activities are carried out, and should maintain a complete understanding of statutory compliance requirements and how the estate is performing against them.

It also identifies the need for up-to-date statutory compliance records, maintenance contracts, asbestos information, asset information, condition data and clear board-level accountability.

GEMS health and safety guidance also highlights the importance of maintaining estate-related records such as risk assessments, asbestos records where relevant, fire risk assessment information, statutory maintenance and testing certificates, inspection logs and contractor details.

Schoolgle's estates compliance module provides a practical operating layer for those expectations: statutory checks, due dates, tasks, evidence, helpdesk issues, contractor records, audit trails and reporting.

Source: DfE Good estate management for schools, updated 24 February 2025.
