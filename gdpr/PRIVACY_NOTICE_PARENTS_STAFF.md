## Who are we?

Schoolgle is a software platform that helps schools improve. It is used by school leaders, teachers, governors, and support staff to track school improvement, manage compliance, and prepare for inspections.

**Your school** decides what information to put into Schoolgle. Schoolgle stores and processes that information on behalf of your school.

- **Your school** is the "Data Controller" — they decide what data is collected and why
- **Schoolgle Ltd** is the "Data Processor" — we look after the data securely on the school's behalf

---

## What information does Schoolgle hold?

### If you are a member of school staff:

| What we hold                    | Why                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Your name and email address     | So you can log in and be identified                   |
| Your job title and role         | To give you the right level of access                 |
| Your login activity             | To keep the system secure                             |
| Work you create on the platform | School improvement records, assessments, action plans |

### If you are a parent or carer:

Schoolgle does **not** collect information directly from parents. However, if your child's school uses the Compliance module, the following may be recorded:

| What may be held            | Why                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your name and email address | To record who gave consent for your child                         |
| Your child's name           | To link consent records to the right child                        |
| Consent decisions           | Whether you agreed to photography, trips, medical treatment, etc. |

### If your child is involved in a safeguarding concern:

The school may record limited information about children in the low-level concerns register. This is a legal requirement under safeguarding guidance (Keeping Children Safe in Education). Schoolgle stores this securely on behalf of the school.

---

## Does Schoolgle use AI?

Yes. Schoolgle uses artificial intelligence to help schools with:

- Analysing school documents (policies, improvement plans)
- Transcribing classroom observations from voice recordings
- Scanning rooms for health and safety hazards
- Answering school improvement questions

**Important things to know about AI:**

- Your school's data is **not** used to train AI models
- AI processing happens in real-time — data is not stored by AI providers
- AI providers are based in the US and UK — data is protected by legal agreements
- Only school improvement documents are sent to AI, not personal records

| What              | Where                   | Security                         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Main database     | EU (Frankfurt, Germany) | Encrypted, access controlled     |
| User login system | EU                      | Managed by Google Firebase       |

## How long is data kept?

| Type of data            | How long                                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Staff accounts          | Until the account is deleted, plus 30 days        |
| School improvement data | Until the school deletes it, plus 30 days         |

---

## Who can see the data?

- **School staff** can only see data for their own school
- **Different roles** see different things (e.g., teachers see less than headteachers)
- **Schoolgle staff** can access data only for support purposes, under strict controls
- **AI services** process data temporarily but do not store it
- **Nobody else** can see the data

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

| Right                | What it means                                                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **See your data**    | Ask for a copy of all information held about you                     |
| **Correct mistakes** | Ask for wrong information to be fixed                                |
| **Delete your data** | Ask for your information to be removed                               |
| **Restrict use**     | Ask for your data to stop being used while a concern is investigated |
| **Get a copy**       | Receive your data in a format you can take elsewhere                 |
| **Object**           | Say you don't want your data used for a particular purpose           |

### How to exercise your rights:

**For staff:** Use the "Export My Data" or "Delete My Account" options in Schoolgle Settings, or email dpo@schoolgle.co.uk

**For parents:** Contact your child's school directly. The school is responsible for handling your request. They will work with Schoolgle to provide the information.

**Response time:** Within 30 days

---

## Children's rights

If your child is 13 or older, they can exercise some data rights themselves. For children under 13, a parent or carer must make the request.

If the school records your child's name for consent purposes (e.g., photography permission), you can:

- Ask the school what information is held
- Ask for it to be corrected or deleted
- Withdraw consent at any time
