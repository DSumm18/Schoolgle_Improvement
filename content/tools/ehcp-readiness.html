How this is calculated (assumptions)
  • Risk rating based on: overdue action rate, review backlog vs capacity, and parent complaint frequency
  • GREEN: overdue <15%, reviews manageable within SENCO hours, <1 complaint/month
  • AMBER: overdue 15-30%, reviews stretching capacity, 1-3 complaints/month
  • RED: overdue >30%, reviews exceeding capacity, >3 complaints/month or any tribunal risk
  • Hours per pupil estimate: 0.5-1.0 hrs/week recommended for active EHCP management
  • Tribunal risk increases when overdue actions exceed 30% AND parent complaints are rising
  • These are planning estimates. Every school's SEND context is different.
