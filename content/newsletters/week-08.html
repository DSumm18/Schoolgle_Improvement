Finance

The budget squeeze: three pressures converging

NI increases, energy costs, and pay awards are hitting school budgets simultaneously. Here's the picture.

1. Employer NI — the confirmed hit

The 1.2 percentage point NI increase (13.8% → 15%) is now in payroll. The government's NI grant covers part of it, but for most schools there's a gap of £5,000–£40,000 depending on size. If you haven't modelled this yet, use the calculator below.

2. Energy costs — the unpredictable variable

Energy prices remain volatile. Schools that fixed contracts in 2024 are seeing those deals expire. The DfE energy efficiency scheme helps some, but most schools are managing costs through behaviour change and small capital works.

3. Pay awards — the annual pressure

The STRB recommendation for 2026–27 is expected by May. Based on the pattern, expect 3–5%. Every 1% on your pay bill adds approximately £24,000 for a 80-staff school at £30,000 average salary.

What to do with this number

  • If your net impact is negative: you need to find savings or efficiencies before approving the 2026–27 budget. Start with your largest non-staff cost lines.
  • If staff costs exceed 85%: you have limited flexibility. Consider whether every post is essential at current hours. ICFP analysis is your friend.
  • Share with governors early: Don't wait for the final budget meeting. Share the direction of travel now so the formal meeting is informed, not surprised.
  • Model scenarios: What if the pay award is 3%? What if it's 5%? Governors appreciate seeing the range, not a single number.

Compliance

Admissions: publication deadline 15 March

Determined arrangements must be published on your school website and notified to the local authority and Schools Adjudicator by 15 March.

If your governing body determined arrangements last week (the 28 Feb deadline), your next step is publication. The arrangements must be published in full on your school website, including PAN, oversubscription criteria, and catchment area map where applicable.

You must also send a copy to the local authority and, where relevant, the Schools Adjudicator. Parents can then object to the Adjudicator until 15 May.

Do this next
  • Prepare the website page with full determined arrangements. (1 hour)
  • Send a copy to your local authority admissions team. (15 mins)
  • Notify the Schools Adjudicator where required. (15 mins)
Teaching

SATs: 10 weeks out — logistics matter

The academic preparation is in teachers' hands. The logistics are yours.

Access arrangements. Applications for modified papers and additional time should be submitted early March. If you have pupils who need them and haven't applied, this is urgent.

Invigilation. Who's doing it? Is there cover for absence? What happens if your Year 6 teacher is ill on the day?

Security. Papers must be stored securely from receipt. Designate a secure location. Name the responsible person. Test the process before May.

Do this next
  • Submit access arrangement applications if not already done. (1 hour)
  • Confirm invigilation rota and contingency plan. (30 mins)
  • Designate secure storage location and responsible person for papers. (15 mins)
