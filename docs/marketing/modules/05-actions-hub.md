# Module: Actions Hub (School Improvement)

## One-Line Value
"Turn your school development plan into tracked, research-backed actions that actually get done."

## The Problem
- School Development Plans are written, filed, and forgotten
- Actions are vague ("improve reading outcomes") with no clear steps
- No systematic tracking of what's been done vs what's outstanding
- Staff don't know what EEF research says about what actually works
- SLT spend meeting time asking "where are we with...?" instead of moving forward
- No link between identified priorities and evidence of impact

## What It Does
- **Action Tracking**: Create, assign, and monitor improvement actions with deadlines
- **Dual Status System**: Track both implementation progress AND whether the action is having impact
- **EEF Research Integration**: Every action can be linked to EEF Toolkit strategies with evidence ratings
- **Cost Tracking**: Budget each action with actual vs planned spend
- **Staff Assignment**: Link actions to responsible staff members
- **Evidence Connection**: Link completed actions to evidence in the Ofsted framework
- **AI Suggestions**: Ed AI can suggest research-backed actions for identified improvement areas

## Key Benefits
1. **Accountability** — every action has an owner, deadline, and status
2. **Research-backed** — EEF Toolkit integration means actions are evidence-informed
3. **Visible progress** — SLT can see school improvement at a glance
4. **Impact tracking** — not just "did we do it?" but "did it work?"
5. **Ofsted evidence** — actions link directly to framework areas

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: SDP spreadsheet + ad-hoc tracking + consultant advice on what works
- Standalone worth: **£400-800/year**

## Demo Script (3 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "Think about your school development plan. Right now, without checking — how many actions are on track? How many are overdue?"
2. **Dashboard** (60s): Show actions by status, priority, and owner — traffic light overview
3. **EEF integration** (60s): "You want to improve reading. Here are EEF-rated strategies with evidence strength and cost. Pick one, it becomes a tracked action."
4. **Progress tracking** (30s): Show the dual status — implementation vs impact
5. **Close** (30s): "Your SDP, alive and tracked — not gathering dust in a drawer."

## Pitch Slide Key Points
- Before: SDP in a drawer, vague actions, no accountability, no research link
- After: live tracking, EEF-backed strategies, dual status, visible progress
- "School improvement that actually improves"
