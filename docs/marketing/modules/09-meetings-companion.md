# Module: Meeting Companion

## One-Line Value
"Every meeting — agenda, minutes, actions — structured and tracked automatically."

## The Problem
- Staff meetings, SLT meetings, governor meetings — actions get lost
- Minutes are typed up days later from scribbled notes
- No systematic follow-up on agreed actions
- Meeting templates vary or don't exist
- Time spent on meeting admin instead of the actual work

## What It Does
- **Meeting Templates**: Pre-built templates for SLT, staff, governor, safeguarding, SEND reviews
- **Agenda Builder**: Structured agendas with timed items
- **Live Minutes**: Capture decisions and actions during the meeting
- **Action Tracking**: Every action assigned to someone with a deadline — linked to Actions Hub
- **Attendance**: Track who was present for audit purposes
- **Sign-Off**: Digital approval of minutes

## Key Benefits
1. **Actions tracked** — nothing falls through the cracks
2. **Templates** — consistent meeting structures across the school
3. **Audit trail** — who was there, what was decided, who's responsible
4. **Time saved** — structured capture during meetings, not after
5. **Connected** — meeting actions feed into the school improvement system

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Paper minutes + email action tracking
- Standalone worth: **£200-400/year**

## Demo Script (2 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "How many actions from your last SLT meeting are actually being tracked?"
2. **Meeting flow** (60s): Show template → agenda → minutes → actions in 4 clicks
3. **Close** (30s): "Every meeting produces tracked actions. No more 'I thought someone else was doing that.'"
