# Module: HR & People

## One-Line Value
"Your staff directory, training records, and HR essentials — without the enterprise price tag."

## The Problem
- Many LA schools use spreadsheets for staff records
- Training compliance (safeguarding, first aid, fire marshal) tracked manually
- Single Central Record (SCR) is a paper exercise that's always out of date
- When staff leave, nobody knows what responsibilities need covering
- No overview of staff costs, contract dates, or role distribution
- HR SLAs from the LA cost £1-3K/year and are often slow and generic

## What It Does
- **Staff Directory**: Complete staff profiles with roles, qualifications, contact details
- **Training Matrix**: Track mandatory training with expiry alerts (safeguarding, first aid, etc.)
- **CSV Import/Export**: Bulk import from existing spreadsheets, export for payroll/census
- **Role Management**: Assign statutory roles (DSL, SENCO, Fire Marshal) with succession planning
- **Contract Tracking**: Key dates, probation periods, TLR payments
- **Staff Connectors** (coming): Auto-generate tasks from role responsibilities, monitor ratios

## Key Benefits
1. **Compliance** — training expiry alerts mean safeguarding never lapses
2. **Time saved** — no more updating multiple spreadsheets
3. **Census ready** — export data in the format you need for school workforce census
4. **Continuity** — when staff leave, handover is systematic not chaotic
5. **Oversight** — SLT can see staffing at a glance

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: HR spreadsheets + partial LA SLA
- Standalone worth: **£500-1,000/year**

## Demo Script (3 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "Quick question — if your safeguarding lead is off sick tomorrow, who's the backup? And is their training up to date?"
2. **Staff overview** (60s): Show the directory with role filters, training status indicators
3. **Training alerts** (60s): "See these amber flags? Three staff need safeguarding refresher within 30 days. The system flagged it automatically."
4. **Import** (30s): "Already have a spreadsheet? Import it in 2 clicks. Done."
5. **Close** (30s): "Your staff are your biggest asset and your biggest cost. Know exactly where you stand."

## Pitch Slide Key Points
- Before: spreadsheets, missed training, no role tracking, manual SCR
- After: automated alerts, complete staff profiles, training compliance, census-ready data
- "Know your people, protect your school"
