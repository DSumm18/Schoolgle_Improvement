# Module: Ofsted Readiness & Evidence Scanner

## One-Line Value
"Know exactly where you stand with Ofsted — every day, not just when the call comes."

## The Problem
- Schools scramble to gather evidence when Ofsted calls (often 24-48 hours notice)
- Evidence scattered across Google Drive, OneDrive, filing cabinets, and staff laptops
- No one knows if they have gaps until an inspector points them out
- The EIF 2025 framework changed — many schools haven't caught up
- Consultants charge £500-2,000/day to tell you what's missing

## What It Does
- **AI Evidence Scanner**: Connects to Google Drive/OneDrive, automatically scans documents and maps them to Ofsted framework requirements
- **Framework Tracker**: Visual dashboard showing coverage across all 4 key judgements
- **Gap Analysis**: AI identifies exactly which areas lack evidence — before Ofsted does
- **SEF Generator**: Living Self-Evaluation Form that updates automatically as evidence is added
- **Readiness Score**: At-a-glance percentage showing how prepared you are

## Key Benefits
1. **Always ready** — continuous monitoring, not last-minute panic
2. **Time saved** — AI does in minutes what takes staff days
3. **Framework-aligned** — built to the EIF 2025 specification
4. **Evidence mapped** — every document tagged to specific framework areas
5. **Confidence** — know your strengths and weaknesses before inspectors arrive

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: 3-5 days of head teacher time (£900-1,500) + consultant visit (£500-2,000)
- Standalone worth: **£1,000-2,000/year**

## Demo Script (5 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "How confident are you right now that you have evidence for every Ofsted area? What if you could answer that in 10 seconds?"
2. **Show dashboard** (60s): Show the readiness score and framework heatmap — red/amber/green at a glance
3. **Evidence scanning** (90s): Upload a sample document, watch AI tag it to framework areas in real-time
4. **Gap analysis** (60s): "See these amber areas? That's where you need evidence. The system found this automatically."
5. **SEF generator** (60s): Show how evidence feeds into a living SEF document
6. **Close** (30s): "This runs 24/7. Every time staff add a document to your Drive, Schoolgle checks it."

## Pitch Slide Key Points
- Before: scattered evidence, last-minute panic, expensive consultants
- After: continuous readiness, AI-mapped evidence, always inspection-ready
- "Like having a school improvement partner that never sleeps"
