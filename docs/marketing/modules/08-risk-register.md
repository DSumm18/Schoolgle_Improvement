# Module: Risk Register

## One-Line Value
"See every risk to your school on one page — rated, mitigated, and monitored."

## The Problem
- Most schools don't have a formal risk register (Ofsted and governors expect one)
- Risks are discussed in meetings but not systematically tracked
- No clear ownership of risk mitigation actions
- Governor oversight of risk is informal at best
- When things go wrong, there's no evidence of risk awareness or mitigation

## What It Does
- **Risk Register**: Log risks with likelihood, impact, and risk score (heatmap)
- **Mitigations**: Track actions taken to reduce each risk
- **Heatmap View**: Visual risk matrix showing high/medium/low risks at a glance
- **Decision Log**: Record governance decisions related to risk management
- **Auto-Scoring**: Recalculate risk scores as mitigations are completed
- **AI Risk Suggestions**: Ed can suggest common school risks you may not have considered

## Key Benefits
1. **Governance evidence** — governors can demonstrate risk oversight
2. **Visual clarity** — heatmap makes risk profile instantly understandable
3. **Tracked mitigations** — show what you're doing about each risk
4. **Ofsted ready** — evidence of effective leadership and management
5. **Proactive** — identify and address risks before they become crises

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Ad-hoc risk discussions + lack of formal register
- Standalone worth: **£200-400/year**

## Demo Script (2 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "If a governor asked to see your risk register right now, what would you show them?"
2. **Heatmap** (60s): Show the visual risk matrix with sample school risks
3. **Close** (30s): "Effective governance requires risk oversight. This makes it effortless."

## Pitch Slide Key Points
- Before: no formal register, ad-hoc discussions, no evidence of risk management
- After: visual heatmap, tracked mitigations, governance evidence
- "Risk management that governors and inspectors expect to see"
