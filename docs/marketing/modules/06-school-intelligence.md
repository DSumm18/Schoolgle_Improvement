# Module: School Intelligence Engine

## One-Line Value
"See the patterns in your data that Ofsted will see — before they do."

## The Problem
- Schools collect masses of data but don't have time to analyse it properly
- Pupil assessment data sits in spreadsheets — gaps aren't spotted until too late
- DfE published data (attendance, exclusions, KS2) isn't cross-referenced with school context
- COVID impact on cohorts isn't tracked systematically
- Ofsted inspectors use data to form hypotheses before they arrive — schools should too
- No link between data patterns and research on what interventions work

## What It Does
- **Cohort Tracking**: Follow year groups through their school journey, including COVID impact analysis
- **DfE Data Integration**: Automatic import of attendance, census, KS2, workforce, and exclusions data
- **Pupil Assessment Analysis**: Upload assessment data (GDPR-safe with automatic pseudonymisation) for gap analysis by FSM/SEND/gender/PP
- **Cross-Module Signals**: Correlate data across HR, Estates, Compliance — spot patterns humans miss
- **EEF Strategy Matching**: AI matches identified gaps to EEF Toolkit interventions
- **AI Analysis**: DeepSeek AI analyses all data sources together for pattern detection
- **Inspection Context Builder**: Generates the demographic and contextual narrative inspectors will use

## Key Benefits
1. **Data-informed** — see patterns across all your data sources
2. **GDPR-safe** — pupil names never leave the school (HMAC-SHA256 pseudonymisation)
3. **Research-linked** — every gap comes with EEF intervention suggestions
4. **Inspection-ready** — know what inspectors will see in your published data
5. **Proactive** — spot trends early, not when it's too late to act

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Data analysis time (deputy head, 5+ days/year) + data consultant
- Standalone worth: **£600-1,200/year**

## Demo Script (5 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "An Ofsted inspector has already looked at your published data before they arrive. Do you know what they've seen?"
2. **DfE data view** (60s): Show attendance trends, KS2 comparisons, census data — what inspectors see
3. **Assessment upload** (60s): "Upload your assessment CSV. Names are automatically anonymised — the server never sees them. But look what it finds..."
4. **Gap analysis** (90s): "FSM pupils are 1.2 terms behind in reading in Year 4. That cohort was in Reception during COVID. Here are 3 EEF strategies rated 'high impact' for this gap."
5. **Close** (30s): "This is the conversation the inspector wants to have. Now you're ready for it."
