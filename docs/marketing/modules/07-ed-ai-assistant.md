# Module: Ed AI Assistant

## One-Line Value
"An AI assistant that knows school policy, law, and best practice — available 24/7 for every staff member."

## The Problem
- Head teachers are the "go-to" for every question — HR, estates, safeguarding, data, curriculum
- Staff Google things and get generic (often wrong) advice
- LA advice lines have limited hours and long response times
- Nobody reads the 100+ page staff handbook
- New staff don't know school procedures
- Questions get asked repeatedly — same problems, different staff member

## What It Does
- **12 Specialist Agents**: Estates, HR, SEND, Data, Curriculum, IT, Procurement, Governance, Communications, Forms, Intelligence, General
- **Function Calling**: Ed doesn't just chat — it can create actions, log helpdesk tickets, look up policies, analyse data
- **School Context**: Ed knows your school type, size, phase, and local context
- **Knowledge Base**: Self-improving — learns from your school's specific answers and procedures
- **Proactive Alerts**: Surfaces relevant information based on what staff are working on
- **43+ Skills**: From creating staff records to running intelligence analyses

## Key Benefits
1. **24/7 availability** — staff get answers at 10pm on a Sunday, not just during office hours
2. **Consistent** — same accurate answer every time, based on policy and law
3. **Specialist knowledge** — 12 domain experts, not one generalist
4. **Action-oriented** — doesn't just advise, can actually do things in the system
5. **Reduces head teacher burden** — staff self-serve for common questions
