## Why Ed Is NOT ChatGPT

This is the critical differentiator. Head teachers will say "can't I just use ChatGPT?" — here's why not:

| Capability | ChatGPT | Ed |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| Knows your school name | No (you have to tell it every time) | Yes — auto-loaded from DfE data |
| Knows your school type/phase | No | Yes — primary, secondary, special, faith |
| Sees your compliance status | No | Yes — overdue tasks, expiring training |
| Sees your Ofsted evidence | No | Yes — gaps, strengths, framework coverage |
| Sees your DfE published data | No | Yes — attendance, KS2, workforce trends |
| Can create a helpdesk ticket | No | Yes — logged, assigned, tracked |
| Can create an improvement action | No | Yes — with EEF research backing |
| Can run a data analysis | No | Yes — pupil assessment gap analysis |
| Trained on education policy | Generic | Purpose-built with education law, DfE guidance, Ofsted framework |
| Understands school context | No — treats every question the same | Yes — a faith school gets different SIAMS context |
| GDPR compliant for school use | Questionable — data sent to OpenAI | Yes — UK servers, no pupil data, pseudonymised |
| Costs | £20/month per person (£240/yr × 30 staff = £7,200) | £995 for unlimited staff |

### The ChatGPT Cost Trap
"If you gave ChatGPT Plus to just 5 staff members, that's £1,200/year. And they'd get generic answers with no school context. Ed costs £995 for your entire staff with full school context."
