# Module: Estates Compliance

## One-Line Value
"Never miss a statutory check again — fire safety, legionella, asbestos, all tracked automatically."

## The Problem
- Schools have 50+ statutory compliance requirements for their buildings
- Most track them on spreadsheets or paper — things get missed
- Contractors tell you what needs doing (and charge for it) — but what's actually statutory?
- A missed legionella flush or fire alarm test can result in prosecution
- Insurance can be voided if compliance lapses
- Caretakers carry knowledge in their heads — what happens when they leave?

## What It Does
- **Compliance Calendar**: Every statutory requirement scheduled with automated reminders
- **Task Management**: Assign, track, and evidence completion of all estates tasks
- **Contractor Management**: Track who does what, contract dates, costs, and certifications
- **Asset Register**: Record all building assets with location, condition, and maintenance schedules
- **Helpdesk**: Staff can report issues, track resolution, maintain audit trail
- **Statutory vs Good Practice**: AI separates what's legally required from contractor upselling
- **Mobile Inspections**: Walk-round checklists with photo evidence
- **Budget Planning**: Track actual spend vs budget on estates

## Key Benefits
1. **Legal protection** — statutory compliance evidenced and auditable
2. **Nothing missed** — automated reminders for every check and inspection
3. **Contractor control** — know what's statutory vs what's a nice-to-have
4. **Knowledge retention** — when caretaker leaves, the system has everything
5. **Cost control** — budget tracking and spend analysis

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Estates management spreadsheets + paper systems + risk of non-compliance
- Avoids: Prosecution risk, insurance voidance, HSE enforcement
- Standalone worth: **£800-1,500/year**

1. **Hook** (30s): "Can you tell me right now — is your legionella flushing up to date? When was your last fire alarm test? If you're not sure, that's exactly the problem."
