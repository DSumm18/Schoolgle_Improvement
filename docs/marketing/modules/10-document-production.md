# Module: Document Production

## One-Line Value
"Generate professional school documents — policies, newsletters, reports — in minutes, not hours."

## The Problem
- School documents take hours to produce (newsletters, policy updates, reports)
- Formatting is inconsistent across the school
- Statutory documents (PP statement, sports premium, SDP) are often late or rushed
- No templates — each document starts from scratch
- Sending documents to parents/staff is manual

## What It Does
- **Document Templates**: Pre-built templates for common school documents
- **AI Generation**: Ed AI can draft newsletters, policy documents, and reports
- **Professional Formatting**: Consistent branding and layout
- **Send/Distribute**: Email documents directly to parents or staff groups
- **Version Control**: Track document history and changes

## Key Benefits
1. **Time saved** — AI-assisted drafting cuts production time by 80%
2. **Professional** — consistent, well-formatted documents
3. **Statutory compliance** — templates for required documents
4. **Distribution** — send directly from the platform
5. **Audit trail** — version history for every document

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Hours of admin time on document production
- Standalone worth: **£300-500/year**

## Demo Script (2 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "How long does your weekly newsletter take? What if it took 5 minutes?"
2. **Generate** (60s): "Ed, draft a newsletter for this week including the Year 4 trip and safeguarding reminder" — show the AI-generated result
3. **Close** (30s): "Professional documents, AI-assisted, sent in minutes."
