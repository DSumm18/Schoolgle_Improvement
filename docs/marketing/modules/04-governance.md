# Module: Governance Portal

## One-Line Value
"Give your governors everything they need — meetings, training, policies, visits — in one place."

## The Problem
- Governor information scattered across emails, shared drives, and paper files
- Training compliance is hard to track — Ofsted asks about governor development
- Meeting agendas and minutes are emailed around, versions get confused
- Governor visits happen but aren't recorded systematically
- Clerk spends hours on admin that could be automated
- Governors don't always know what they should be monitoring

## What It Does
- **Governor Directory**: Profiles with terms, committees, DBS status, declarations of interest
- **Meeting Management**: Agendas, papers, attendance, actions, minutes — all in one place
- **Training Tracker**: Monitor governor training with recommendations for gaps
- **Policy Management**: Track policy review cycles, approval status, and version history
- **Visit Records**: Structured governor visit forms with evidence linking
- **Board Dashboard**: Overview of governance health, upcoming deadlines, actions outstanding

## Key Benefits
1. **Organised** — no more hunting through emails for meeting papers
2. **Compliant** — training and DBS tracked with automated alerts
3. **Evidence** — governance activity linked to Ofsted framework areas
4. **Efficient** — less admin time for clerk and chair
5. **Strategic** — governors can focus on challenge and support, not paperwork

## Value to School (Annual)
- Replaces: Email-based governance admin + paper visit records
- Standalone worth: **£400-800/year**

## Demo Script (3 Minutes)
1. **Hook** (30s): "How do you share board papers with governors? Email attachments? What happens when someone asks for last term's minutes?"
2. **Board overview** (60s): Show governor profiles, committee assignments, training status
3. **Meeting workflow** (60s): "Create agenda, attach papers, record attendance, capture actions — governors access it all from their phone."
4. **Close** (30s): "Ofsted want to see effective governance. This is your evidence."

## Pitch Slide Key Points
- Before: emails, paper files, lost minutes, training gaps unknown
- After: structured governance, tracked training, evidenced visits, Ofsted-ready
- "Professional governance, without the enterprise cost"
