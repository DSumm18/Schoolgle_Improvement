# Idea: School Calendar Campaign Themes

**Status:** 💡 Idea
**Rating:** —
**Season:** All Year
**Audience:** HT / SBM
**Channel:** LinkedIn / Email / Social

## The Idea

A year-round content calendar tied to what schools are actually dealing with each month. Not generic B2B marketing — school-specific pain points at the exact moment they hurt most.

## Campaign Ideas by Month

### September — "Back to School, Sorted"
- **Pain point:** New term chaos, new staff, systems not set up
- **Angle:** "Start the year ahead, not behind"
- **Content:** Checklist infographic — "10 things your school should have in place by Week 2"
- **Push modules:** Staff Directory, Estates Compliance, Governance

### October — "Ofsted Season"
- **Pain point:** Ofsted calls start coming, evidence isn't ready
- **Angle:** "The call comes. Are you ready?"
- **Content:** Mock phone call script / "What would you say?" interactive
- **Push modules:** Ofsted Readiness, Evidence Scanner, Living SEF

### November — "Data Drop"
- **Pain point:** Census returns, data headaches, GDPR anxiety
- **Angle:** "Your data works for you, not the other way round"
- **Content:** Before/after comparison — spreadsheet hell vs Intelligence dashboard
- **Push modules:** School Intelligence, Data analysis, Ed AI

### December — "Year in Review"
- **Pain point:** Exhaustion, reflecting on what worked
- **Angle:** "What if next year was different?" / lighthearted Christmas content
- **Content:** "12 Days of School Improvement" countdown, each day = one module
- **Push modules:** All — brand awareness play

### January — "New Year, New Systems"
- **Pain point:** New year energy, wanting to fix what's broken
- **Angle:** "Your New Year's resolution: stop using 5 logins"
- **Content:** Resolution-style posts, "I will stop..." series
- **Push modules:** Platform overview, consolidation message

### February — "Inspection Ready"
- **Pain point:** Spring Ofsted wave, governors asking awkward questions
- **Angle:** "Your governors are asking. Do you have answers?"
- **Content:** Governor question cards — "Can you show me evidence of...?"
- **Push modules:** Governance, Ofsted, Evidence Scanner

### March — "Budget Crunch"
- **Pain point:** LA budget deadline 31 March, every penny scrutinised
- **Angle:** "£1,995 for everything vs £10,000 for bits and pieces"
- **Content:** Motorway sign / cost comparison visuals
- **Push modules:** Pricing, ROI calculator, Ed vs staff cost (11p/hr)

### April — "Fresh Start" (Academy budget year)
- **Pain point:** Academies start new financial year, procurement window
- **Angle:** "New budget. Smart spend."
- **Content:** "What could you buy with the money you're wasting on separate tools?"
- **Push modules:** Full platform, MAT pricing

### May — "SATs & SEND"
- **Pain point:** SATs pressure, SEND reviews, mental health awareness
- **Angle:** "Every child's data, understood — not just the averages"
- **Content:** Pupil assessment gap analysis demo, anonymised case study
- **Push modules:** Intelligence, Pupil Assessment, SEND (when built)

### June — "Transition Time"
- **Pain point:** Year 6 transition, staffing changes for September
- **Angle:** "Who's leaving? Who's covering? Is anything falling through the cracks?"
- **Content:** Staff connector / handover scenario
- **Push modules:** HR, Staff Connectors (when built), Actions Hub

### July — "Get Ready for September"
- **Pain point:** End of year admin, setting up for next year
- **Angle:** "September you will thank July you"
- **Content:** "Summer setup guide" — what to configure before the holidays
- **Push modules:** Onboarding, setup guides, founding school offer

### August — "The Quiet Window"
- **Pain point:** Heads are planning but schools are empty
- **Angle:** Direct outreach — "While it's quiet, let me show you something"
- **Content:** Personal demo invitations, not mass marketing
- **Push modules:** Demo bookings, founding school pricing

## Why It Could Work

- Schools don't think in Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 — they think in terms and half-terms
- Hitting pain points at the exact right moment makes content feel relevant, not salesy
- Creates a content machine — 12 months of planned themes, each with multiple content pieces
- Seasonal content is shareable — teachers love "that's so true right now" posts

## Effort to Build
