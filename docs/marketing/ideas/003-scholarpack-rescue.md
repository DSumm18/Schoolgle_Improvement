# Idea: ScholarPack Rescue — Data Lifeline for Displaced Schools

**Status:** 🤔 Considering
**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Season:** NOW — urgent, time-limited window (Feb-Sep 2026)
**Audience:** HT / SBM / IT
**Channel:** LinkedIn / Email / Direct outreach / LA briefings

## The Idea

1,600+ ScholarPack primary schools are being force-migrated to Arbor (Feb 2026). Schools are stressed, confused, and sitting on CSV data exports they don't know what to do with.

**Schoolgle offers a "ScholarPack Rescue" package:**

1. **Data Import Tool** — take ScholarPack CSV exports (staff, assessments, SEND, attendance) and pull them into Schoolgle modules
2. **Staff Connectors** — capture who does what before institutional knowledge is lost in the migration chaos (DSL, SENCO, fire marshal, legionella lead, etc.)
3. **Ed AI Transition Support** — "Ask Ed anything about your school data while you sort out your MIS"
4. **Everything ScholarPack never did** — Ofsted readiness, estates compliance, governance portal, school improvement — available immediately

**The pitch:** "Your MIS just disappeared. We'll catch your data and give you everything ScholarPack couldn't."

## Why It Could Work

- **Time-limited urgency** — these schools need help NOW, not in 6 months
- **Emotional trigger** — they didn't choose this. They're being pushed. We're the helping hand
- **Low competition** — Arbor is the only option being presented. Nobody else is targeting displaced ScholarPack schools specifically
- **Land and expand** — get them on Schoolgle for the rescue, keep them for the value
- **Connectors angle** — migration is exactly when responsibilities get lost. Our Staff Connectors tool is purpose-built for this moment
- **Ed AI differentiator** — "While you're figuring out Arbor, Ed's already answering your questions"
