# Marketing Ideas Board

Dump ideas here. Assess later. Build campaigns from the best ones.

## How to Use

1. **Add ideas** — Drop any idea into this folder as a numbered file (e.g., `003-idea-name.md`)
2. **Don't filter yet** — Bad ideas sometimes spark good ones. Get them all down.
3. **Review & score** — Come back periodically and update the status/rating
4. **Build campaigns** — Group ideas by season, audience, or theme below

## Campaign Themes by School Calendar

Use these windows when planning content. Schools think in terms, not quarters.

### Autumn Term (September – December)
- **September**: New year energy, fresh starts, "get set up right" messaging
- **October**: Half-term planning, Ofsted season ramps up, governors' first meeting
- **November**: Budget planning begins, data drops (census), anti-bullying week
- **December**: Christmas — lighter content, "year in review", "new year new systems"

### Spring Term (January – March)
- **January**: New year resolutions, "start the term ahead not behind"
- **February**: Half-term, Ofsted still heavy, mock inspections
- **March**: Budget deadline (LA schools 31 March), end of financial year, Easter prep

### Summer Term (April – July)
- **April**: New financial year (academies), fresh budget = buying window
- **May**: SATs week, SEND annual reviews, Mental Health Awareness Week
- **June**: Transition planning, staffing for September, sports day season
- **July**: End of year reports, leavers, "get ready for September" push

### Holidays & Off-Peak
- **August**: Decision-making window — heads planning, no distractions, good for demos
- **Half-terms**: Heads catch up on strategic thinking — email campaigns work well
- **Bank holidays**: Avoid — nobody's reading
