# Idea: Motorway Route Sign — "The Old Road vs Schoolgle"

**Status:** ✅ Built
**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Season:** Any
**Audience:** HT / SBM / LA
**Channel:** LinkedIn / Presentation / LA Briefings

## The Idea

A UK motorway overhead gantry sign showing two routes:
- **Left exit (amber)** — "The Old Road" — SIMS, Bromcom, Arbor, The Key, spreadsheets
- **Straight ahead (green)** — "Schoolgle" — one platform, AI-powered, built for schools

The visual metaphor: competitors are the exit ramp to a dead end. Schoolgle is the road ahead.

Headline: **"Which route is your school on?"**

## Why It Could Work

- Instantly recognisable — every head teacher has sat on the M1/M6 staring at these signs
- Makes a complex comparison (11 modules vs 5+ tools) feel simple and visual
- The "old road" framing puts competitors on the back foot without being aggressive
- Works as a single-image LinkedIn post, pitch deck slide, or printed handout
- The price comparison (£5-10K vs £1,995) lands hard in this format
