## Products

### 1. School MIS (Primary, Secondary, Special)
Cloud-based management information system for individual schools.

**Tiered Packages:**
| Tier | What It Adds |
|------|-------------|
| **Core** | Attendance, Behaviour, Progress Tracking, Census & Statutory Reporting, Custom Report Builder, Student & Staff Profiles, Email, SMS |
| **Comms** | Parent Portal, Parents Evening Booking, Arbor App, Student Report Cards, Student Portal, Meals management, Payments |
| **Perform** | Curriculum Tracking, Intervention Tracking, Provision Map, Assignments, My Classroom, Seating Plans, School Shop, Staff Performance |

### 2. MAT MIS (Group MIS)
Unified trust-level management with central analytics hub.

- School and MAT MIS work as a "seamless pair" with bidirectional data flow
- Central team can act on data without logging into individual schools
- Powered by data warehouse combining MIS, Assessment, HR, Finance, and ASP data
- Cross-school suspension monitoring, behaviour analytics, assessment comparison
- Roll out assessment target rules from MAT to schools

### 3. Arbor Insight (Benchmarking)
Separate benchmarking analytics product.

- Analyses ASP (Analyse School Performance) data
- 7 personalised PDF reports per school
- 3-year attainment trends
- Gap identification between student groups

### 4. Arbor AI (Add-on)
AI features built into the MIS.

**Free monthly allowance includes:**
- **Ask Arbor** - AI assistant for instant school/student data queries, can take action (send comms, create interventions, log absences, find rooms)
- **Suggest Email** - Draft emails, letters, SMS
- **Student Summary** - Instant student report generation
- **Suggest Formula** - Natural language to custom tables/formulas

**Paid add-on:**
- **Auto Absence** - Listens to, transcribes, translates (EAL), and logs absence voicemails; attendance teams just approve. Separate add-on pricing.

**AI Infrastructure**: Uses OpenAI with IDTA (International Data Transfer Agreement) for GDPR compliance.

### 5. BI Connector
- Connect to Power BI or your own data warehouse
- Live National Benchmarks from 6,000+ Arbor schools
- Updated daily: attendance, suspension, exclusion, trip rates
- Included in MIS Plus tier for MATs; paid add-on for individual schools

## Pay-for-One Promise (Key Sales Strategy)

Arbor's headline competitive move against SIMS:
- **Promise**: "Your school should only pay for one MIS at a time"
- **Mechanic**: If locked into paying for SIMS until 2027 or 2028, Arbor Core license is FREE until that contract ends
- **Conditions**: Must fully adopt Arbor and stop using SIMS (verified via census); must give notice to SIMS
- **Upsell**: Can upgrade to Comms or Perform by paying the difference
- **Context**: SIMS forced 3-year contracts; Arbor is exploiting schools' frustration with lock-in

## Competitive Weaknesses (Opportunities for Schoolgle)

1. **No school improvement tools** - Arbor is purely MIS; no Ofsted readiness, SEF, or improvement planning
2. **No estates/compliance management** - No facilities, health & safety, or compliance tracking
3. **No governance portal** - No governor meeting management, training tracking, or policy management
4. **AI is MIS-centric only** - Ask Arbor queries MIS data; doesn't help with strategic school improvement
5. **No document/evidence scanning** - No cloud storage scanning or evidence mapping to frameworks
6. **Pricing at scale is expensive** - Large secondaries could pay £15k+/year for Perform tier
7. **Parent app complaints** - Poor UX for parents is a known weakness
8. **Lock-in concerns** - ScholarPack/Integris discontinuation shows consolidation strategy
9. **OpenAI dependency** - Using US-based AI provider raises ongoing GDPR questions despite IDTA
10. **No EEF research integration** - No evidence-based intervention recommendations
