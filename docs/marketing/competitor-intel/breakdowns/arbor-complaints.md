## 1. Unintuitive, Clunky Interface

- **Problem**: Everything buried deep in menus. Simple tasks require excessive clicks. "Hours going round and round in circles." "Antiquated by every modern IT metric"
- **Who**: School admin, SBMs, parents
- **Tone**: Exasperated — "the worst MIS ever"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Ed AI eliminates menu-diving. Ask in plain English, get answers
- **Sources**: [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/arbor-education.com), [EduGeek](https://www.edugeek.net/forums/topic/210177-is-anyone-else-struggling-with-arbor-as-much-as-we-are/)

## 2. Chronic Login/Password Failures

- **Problem**: Random logouts, password resets don't send, "wrong password" when correct. Users set aside 15 minutes just to log in
- **Who**: Parents, teachers, staff
- **Tone**: Furious — "shockingly bad password management"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Firebase Auth with Google/Microsoft SSO. One-click login
- **Sources**: [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/arbor-education.com), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arboreducation.parentapp&hl=en_GB)

## 3. Frequent Cloud Outages (7.1/month)

- **Problem**: 72+ outages in 8 months. One lasted 14h19m. Schools locked out during payday. Can't take attendance or run safeguarding checks
- **Who**: All school staff — especially office managers, DSLs
- **Tone**: Panicked — "full outage during payday; customers locked out"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Schoolgle's compliance tracking and estates tasks work independently of MIS
- **Sources**: [IsDown](https://isdown.app/status/arbor-education), [StatusGator](https://statusgator.com/services/arbor-education)

## 4. Abysmal Customer Support ("Black Hole")

- **Problem**: Days to respond. Replies are just links to help articles. Sales team disappears after contract signed. No project managers assigned
- **Who**: IT managers, SBMs, data managers
- **Tone**: Betrayed — "the black hole of support," "generally ignored"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Ed AI instant 24/7 support across 12 specialist domains
- **Sources**: [EduGeek](https://www.edugeek.net/forums/topic/221783-arbor-support-or-lack-of/)

- **Problem**: No siblings linked (hundreds of duplicate contacts). SEN needs don't migrate. Former roll students have no destination schools. DfE codes wrong. Staff forced to manually re-enter
- **Who**: Data managers, SENCOs, IT managers
- **Tone**: Dismayed — "migrations have been pretty poor"

## 6. Custom Reporting Limitations

- **Problem**: Assessment columns can't be exported. No grouping by week. No report folders. Must pay for "Advanced Support" to get custom reports
- **Who**: Data managers, SBMs, SLT
- **Tone**: Frustrated — paying extra for basic functionality

- **Problem**: Can't select child by name (only by school). Must force-close to switch children. Separate logins per child. Payments applied to wrong child
