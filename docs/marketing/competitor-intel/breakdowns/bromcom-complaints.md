# Bromcom User Complaints — 18 Real Pain Points from Forums, Reviews & Media

**Last updated:** 2026-03-23
**Sources:** Trustpilot, EduGeek forums, Schools Week, The Register, WhichMIS, The Key Group 2024 Survey, Google Play, Apple App Store

---

## The Headline Numbers

- Trustpilot rating: **1.7/5 ("Bad")**
- The Key 2024 overall satisfaction: **6.7/10** (second lowest)
- Integration satisfaction: **57%** (lowest of all providers)
- Only **20%** of users "extremely satisfied" (vs 34% Arbor)
- September 2025 outage: **5 days, 2 days at 100% degradation**
- Revenue growing to **£32M** despite complaints (riding SIMS exodus)

## 1. Catastrophic Start-of-Year Outages (Annual Pattern)

- **Problem**: September 2025 — "100% severe degradation" for 2 full days. Schools couldn't take registers, access timetables, or contact parents. Described as "becoming an annual problem"
- **Who**: Headteachers, MAT leaders, attendance officers
- **Tone**: Fury, panic — "car crash"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Position as "resilience layer" — Schoolgle works independently when the MIS goes down
- **Sources**: [Schools Week](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/mis-outage-leaves-schools-in-the-lurch/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/09/10/bromcom_fail/)

## 2. Safeguarding Compromised During Outages

- **Problem**: Can't access safeguarding logs, fire registers, or parental contacts during downtime. Sharples School formally complained about impact on safeguarding. Trustpilot reviewer asked: "Is Bromcom a safeguarding issue?"
- **Who**: DSLs, headteachers
- **Tone**: Alarm — safeguarding is non-negotiable
- **Schoolgle angle**: Independent safeguarding evidence trail via compliance module and cloud storage scanning
- **Sources**: [Schools Week](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/mis-outage-leaves-schools-in-the-lurch/), [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/bromcom.com)

## 3. System Crashes at 9am (Register Time)

- **Problem**: "Always seems to fail at 9 AM." Teachers forced to paper registers. One wrote: "Thanks to Bromcom I have been transported back to 1993: Paper registers, children running slips of paper down to student support"
- **Who**: Teachers, form tutors
- **Tone**: Dark humour, exasperation
- **Sources**: [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/bromcom.com)

## 4. Overly Complicated Interface

- **Problem**: "So overly complicated and difficult to use," "slow, illogical," "too many tabs you will never use." Only 16% rate it "extremely satisfied" for intuitiveness
- **Who**: Teachers, support staff, new users
- **Tone**: Bewilderment — "feels like a cheap version of ClassCharts made in a weekend"
- **Schoolgle angle**: Ed AI provides natural language interface — ask questions, get answers, no navigation
- **Sources**: [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/bromcom.com), [The Key Group 2024](https://thekeygroup.com/news-insights/ggqwz19jr0cxwldeh74rc1yhs05h7z)

## 5. Increased Workload (Not Reduced)

- **Problem**: "This program has increased my workload." Everything "very long winded compared to our previous MIS." Staff working "early mornings and into the evenings to rectify issues"
- **Who**: Teachers, SBMs, admin
- **Tone**: Exhaustion, resentment
- **Schoolgle angle**: "AI that saves time, not software that wastes it"
- **Sources**: [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/bromcom.com)

## 6. Support: "Blame the User, Close the Ticket"

- **Problem**: EduGeek thread titled exactly that. Bug reports closed, told to submit "feature requests." Support team "do not have a direct way of passing items to product." Feature requests with votes "disappeared or been deleted"
- **Who**: IT managers, data managers
- **Tone**: Cynicism, distrust
- **Schoolgle angle**: "An AI that helps vs a helpdesk that blames"
- **Sources**: [EduGeek](https://www.edugeek.net/forums/topic/220752-bromcoms-idea-of-support-blame-the-user-close-the-ticket-and-hope-they-go-away/)

## 7. 1-Hour Phone Wait Times

- **Problem**: "1 hour on the phone to get through to support every day." Hours limited to 8am-5pm (4pm Wednesdays). Issues get lost, require constant chasing
- **Who**: IT managers, office managers
- **Tone**: Helplessness
- **Schoolgle angle**: Ed AI available 24/7 at 11p/hour. No phone queues. No Wednesday early closing
- **Sources**: [EduGeek](https://www.edugeek.net/forums/topic/221031-bromcom-customer-support-are-awful/)

## 8. Rude Support Staff

- **Problem**: "Staff are rude, never get back to you, refuse to send invoices." When asked how to make a complaint: told the company "couldn't provide that information"

## 9. Parent App Causing Phone Calls
