## Products

### 1. SIMS Next Gen (Cloud MIS) — Flagship Product

The cloud-native, mobile-first rebuild of SIMS. Being rolled out in phases alongside classic SIMS 7 during transition. No additional cost for current SIMS customers.

**Live Features:**
- **Statutory Attendance Register** — Take session registers anywhere, on any device. Quick, efficient, with comments and "minutes late" in one window
- **Lesson Attendance Register** — Classroom-level attendance tracking
- **My Markbook** — Teachers create and manage assessments directly, no Data Manager needed. Capture continuous assessments (homework, coursework), add notes, filter by SEND/PP groups
- **Conduct Management** — Redesigned behaviour tracking with customisable conduct event types, severity levels, positive/negative recording, conduct dashboards, exclusion/suspension management
- **Class View** — All-in-one classroom management tool: attendance, seating plans, pupil info, conduct events. AI-powered smart seating plans coming
- **Staff Profile** — Staff information management
- **Census** — Summer 2026 Census can only be completed in Next Gen (forcing adoption)
- **Pupil Profile** — Student information and records

**Attendance Reporting Suite:**
- Attendance by Category (for governors and MAT leaders)
- Attendance by Cohort (identify trends across groups)
- Attendance by Code (in-depth pattern view)
- Attendance Tracker (spot patterns over time)

**Coming Soon (Roadmap):**
- Reporting module
- Communications module
- Finance module
- AI-powered Smart Seating Plans
- Parent messaging for safeguarding communications

**Rollout Status**: Phased — schools can move at their own pace using Next Gen alongside SIMS 7. Programme set to complete by end of 2026.

### 2. SIMS 7 (Classic / On-Premise)

The legacy on-premise MIS still in use by many schools during transition.

**Modules include:**
- Core student/staff records
- Registration and attendance
- Assessment and reporting
- Behaviour management
- Timetabling
- Options management
- Statutory returns (Census, School Workforce)
- Fee billing (independent schools)
- Financial management
- Examinations management

### 3. SIMS Connected (MAT/Trust Solution)

Unified cloud access for multi-academy trusts.

- Single SIMS ID accesses all school sites within a Trust
- Changes made from one location (e.g., marksheet updates) apply across trust
- HR team can access all sites without physical visits or multiple logins
- No changes to interface or data structure during migration (minimal training)

### 4. EntrySign (Visitor Management)

- Digital visitor management system
- Records attendance of staff, pupils, visitors, contractors
- Monitors who is onsite at all times
- Integrates with SIMS registers (late marks for students)
- Links with Paxton access systems
- "Almost instant" fire registers

### 5. Parents' Evening System (SchoolCloud)

- Online booking system for parents' evenings
- Paperless appointment management
- Admin workload reduction

### 6. FMS (Financial Management System)

- Cloud-based financial management for schools
- Built for LA maintained schools, academies, and independents
- DfE-compliant with built-in validation
- Automatic submission for Consistent Financial Reporting and Accounts Returns

### 7. SIMS InTouch (Communications)

- School-to-home communication platform
- Integrated with SIMS data

## Pricing

### Published Pricing

SIMS does not publicly list pricing. Schools must contact ESS or a SIMS support partner for quotes.

### Known Pricing Intelligence

- **Model**: Per-pupil pricing with module-based licensing
- **Estimated range**: ~$5 per student per month (from Capterra, may be approximate)
- **Historic reference**: ~£5,000/year for a single-site licence (older forum data)
- **LA-maintained schools**: Sometimes centrally funded from Dedicated Schools Grant via de-delegation
- **SIMS Next Gen**: No additional cost for existing SIMS customers (included in contract)
- **Training**: Offered at high price (noted as a criticism)
- **Contract terms**: Multi-year contracts (3-year terms common); criticised as restrictive with no easy exit
- **Price cutting**: ESS has been "radically" cutting prices to stem school exodus (Schools Week, 2023-2024)
- **SIMS API**: Free/reduced for schools; paid yearly fee for third-party companies

## Pros (from Reviews)

1. **Comprehensive**: Everything in one place — attendance, behaviour, assessment, statutory returns, timetabling
2. **Established**: 40+ years of education domain knowledge; deep statutory compliance understanding
3. **Ecosystem**: Part of ParentPay Group with payments, communications, visitor management, finance
4. **Scale**: 19,000+ schools — large community and partner network
5. **Reporting**: Powerful data reporting and analytics once set up
6. **Scalable**: Accommodates school growth — easily add students, teachers, courses
7. **Security**: Robust data protection features, GDPR compliance
8. **Mobile**: Next Gen is mobile-first — access from any device
9. **No extra cost**: Next Gen included in existing contracts

---

## Cons (from Reviews)

1. **Declining market share**: Lost market leadership to Arbor; perceived as legacy/outdated
2. **Complex interface**: Not user-friendly even in Next Gen; correcting mistakes requires significant effort
3. **Expensive training**: Training offered at high prices and may not cover everything needed
4. **Restrictive contracts**: 3-year lock-in; no easy exit; criticised as inflexible
5. **Slow Next Gen rollout**: Phased approach means many features still missing; schools stuck on SIMS 7
6. **Report creation**: Difficult to create initial reports; users want more templates
7. **On-premise legacy**: Many schools still running local servers with patching/backup overhead
8. **Price cutting desperation**: Aggressive discounting signals vulnerability
9. **Forced migration**: Summer 2026 Census forcing Next Gen adoption before features are ready
10. **Support variability**: Different SIMS support units (local authority partners) provide inconsistent quality

## Competitive Weaknesses (Opportunities for Schoolgle)

1. **No school improvement tools** — SIMS is purely MIS; no Ofsted readiness, SEF, improvement planning, or action tracking
2. **No estates/compliance management** — No facilities management, health & safety, fire safety, legionella, or compliance tracking
3. **No governance portal** — No governor meeting management, training tracking, or policy management
4. **No evidence scanning** — No cloud storage scanning or evidence mapping to inspection frameworks
5. **No EEF research integration** — No evidence-based intervention recommendations
6. **AI is minimal** — Only smart seating plans announced; no AI assistant, no AI-powered analysis
7. **Forced migration anxiety** — Schools facing mandatory Next Gen adoption may be open to complementary tools
8. **Contract renewal window** — 2025-2026 is the critical window when most SIMS contracts renew
9. **No risk management** — No risk register, heatmap, or mitigation tracking
10. **No document production** — No template-based document generation for policies, letters, reports
11. **No meeting management** — No meeting companion, minutes generation, or action tracking
12. **No survey tools** — No built-in stakeholder feedback or survey capabilities
