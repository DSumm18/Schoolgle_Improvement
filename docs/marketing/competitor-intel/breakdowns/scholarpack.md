# ScholarPack — Competitor Intelligence

**Last scraped:** 2026-03-23
**Status:** DISCONTINUED (Feb 2026) — all schools being migrated to Arbor

---

## Key Finding: Opportunity Window

ScholarPack shut down Feb 2026. Their 1,600+ primary schools are being **forcibly migrated to Arbor**. This creates immediate opportunity:
- Schools valued ScholarPack's **simplicity** — Arbor is more complex
- Small primaries valued **affordability** — Arbor pricing is higher
- Forced migration = **disruption and dissatisfaction**
- Schools actively searching for alternatives RIGHT NOW

**Action:** Target ScholarPack schools with messaging around simplicity, affordability, and "you don't have to go to Arbor."

## What Users Will Miss (= What We Should Emphasise)

1. **Simplicity** — ScholarPack was dead simple. Arbor is not. Schoolgle should emphasise ease of use
2. **Primary focus** — felt like it was built for them, not scaled down from secondary
3. **Affordable** — small school budgets. Our £1,995 all-in is competitive
4. **Personal support** — phone calls, patience, UK-based. Ed AI gives 24/7 instant help
5. **Quick onboarding** — 1 day training. We should match or beat this
