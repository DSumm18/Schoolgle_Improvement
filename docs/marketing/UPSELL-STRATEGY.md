## Add-On 1: Ed Website Chat (The Big Upsell)

### What It Is
Ed AI sits on the school website as a chat widget, answering parent and visitor questions 24/7.

### Price: £995/year on top of base

### Why Schools Will Want It

**The "I want that" moment:**
Staff are using Ed internally — asking about policies, procedures, compliance. The head sees it working and thinks: "What if parents could get this too?"

**What Ed does on the website:**
- Answers parent FAQs: term dates, uniform, admissions, wraparound care
- Provides school information: Ofsted report, policies, contact details
- Handles enquiries: "How do I apply for a place?" → structured guidance
- Multi-language support: parents who don't speak English can chat in their language
- Reduces office calls: the top 20 questions parents ask, answered instantly
- Available 24/7: parents checking at 10pm get an answer, not voicemail

**What Ed does NOT do on the website:**
- No access to student data (GDPR safe)
- No access to internal school systems
- Only shares publicly available information
- Escalates complex queries to the school office

### Revenue Maths
- 50 schools × £995 = £49,750 additional ARR
- If 40% of School in a Box customers add this = ~£20K from first cohort
- Very high margin — same Ed infrastructure, just a different context

### Sales Trigger
After 1-2 months of using Schoolgle, send email:
> "Your staff asked Ed 247 questions last month. Imagine if parents could do the same — on your website, 24/7, in any language. Add Ed Website Chat for £995/year."

## Pricing Presentation Strategy

### At the heads' briefing:
- Lead with £1,995 School in a Box — that's the no-brainer
- Do NOT mention add-ons at the initial pitch
- Let them sign up and experience the value first

### After 30-60 days:
- Introduce Ed Website Chat as "something other schools are asking for"
- Frame it as "your parents are already trying to contact you at 9pm — Ed can handle that"
- £995 feels small after they've already committed to £1,995

### The £3K natural target:
- School in a Box (£1,995) + Ed Website Chat (£995) = £2,990
- Round to "about £3K for everything including the public-facing AI"
- This is still less than most schools pay for their MIS alone
- Head can still approve without governors (under £5K threshold)

## What NOT to Upsell (Avoid These Mistakes)

| Don't do this | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Mention add-ons at initial sale | Complicates the "no-brainer" message |
| Create feature-gated frustrations | Never block core features to force upgrades |
| Charge per user | Schools hate per-seat pricing — it creates admin burden |
| Annual price increases without warning | Founding School = 2-year lock, new pricing for renewals only |
| Force annual contracts | Offer monthly for nervous buyers, but incentivise annual (2 months free) |
