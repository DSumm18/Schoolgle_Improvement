## Historical/art direction checks

Fire artwork includes burning timber streets, the Thames, people moving possessions by boat and a largely intact inhabited bridge. Its cathedral has no modern dome or tall spire; no Big Ben, Tower Bridge or Monument appears. Composition and building forms remain artistic interpretation. The Plot image uses a sealed letter, lantern, cloak and medieval Westminster-inspired setting; it is not a measured reconstruction of the palace. Nile intentionally combines Egyptian locations and periods as adventure illustration.

References: [London Museum: Great Fire](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/great-fire-of-london/), [Old St Paul's](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/a-history-of-st-pauls-cathedral/), and the contracts in `apps/learning-worlds/FIRE-HISTORY.md` and `PLOT-ARCHITECTURE.md`.
