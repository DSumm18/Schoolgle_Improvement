# Competitive Analysis — Schoolgle vs The Market

## Positioning Statement

**Schoolgle is NOT a replacement for your MIS (SIMS, Arbor, Bromcom) or finance system (FMS).**

Schoolgle fills the gap between what those systems do and what schools actually need. Your MIS stores data — Schoolgle helps you *use* it. Your finance system tracks spending — Schoolgle helps you plan it. Most importantly, Schoolgle does everything those systems don't: Ofsted readiness, estates compliance, AI support, school improvement, governance, and more.

**Think of it this way:**
- SIMS/Arbor/Bromcom = your school's filing cabinet
- Schoolgle = your school's improvement partner

## What Schoolgle Does NOT Do (Be Honest)

| We don't do | Use instead | Why |
|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Student records / attendance | SIMS, Arbor, Bromcom | Schools need a certified MIS for statutory returns |
| Accounting / purchase orders | FMS, PS Financials, Sage | Proper accounting requires certified software |
| Timetabling | SIMS, Arbor, TimeTabler | Specialist tool with complex algorithms |
| Payroll | LA payroll, outsourced | Payroll is a specialised, regulated function |
| Parent communication app | Arbor, ClassDojo, Parentmail | We have surveys and documents, not a full parent app (yet) |

**Being honest about what we don't do builds trust.** Schools respect a vendor who says "we do this, not that" rather than claiming to do everything badly.
