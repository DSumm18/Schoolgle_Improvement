## Final LinkedIn Post

Some school problems are too specific for a big system, but too important for another spreadsheet.

That is the idea behind Schoolgle Toolbox.

Think of it as a place for focused mini-apps: small tools that solve real operational problems quickly, using the data a school already manages in Schoolgle.

One example is Class Builder.

The problem sounds simple until you actually sit with it:

How do you build next year's classes when you need to balance friendships, working relationships, SEND, EHCP, EAL, pupil premium, gender, current class groups, mixed-age structures and the professional judgement of staff?

It is not just "move everyone up a year".

So we built a mini-app that helps schools collect pupil voice through a simple survey, review the patterns, and create explainable draft class groupings.

Leaders stay in control. Teachers still make the decisions. The app just makes the hidden complexity easier to see.

We have also started using the same information to support seating-plan options, so staff can think practically about peer support, classroom layout and additional needs.

That is the bit I am most interested in with Toolbox:

Not another huge system.

Focused school problems, solved properly.

If there is a small but awkward task in your school that takes more time than it should, I would genuinely love to hear what it is.

There may be a mini-app for that.
