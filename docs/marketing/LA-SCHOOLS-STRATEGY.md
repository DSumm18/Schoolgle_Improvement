# Schoolgle — LA Maintained Schools Strategy

## "School in a Box" — Go-to-Market Plan

### The Opportunity

- ~16,000 LA maintained schools in England
- Minimal central support from local authorities
- Most still using spreadsheets for compliance, HR, estates
- Heads "don't know what they don't know" — no awareness of modern tools
- Tight budgets but massive hidden costs from manual processes
- LA cluster networks = built-in distribution (one meeting → 50 schools)

### Target Customer Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| School type | LA maintained primary (1-2 form entry) |
| Budget | £1-3M delegated |
| Staff | 15-40 staff |
| Current tools | Google Drive/OneDrive, Excel spreadsheets, paper files |
| Pain points | Ofsted anxiety, compliance gaps, no systems, time poverty |
| Decision maker | Head teacher (can sign off under £5K without governors) |
| Influencer | School business manager, LA school improvement officer |

## Sales Process for LA Clusters

### Step 1: Get the Introduction
- Contact LA school improvement officer or head of education
- Ask for a 15-minute slot at the next heads' briefing / cluster meeting
- Position as "helping your schools with Ofsted readiness and compliance"

### Step 2: Pilot (3-5 Schools, 30 Days Free)
- Hand-pick 3-5 schools with upcoming inspections or known challenges
- White-glove onboarding — help them upload their first documents
- Get them using Ed AI within the first session
- Goal: "wow moment" in under 10 minutes

### Step 3: Heads' Briefing Presentation (15-20 Minutes)
- Use the HTML pitch deck (see `/docs/marketing/pitch-deck/`)
- Live demo of evidence scanning + Ed AI
- Pilot school head gives 2-minute testimonial
- "Founding School" offer — sign up today, £1,995 locked for 2 years

### Step 4: Onboarding at Scale
- Bulk school setup (import from DfE GIAS data)
- Group training webinar (1 hour)
- Ed AI handles ongoing support questions
- Monthly check-in with LA coordinator
