# Demo Script: LA Heads' Briefing (15-20 Minutes)

## Before You Start
- Have a sample school already set up in Schoolgle (use a demo school or pilot school)
- Have 2-3 sample documents ready to upload live
- Test your internet connection and screen sharing
- Have the HTML pitch deck open in browser (full screen: F11)

## The Presentation Flow

### Opening (2 Minutes) — Use Slides 1-2

**Say:** "Thank you for having me. I'm going to show you something in the next 15 minutes that I think will change how you run your school. I'm not going to show you a sales deck — I'm going to show you the actual product, working, with real school data."

**Slide 1:** School in a Box headline — let it land.

**Slide 2:** "Quick show of hands — how many of you track your estates compliance on a spreadsheet? How many could tell me right now if your safeguarding lead's training is up to date? How confident are you that your Ofsted evidence is complete?"

*Pause. Let the discomfort sit for a moment.*

### The Problem (2 Minutes) — Use Slides 3

**Say:** "You're spending thousands on this stuff already — you just don't see it because it's buried in your time and your staff's time."

**Slide 3:** Walk through the hidden costs table. Let them add it up mentally.

### Live Demo (8 Minutes) — Switch to Schoolgle

**Switch from slides to the live platform.**

**Demo 1: Ofsted Readiness (3 mins)**
1. Show the dashboard — readiness score, framework heatmap
2. Upload a document live: "This is a behaviour policy I grabbed from a school website. Watch what happens..."
3. AI tags it to framework areas in real-time
4. Show the gap analysis: "See these red areas? That's what an inspector would probe."

**Demo 2: Estates Compliance (2 mins)**
1. Show the compliance calendar — "Here's everything that's statutory for your building"
2. Show an overdue item: "This legionella flush was due 3 days ago. The caretaker got a reminder. It's now overdue and flagged."
3. Show the helpdesk: "Teacher reports a broken window — logged, tracked, evidenced."

**Demo 3: Ed AI (2 mins)**
1. Type a question: "A parent has made a formal complaint about bullying. What should I do?"
2. Show Ed's structured response with policy references
3. Then: "Ed, create a helpdesk ticket for a broken fence panel near the playground"
4. Show it actually creates the ticket

**Demo 4: Intelligence (1 min)**
1. Show the DfE data view briefly
2. "This is what an inspector sees before they arrive. Now you see it too."

### The Offer (3 Minutes) — Back to Slides 9-12

**Switch back to the pitch deck.**

**Slide 9:** "All of that — 11 modules, AI assistant, everything you just saw — for £1,995 a year. That's under two grand. You can sign off on that without going to governors."

**Slide 10:** "Look at what you're spending now vs what this costs. The maths is obvious."

**Slide 11:** "Getting started takes a week, not a term."

**Slide 12:** "We're offering Founding School pricing to the first 50 schools in [LA name]. That price is locked for 2 years. After that, new schools pay standard pricing."

### Close (2 Minutes)

**Say:** "I'm going to leave you my details. If you want to try it — completely free for 30 days, no credit card — scan the QR code or email me. For those of you who sign up as Founding Schools, I'll personally do your onboarding."

**If a pilot school head is present:** "Can I ask [Name] to say a quick word about their experience?"

*Pilot school testimonial (1-2 mins).*

**Final line:** "Thank you. I'm around for questions — or just email me and we'll get you set up this week."

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We can't afford it" | "You're already spending more than this in hidden costs. £5.46/day. What are you paying your LA SLA?" |
| "We're happy with what we have" | "Great — can you show me your Ofsted readiness right now? Can you tell me if your legionella flushing is up to date?" |
| "We don't have time to set up a new system" | "It takes one week. Connect your Drive, import your staff CSV. The AI does the rest." |
| "We had a good Ofsted" | "Congratulations. When's the next one? Will you still be ready in 2 years?" |
| "Is our data safe?" | "Your data stays in the UK on encrypted servers. Pupil names never leave your browser — we use military-grade anonymisation." |
| "Can we just buy one module?" | "You can — but it costs more. The Founding School deal gives you everything for less than our Ofsted module alone." |
| "We need to discuss with governors" | "It's under £2K — most heads can approve that directly. But happy to present to your governing board too." |

## What to Bring
- [ ] Laptop with Schoolgle demo school loaded
- [ ] Pitch deck HTML file (works offline once loaded)
- [ ] QR code printed on cards (link to sign-up page)
- [ ] 1-page handout with module summary and pricing
- [ ] Business cards
- [ ] Testimonial quote from pilot school (printed or on slide)

## Follow-Up (Within 24 Hours)
1. Email all attendees with:
   - Thank you note
   - Link to sign up for free trial
   - 1-page PDF summary (module list + pricing)
   - Your direct contact details
2. Phone/email any head who showed strong interest
3. Contact the LA coordinator to schedule follow-up support
