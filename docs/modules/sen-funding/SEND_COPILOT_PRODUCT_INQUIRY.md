# SEND Copilot Product Inquiry

**Date:** 2026-05-09  
**Decision:** Proceed to Phase 1 build  
**Product line:** Schoolgle SEND & Inclusion Copilot  

---

## Inquiry Question

Can Schoolgle create a practical SENCO and inclusion-lead product that reduces SEND administration, improves statutory confidence, supports vulnerable/disadvantaged pupil oversight, and gives schools/trusts a clear operational advantage over current SEND register/provision-map tools?

## Short Answer

Yes. The strongest product opportunity is not to copy existing provision-mapping systems. The gap is a SEND and inclusion workflow copilot that connects pupil records, statutory workflows, vulnerable/disadvantaged pupil oversight, meetings, evidence packs, documentation, funding and trust oversight.

The product should be positioned as:

> A simple SEND and inclusion command centre that helps SENCOs and inclusion leaders get the work done: know what is due, run the right meeting, capture the evidence, generate the paperwork, support staff and close the loop.

## Problem

SENCOs are overloaded by:

- fragmented pupil information;
- APDR and EHCP review cycles tracked in spreadsheets;
- annual review paperwork;
- evidence collation;
- chasing parent, pupil and professional views;
- provision and impact records;
- local authority funding/banding evidence;
- meeting notes and actions;
- statutory deadline anxiety;
- inconsistent practice across schools in a trust.
- pressure on inclusion leaders to report impact for SEND, disadvantaged, vulnerable and wellbeing cohorts.

This creates friction, risk and lost time. It also weakens the school's ability to evidence inclusive practice, secure appropriate support and show strong SEND oversight.

## Product Opportunity

The market already contains SEND registers and provision maps. Schoolgle should surpass them by owning the hard operational layer:

1. day-to-day SENCO command centre;
2. pupil one-view;
3. statutory workflow engine;
4. meeting copilot;
5. evidence readiness;
6. documentation automation;
7. funding intelligence;
8. trust-wide SEND oversight.
9. inclusion leadership reporting across SEND, disadvantaged, vulnerable and wellbeing priorities.

## Why Schoolgle Can Win

Schoolgle already has foundations competitors usually lack in one platform:

- existing SEND dashboard/API foundations;
- meeting recorder, templates, scripts, transcripts and minutes;
- document generation;
- evidence/document intelligence;
- actions/tasks;
- school intelligence and pupil assessment analysis;
- cloud source-of-truth model;
- staff/HR/governance/finance/Ofsted modules;
- Ed specialist agent architecture.

The defensible wedge is cross-module SEND intelligence: meetings become evidence, evidence becomes documents, documents become actions, actions update statutory workflows, and workflows support trust/Ofsted/funding visibility.

## Phase 1 Product

Build the first commercially demonstrable product around:

- `SEND Today`: calm daily priority board.
- `Pupil One View`: all SEND information in one place.
- `Annual Review Meeting Copilot`: agenda, attendees, consent, recording, live prompts, minutes, actions.
- `EHCP Request Evidence Pack`: readiness check, missing evidence, generated summary.
- `Arbor API/connected sheet/CSV import`: read-only import into Schoolgle SEND records.
- `Statutory Rule Pack`: APDR, EHCP request and annual review guidance with source references.

The Assistant Headteacher for Inclusion role review confirms the roadmap also needs:

- `Inclusion Leadership`: senior overview of SEND, disadvantaged, vulnerable and wellbeing priorities.
- `Leadership Outputs`: SLT inclusion brief, governor/trust report, annual inclusion development plan and Ofsted inclusion evidence pack.
- `Staff Support`: CPD, coaching and inclusive-practice actions linked to pupil/cohort needs.
- `Resource Planning`: provision cost, funding gap, support staff deployment and accommodation/resource adjustments.
- `SEND Funding Reconciliation`: expected vs received high-needs/top-up funding by pupil, including band/points, payment schedule, backdating, variances and LA query packs.

## Buyer

Start with schools and MATs, not councils.

Best initial buyers:

- overloaded primary SENCOs;
- secondary SENCO teams;
- special/AP/resource-base leaders;
- MAT SEND leads trying to standardise practice across schools;
- trusts employing SEND administrators across hubs.

Councils are a later marketplace for evidence-quality/readiness and school-facing standardisation, because they already use heavier statutory case-management systems.

## Product Safety

The product must not claim to provide legal advice, guarantee local authority outcomes, or replace professional judgement.

Approved language:

- statutory guidance support;
- evidence readiness;
- suggested challenge wording;
- human-approved prompt;
- source-linked guidance;
- SEND admin reduction;
- SENCO enablement.

Avoid:

- legal advice;
- guaranteed EHCP/funding success;
- fully automated LA challenge;
- replacing SENCO judgement;
- changing statutory wording without approval.
