# SEND Copilot Role Coverage: Assistant Headteacher for Inclusion

**Source document:** `C:\Users\dsumm\Downloads\43d2520864.docx`  
**Reviewed:** 2026-05-09  
**Purpose:** Check the advertised Assistant Headteacher role against the Schoolgle SEND Copilot / Inclusion product line so the software covers the real day-to-day job, not just narrow SEND paperwork.

---

## Key Finding

The job description is not just a SENCO administration role. It is an Assistant Headteacher for Inclusion role covering SEND, disadvantaged pupils, vulnerable pupils, emotional wellbeing, teaching and learning quality, staff development, curriculum implementation, behaviour, data, reporting to leaders/trustees, policy, meetings, resources, safeguarding and partnership working.

That means the product should remain simple for SENCO daily use, but the product line should be positioned as:

> `Schoolgle SEND & Inclusion Copilot`: one calm operating layer for SEND, vulnerable pupils, disadvantaged pupils, wellbeing, provision, meetings, evidence, staff support and leadership reporting.

Phase 1 can still be SENCO-first. The final product should include an `Inclusion Leadership` layer for Assistant Heads, SENCOs, DSLs, pastoral leads and trust SEND/inclusion leads.

## Final Product Tweaks

### 1. Rename The Strategic Layer

Keep `SEND Copilot` as the clear school-facing module, but add an `Inclusion Leadership` layer for broader roles.

Recommended navigation:

- `SEND Today`
- `Pupils`
- `Meetings`
- `Evidence Packs`
- `Provision & Funding`
- `Inclusion Leadership`

### 2. Add Inclusion Categories

Pupil one-view should support:

- SEND;
- EHCP;
- SEN Support;
- monitoring;
- disadvantaged / pupil premium;
- vulnerable;
- emotional wellbeing;
- attendance concern;
- behaviour concern;
- looked-after / previously looked-after where permitted;
- EAL/contextual flags where relevant.

These should be controlled by permissions and data minimisation.

### 3. Add Simple Senior Leader Outputs

Generate:

- `SLT Inclusion Brief`;
- `Governor SEND/Inclusion Report`;
- `Trust SEND/Inclusion Dashboard`;
- `Annual Inclusion Development Plan`;
- `Ofsted Inclusion Evidence Pack`;
- `New Staff SEND/Inclusion Briefing Pack`.

### 4. Add Inclusion Meeting Templates

Beyond the SEND statutory meetings, include:

- Inclusion Strategy Review;
- Vulnerable Pupil Review;
- Disadvantaged Pupil Progress Review;
- Emotional Wellbeing Support Review;
- Inclusive Practice Coaching Meeting;
- SEND Governor Visit;
- Support Staff Provision Review;
- Transition / External Partner Review.

### 5. Keep Phase 1 Narrow

Do not overload the first build. Phase 1 should still be:

1. SENCO dashboard;
2. pupil one-view;
3. annual review meeting copilot;
4. EHCP request evidence pack;
5. Arbor API/connected sheet/CSV import;
6. statutory rule pack.

The Assistant Headteacher role coverage should shape the roadmap and navigation, not turn Phase 1 into an unbuildable mega-platform.

### 6. Add Finance Buy-In

The finance team should have a dedicated reason to buy into the product. Add `SEND Funding Reconciliation` so the school can compare:

- what the pupil is funded for;
- what band/points/level has been agreed;
- what should be received and when;
- what has actually been received;
- what is backdated or outstanding;
- what evidence supports a query to the local authority.

This should link SENCO workflows to business manager/CFO workflows and make the product valuable beyond the SEND office.

---

## Product Line After This Job Description

The product line should now be described as:

> `Schoolgle SEND & Inclusion Copilot` helps SENCOs and inclusion leaders manage SEND, vulnerable learners, provision, statutory meetings, evidence, wellbeing support, staff actions, funding and leadership reporting from one simple place.

This better matches the advertised role and creates a bigger market story than narrow SEND administration alone.
