# SEND Admin Copilot Market Research

**Date:** 2026-05-09  
**Purpose:** Understand the school, trust and local authority SEND software market so Schoolgle can position SEND Admin Copilot as a practical SENCO workflow product rather than another passive provision map.

---

## Executive Read

The SEND software market is crowded, but it is not yet solved. Existing tools cluster into three groups:

1. **School SENCO systems**: provision maps, APDR cycles, learning plans, EHCP review reminders, reporting.
2. **Local authority statutory systems**: EHCP case management, portals, plan builders, statutory timelines, SEN2 reporting, panel workflows.
3. **Quality/AI specialist systems**: EHCP QA, annual review QA, evidence scoring, EHCP drafting, document structuring.

Schoolgle should not try to beat every system by copying their entire feature list first. The strongest wedge is:

> **A pupil-linked SEND Meeting and Evidence Copilot that books the meeting, sets the statutory agenda, supports the SENCO live with guidance prompts, captures/transcribes/minutes the meeting, creates actions, files evidence, and generates the annual review/EHCP/HNF paperwork.**

That is the gap between school-facing provision map tools and council-facing EHCP portals. Schools are still left doing the hard meeting, evidence, pushback and documentation work.

## What Councils Use

Councils generally do have systems, but these are not the same as school SENCO tools.

Common council stack patterns:

- **Idox EHC Hub** for EHCP portal/collaboration/reviews.
- **Access Synergy** for broader LA education management including SEN.
- **MRI/Capita One Education** for education services including SEND and transport portals.
- **Liquidlogic/System C** for social care/early help/education integrated case management.
- **Invision360** for EHCP and annual review QA rather than core case management.
- Some councils have local portals branded as “Citizen Portal”, “EHC Hub”, or “EA Connect”.

Council systems are usually:

- Statutory-process centred.
- LA caseworker centred.
- Portal based for parents/professionals/settings.
- Heavy procurement, implementation and integration projects.
- Focused on statutory compliance, SEN2, timeliness and plan production.

Schoolgle should not initially sell “replace your LA EHCP system.” Better marketplace claim:

> **Schoolgle makes school and trust inputs into the SEND statutory process complete, timely, evidenced, minuted and ready for whichever LA portal/case system the council uses.**

That opens two routes:

1. **School/MAT route:** buy Schoolgle to save SENCO time, improve evidence quality, and recover/secure funding.
2. **LA route:** buy Schoolgle as a school-facing evidence and annual-review quality layer to improve submissions into Idox/Synergy/MRI/Liquidlogic.

## Academy Trust / MAT Marketplace

### What Exists

- Provision Map, Insight, Senflow, SEND Matrix and others explicitly target trusts or multi-school visibility.
- Whole Education runs a MAT SEND School Improvement programme with 90+ MATs and 1,000+ schools, but this is improvement/CPD rather than software.
- Evaluate-Ed and MATvista target MAT quality assurance and school improvement, including SEND as an evaluation area, but not operational SENCO workflow.
- Trusts often have central SEND leads but lack a consistent operating system across schools.

### Trust Buying Problems We Can Solve

- Inconsistent APDR and annual review quality between schools.
- No trust-wide visibility of EHCP pupils, annual reviews due, HNF/band gaps, evidence quality or provision cost.
- Central SEND lead cannot see live workflows without asking each SENCO.
- Trust schools work with multiple local authorities, each with different forms, funding bands and portal behaviours.
- SEND administrator roles are appearing across hubs because the workload is now too fragmented for individual SENCOs.

### MAT Product Angle

Create `SEND Admin Copilot Trust`:

- Cross-school SEND dashboard.
- Annual reviews due/overdue.
- EHCP/HNF cases by LA.
- Evidence pack readiness scores.
- Funding gap and provision cost summary.
- Meeting prompt usage and action completion.
- Ofsted inclusion evidence pack.
- LA-specific template library shared across trust schools.
- Central quality assurance of annual review reports before submission.

This is likely more compelling than selling one-school SEND software into every school separately.

## Suggested Pricing Position

School tools are cheap at the low end:

- Blue Hills: GBP 350-650/year.
- Baobab: GBP 395/year.
- Insight: GBP 4.50/pupil up to GBP 2,250.

Council tools are expensive:

- Idox: roughly GBP 40k-56k/year plus implementation for LA EHC Hub.
- Access Synergy: minimum 100 users; SEN module starts around GBP 500/user/year at smaller bands, excluding portals/implementation.
- Invision360 QA: old public G-Cloud bundles roughly GBP 20k-32k/year; real awards such as Staffordshire show GBP 36k over 2 years.

Recommended Schoolgle pricing:

- **SEND Admin Copilot School:** GBP 995-1,495/year per primary/small school.
- **SEND Admin Copilot Secondary/Special:** GBP 1,995-2,995/year.
- **SEND Admin Copilot Trust:** GBP 795-1,295 per school/year with minimum trust package, plus central dashboard.
- **Funding Intelligence Add-on:** GBP 500-1,000 per school/year or included in premium.
- **LA Evidence Quality Pilot:** GBP 15k-35k/year for a council pilot covering school-side annual review/evidence quality dashboard.

Price must be justified by:

- Avoiding/offsetting SEND administrator time.
- Recovering or securing top-up/high-needs funding.
- Reducing annual review admin.
- Improving evidence quality and inspection readiness.
- Better trust consistency.

## Bottom Line

There is a market. It is already spending:

- Hundreds to low thousands per school for SEND/provision mapping.
- Tens of thousands per local authority for EHCP portals/QA.
- Large six/seven-figure contracts for full LA education systems.

The gap is not another provision map. The gap is **SENCO operational intelligence**:

- before the meeting,
- during the meeting,
- after the meeting,
- into evidence,
- into funding,
- into trust and Ofsted inclusion reporting.

Schoolgle’s best route is to become the school/trust layer that makes SEND statutory work executable. The live meeting copilot is the wedge; evidence and funding intelligence are the moat.
