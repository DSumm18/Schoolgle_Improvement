# Rawdon St Peter's Estates Compliance Demo Runbook

## Prepared Instance

- Organisation: Rawdon St Peter's C of E Primary School (the active record with four members).
- 137 statutory/good-practice checks provisioned.
- 2 clearly labelled local demo routines provisioned.
- 136 checks are marked as requiring baseline confirmation; no false completion history was created.
- 1 clearly labelled illustrative Legionella scenario is set to `awaiting_documentation` with a demo contractor.

## Recommended 15-Minute Demo

1. Open the estates dashboard and explain statutory versus good-practice responsibilities.
2. Open **Daily Gate and Perimeter Lock Check** and show the simple daily checklist.
3. Record a completion with notes and a photo; show the next daily due date.
4. Open **Monthly Temperature Monitoring** under Legionella.
5. Show the labelled contractor-attended scenario in `awaiting_documentation`.
6. Click **Record contractor chase** and show the audit change.
7. Click **Paperwork received** only if demonstrating the state transition; explain that this is illustrative, not a real certificate.
8. Add a new temperature reading. Use a safe example (cold 18°C, hot 52°C) to show pass.
9. Use a breach example (cold 21°C or hot 47°C) to show automatic failure and linked ticket creation.
10. Open the generated ticket; show assignment, evidence, comments, status and resolution history.
11. Open **Fixed Wire Testing (EICR)** and show the exact five-year recurrence.
12. Create a school-specific check to demonstrate daily/weekly/monthly/multi-year/ad-hoc flexibility.

## Demo Guardrails

- Every seeded scenario is illustrative and must be described as such.
- Do not say Rawdon is compliant or non-compliant until real certificates and dates are entered.
- The 136 baseline rows should be reviewed with the school after the demo for applicability, last completion date, contractor and evidence.
- Mark equipment-specific checks not applicable only after confirming the school does not have the relevant asset/system.
