# Rawdon St Peter's Estates Compliance Demo Runbook

## Prepared Instance

- Organisation: Rawdon St Peter's C of E Primary School (the active record with four members).
- 137 statutory/good-practice checks provisioned.
- 3 clearly labelled local demo routines provisioned.
- 136 checks are marked as requiring baseline confirmation; no false statutory completion history was created.
- 1 clearly labelled illustrative Legionella scenario is set to `awaiting_documentation` with a demo contractor.
- **DEMO - Monthly Site Security Walkround** has one completed demo record with notes, image evidence, and a next due date of 30 August 2026.

## Recommended 15-Minute Demo

1. Open the estates dashboard and explain statutory versus good-practice responsibilities.
2. Open **Security** and select **DEMO - Monthly Site Security Walkround**.
3. Show that the record is private, non-statutory, monthly, and clearly labelled as illustrative.
4. Show **Fully Completed**, the saved notes and image, and the next due date of **30 August 2026**.
5. Return to the Security list and show the completed status and last inspection date.
6. Reopen the check and select the evidence filename to open the stored image.
7. Open **Monthly Temperature Monitoring** under Legionella.
8. Show the labelled contractor-attended scenario in `awaiting_documentation`.
9. Explain that cold readings below 20 degrees C and hot readings at least 50 degrees C pass. Unsafe readings automatically fail and create a linked ticket, but demonstrate invented failure data only in Aurora UAT.
10. Open a linked demo ticket; show assignment, evidence, comments, status and resolution history.
11. Open **Fixed Wire Testing (EICR)** and show the exact five-year recurrence.
12. Show the custom-check wizard to demonstrate daily, weekly, monthly, multi-year, and ad-hoc flexibility.

## Demo Guardrails

- Every seeded scenario is illustrative and must be described as such.
- Do not say Rawdon is compliant or non-compliant until real certificates and dates are entered.
- Rawdon is protected from local/UAT access. Use the production app for authorised Rawdon walkthroughs rather than bypassing the safety guard.
- The 136 baseline rows should be reviewed with the school after the demo for applicability, last completion date, contractor and evidence.
- Mark equipment-specific checks not applicable only after confirming the school does not have the relevant asset/system.
