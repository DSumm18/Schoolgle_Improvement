## The Problem

"Every year, UK schools spend millions on compliance work they don't actually need.

Site managers and business managers are overwhelmed by complex regulations. Contractors provide recommendations mixed with statutory requirements, leaving schools uncertain about what's legally required versus what's 'nice to have.'

Budgets are tight. Every pound spent on unnecessary work is a pound taken from the classroom."

---

## Our Solution

**Schoolgle Estates Compliance** is an AI-powered compliance management system that:

1. **Knows the difference** between statutory requirements and good practice
2. **Validates contractor recommendations** against actual legislation
3. **Guids non-experts** through complex compliance tasks
4. **Plans for the future** with budget forecasting and estates strategy

---

## Key Features

### 1. Ed: Your Compliance Expert

"Ed is an AI assistant who knows UK school regulations inside out. Unlike other systems that just store records, Ed guides you through tasks, validates inputs, and explains *why* things are required."

**What Ed does:**
- Guides staff through compliance tasks step-by-step
- Validates temperature readings against regulatory limits
- Checks authorization before allowing task completion
- Distinguishes statutory from good practice recommendations
- Explains requirements in plain English

### 2. Statutory vs Good Practice

"Most systems lump everything together. Schools can't tell what they MUST do versus what they SHOULD do.

We separate everything into clear categories:

- **Statutory Required** - You MUST do this (it's the law)
- **Good Practice** - You SHOULD do this (recommended)
- **Contractor Recommendation** - Consider this (but it's optional)

Never be misled by contractor upselling again."

### 3. Mobile-First Compliance

"Your site team works on the move. So does our app.

Native iOS and Android apps with offline mode mean compliance tasks can be completed anywhere - even in basements with no signal. Photos, QR scanning, and voice input make it fast and easy."

### 4. Contractor Validation

"Get a contractor report? Upload it and Ed will:
- Extract temperature readings and findings
- Cross-check against regulatory limits
- Flag statutory vs good practice items
- Highlight anything that seems unusual

You'll know exactly what needs action and what can wait."

### 5. Unified Helpdesk

"One email address for everything.

`estates@yourschool.schoolgle.co.uk`

Staff report issues via email. The system automatically categorizes, prioritizes, and assigns. Whether it's a stuck window, a leaky tap, or a broken fire extinguisher - it's all tracked in one place."

### 6. Budget Planning & Estates Strategy

"Compliance findings feed directly into your estates strategy.

See what's coming up in 1, 3, and 5 years. Plan capital expenditures. Budget for replacements.

Turn day-to-day compliance into long-term strategic planning."

## How It Works

### For the Site Manager

"Monday morning arrives. Instead of wondering what needs doing, you open the app:

1. See your tasks for the week (5 outlets to flush, 2 equipment checks)
2. Tap 'Classroom 1 Sink' - Ed guides you through the weekly flush
3. Record the temperature - Ed validates it's within limits
4. Complete the task - automatically logged, certificate stored

30 minutes later, you're done. No uncertainty. No paperwork."

### For the Business Manager

"Monthly inspection due. Instead of phone calls and paperwork:

1. Open the dashboard, see the inspection is due
2. Select your preferred contractor from the register
3. Choose an appointment time (7-8am before school)
4. System sends calendar invite and secure upload link

Contractor completes the work, uploads the report. Ed processes it:
- Extracts findings
- Flags 1 statutory item (high temperature - needs action)
- Flags 2 good practice items (nice to have, can wait)
- Creates a helpdesk ticket for the statutory item

You review, approve, and the certificate is stored. Total time: 10 minutes."

### For the Trust/Director

"Governors need a report? One click generates:
- RAG status for all compliance areas
- Outstanding statutory items (red)
- Planned expenditures for next 3 years
- Certificate expiry status

No compilation. No chasing. Just click, export, present."
