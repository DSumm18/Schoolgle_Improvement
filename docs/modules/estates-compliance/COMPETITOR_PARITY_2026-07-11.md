# Estates Compliance Competitor Parity — 11 July 2026

## Executive Finding

Schoolgle now covers the core compliance workflow needed for the Rawdon St Peter's demonstration: recurring checks, exact multi-year intervals, mobile-responsive completion, structured evidence, notes, assignments, automatic failed-check tickets, contractor/documentation follow-up, audit history, custom checks and governor reporting.

It does not yet match every feature marketed across the full Civica Education Operations and Every Compliance suites. The remaining gaps are mainly enterprise breadth rather than the day-to-day check workflow.

## Ownership and Current Market Position

- Parago was acquired by Civica in 2020 and is currently sold as **Civica Education Operations (formerly Parago)**.
- Every Compliance is currently sold as **Every Compliance by IRIS**.
- Civica states that its product connects assets, estates, contracts, incidents and compliance, with mobile access, automated reminders and audit trails.
- IRIS markets trust-wide RAG dashboards, mobile activity recording, trends, condition management, projects, incidents, risk, policies, training and e-learning.

## Compliance Library Reconciliation

- The previous internal claims of 156 or 200+ live checks were not supported by the executable product.
- Five check packs existed but were disconnected: COSHH, food safety, transport, safeguarding and seasonal maintenance.
- These packs are now connected, producing **137 executable statutory and good-practice checks across 18 user-facing domains**.
- A separate 100-row database catalogue exists across 24 categories, but it uses an older/inconsistent schema and classifications. It should not be presented as an additional 100 verified duties until legally and technically reconciled.
- Exact scheduling now supports: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, six-monthly, termly, annual, two-, three-, five- and ten-yearly, as-needed and ad-hoc.

## Regulatory Product Principles

- Schoolgle must distinguish statutory duties, good practice and school-created routines.
- Frequency must come from the specific regulation, approved guidance, competent-person scheme or local risk assessment; the software must not imply one universal period where the duty is risk-based.
- “Completed awaiting documentation” means the inspection/work occurred, but the certificate/report remains outstanding. It must not count as fully evidenced compliance.
- Contractor appointment does not transfer the school's/employer's underlying health and safety responsibility.
- Baseline dates for a new customer must be confirmed from real certificates and records rather than invented during onboarding.

## Priority Roadmap After Rawdon Demo

1. Generalise Parago-style smart fields and thresholds beyond Legionella temperatures.
2. Add trust-wide standards, exceptions and cross-school drill-down.
3. Add an offline-capable mobile/PWA daily task queue.
4. Add a contractor portal for attendance, upload and correction without full staff access.
5. Consolidate capital projects, milestones, quality assurance and e-signatures.
6. Legally reconcile the older 100-row master catalogue with the 137 executable checks and remove unsupported count claims.
