## User Personas

### Primary Users

#### 1. School Site Manager / Caretaker

**Profile:**
- Often sole person responsible for estates
- May lack formal qualifications
- Juggles multiple priorities
- "Jack of all trades, master of none"
- Needs guidance and support

**Pain Points:**
- Don't know what they don't know
- Unclear about regulatory requirements
- Fear of getting it wrong
- Overwhelmed by compliance burden
- Limited time/resources

**Goals:**
- Stay compliant
- Avoid issues
- Get clear guidance
- Know what to do when

**Ed's Role:**
- Expert partner
- Validates knowledge
- Prevents mistakes
- Provides confidence
- Bridges knowledge gap

#### 2. School Business Manager

**Profile:**
- Oversees operations including estates
- Needs oversight and reporting
- May delegate to site manager
- Requires compliance assurance

**Pain Points:**
- Can't be expert in everything
- Needs confidence in compliance
- Requires clear reporting
- Audit trail necessity

**Goals:**
- Assurance of compliance
- Clear oversight
- Easy reporting
- Risk identification

**Ed's Role:**
- Compliance dashboard
- Exception alerts
- Expert explanations
- Audit support

#### 3. Trust Estates Manager

**Profile:**
- Manages estates across multiple schools
- Needs centralized oversight
- Standardization requirement
- Delegates to local site teams

**Pain Points:**
- Inconsistent practices across schools
- Limited visibility
- Reporting burden
- Knowledge sharing

**Goals:**
- Standardized processes
- Central oversight
- Easy reporting
- Knowledge base

**Ed's Role:**
- Consistency across schools
- Centralized insights
- Expert validation
- Knowledge sharing
