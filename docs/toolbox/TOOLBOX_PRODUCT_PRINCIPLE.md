# Toolbox mini-app store

Toolbox is the home for small, high-value Schoolgle mini apps and curated resources that do not need a full product module of their own.

## Product principle

When customers suggest niche workflow ideas, we can build them as focused mini apps in Toolbox rather than cluttering the main module navigation. This gives subscribers a growing shelf of practical tools they can use when needed, while keeping major areas such as Ofsted Readiness, Compliance, HR, Estates and Finance clean.

## Catalogue model

Toolbox should present items like an app store:

- **Schoolgle mini apps**: subscriber-facing tools built into the platform, such as Class Builder.
- **Curated resources**: useful external school tools and templates from trusted sources.
- **Customer ideas**: planned or beta concepts that can be promoted when ready.

Each item should have a name, short description, type/category, status, tags, an icon/logo treatment, and a launch/open action.

## Navigation rule

Only mini apps and lightweight utilities live in Toolbox. Full operational modules remain in their domain areas.
