### Key Principle

Under Regulation 12 of the School Information (England) Regulations 2008 (as amended by the 2012 Regulations):

- Where a school website exists, governing bodies **must** publish Schedule 4 information on it
- Information must be updated **as soon as reasonably practicable** after any change, and **at least annually**
- Schools without websites must publish on an alternative website and inform parents of the address
- Paper copies must be provided **free of charge** on request

## 4. SEND Information Report (Detailed)

### Legal Basis

- **Children and Families Act 2014, s.69**
- **Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, Schedule 1**
- **SEND Code of Practice** (paras 6.79-6.82)

### Update Requirements

- Updated **annually**
- In-year changes updated **as soon as possible**
- Must be **easily accessible** and in **clear, straightforward language**

### Schedule 1 - Required Content (13 items)

1. **Kinds of SEN provision** - The kinds of special educational needs for which provision is made at the school

2. **Identification and assessment policies** - Policies for identifying and assessing pupils with SEN (mainstream and maintained nursery schools only)

3. **Provision policies** including:
   - (a) How the school evaluates the effectiveness of provision
   - (b) Arrangements for assessing and reviewing progress
   - (c) The school's approach to teaching pupils with SEN
   - (d) How the school adapts the curriculum and learning environment
   - (e) Additional learning support available
   - (f) How SEN pupils are included in activities alongside non-SEN peers
   - (g) Support for emotional, mental, and social development

4. **SENCO name and contact details** (mainstream and maintained nursery schools)

5. **Staff expertise and training** - Information about staff expertise/training for SEN and how specialist expertise will be secured

6. **Equipment and facilities** - How equipment and facilities to support SEN children will be secured

7. **Parent consultation** - Arrangements for consulting parents of SEN children about, and involving them in, their child's education

8. **Young person consultation** - Arrangements for consulting SEN young people about, and involving them in, their education

9. **Complaints procedure** - Governing body arrangements for handling SEN provision complaints

10. **External bodies engagement** - How the school works with health services, social care, local authority support services, and voluntary organisations

11. **Parent support services** - Contact details for parent support services (including s.32 arrangements under CFA 2014)

12. **Transition arrangements** - Support for pupils transferring between phases of education or preparing for adulthood and independent living

13. **Local offer link** - Where the local authority's local offer is published

### Additional Requirements (Equality Act 2010)

- Disabled pupil admission arrangements
- Steps to prevent less favourable treatment of disabled pupils
- Facilities to assist disabled pupils
- **Accessibility plan** addressing:
  - Increasing disabled pupil participation in the curriculum
  - Improving the physical environment
  - Improving accessibility of information

## 5. Pupil Premium Strategy Statement (Detailed)

### Legal Basis

- School Information (England) Regulations 2008 (maintained schools)
- Funding agreement conditions of grant (academies)
- DfE "Using pupil premium" guidance (March 2025)

### Publication Requirements

| Aspect               | Requirement                                                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Deadline**         | By **31 December** each year                                      |
| **Template**         | Must use the **DfE template** (updated annually)                  |
| **Exemption**        | Schools with **5 or fewer** eligible pupils exempt                |
| **MATs**             | Separate statement required for **each academy**                  |
| **Planning horizon** | DfE recommends **3-year** planning cycle                          |
| **Annual review**    | Must be reviewed and republished annually even if 3-year strategy |

### Required Content (per DfE template)

1. **School overview** - name, pupils on roll, proportion eligible, pupil premium allocation
2. **Pupil premium funding amount** for current year
3. **Strategy aims and priorities** - what the school is trying to achieve
4. **Intended spending** breakdown for current year, aligned to EEF tiered model:
   - **Teaching** (e.g. CPD, recruitment/retention)
   - **Targeted academic support** (e.g. tutoring, intervention)
   - **Wider strategies** (e.g. attendance, behaviour, enrichment)
5. **Previous year review** - impact of spending and outcomes achieved
6. **Externally provided programmes** - any purchased programmes and evidence base
7. **Carried forward funding** accounting (if applicable)

### EEF Tiered Model (Referenced in Conditions of Grant)

Academies must spend PP grant on evidence-informed activities aligned to the EEF "menu of approaches":

- High-quality teaching
- Targeted academic support
- Wider strategies addressing non-academic barriers

## 6. Governance Information (Detailed)

### Maintained Schools

**Must publish:**

- Governing body structure and committees
- How governing body constitution is complied with

**Should publish:**

- Names of each governor
- Date of appointment
- Term of office
- Date of stepping down (if applicable)
- Who appointed them (LA, parent election, co-option, etc.)
- Meeting attendance records
- Business, financial, and pecuniary interests
- Associate member details and voting rights
- Board diversity data

_Note: Governors may opt out of sharing personal information under certain circumstances._

**Legislation:** Constitution of Governing Bodies of Maintained Schools guidance

### Academy Trusts

**Must publish (Academy Trust Handbook paras 1.49-1.51):**

- Trust structure and remit of members, board, committees, local governing bodies
- Chair names for each body
- Names of trust members and trustees
- Register of business/financial interests for:
  - Members
  - Trustees
  - Local governors
  - Accounting officer
  - _(Including nil returns)_
- Trustee attendance records at board and committee meetings (last academic year)
- Local governor attendance records (last academic year)
- Memorandum of association
- Articles of association
- Funding agreement and supplemental agreements
- Up-to-date governance arrangements

**Must make available on request:**

- Meeting agendas (trustees, committees, LGBs)
- Approved minutes
- Reports/documents considered at meetings
- _(May exclude named staff/pupil data and confidential matters)_

**Must notify DfE within 14 days** of governance changes via GIAS (Get Information About Schools)

**GIAS records required:**

- Members
- Chair of trustees
- All other trustees
- Accounting officer
- Chief financial officer (with direct contact details)

## 9. Safeguarding / Child Protection (Detailed)

### Legal Basis

- **Keeping Children Safe in Education** (KCSIE) - updated annually (currently 2025 edition, in force from September 2025)
- **Working Together to Safeguard Children**
- **Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014** (for academies)

### Website Publication Requirements

| Item                                     | Detail                                                             | Frequency         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| **Child protection/safeguarding policy** | Must be published on website                                       | At least annually |
| **Policy content**                       | Must describe procedures aligned with government guidance          |                   |
| **Local arrangements**                   | Reference to locally agreed multi-agency safeguarding arrangements |                   |
| **Designated Safeguarding Lead**         | Name and contact details should be easily findable                 |                   |

### Policy Must Address (per KCSIE)

- How the school identifies children who may need early help or are at risk
- Procedures for staff to raise concerns
- How referrals to children's social care are managed
- The role of the designated safeguarding lead (DSL)
- Record-keeping procedures
- Allegations against staff procedures
- Safer recruitment procedures
- Online safety
- Children missing education
- Child-on-child abuse
- Children with special educational needs and disabilities
- Mental health

### Ofsted Safeguarding Check

- Safeguarding is now evaluated as **met/not met** (binary, since November 2025)
- Inspectors check the **single central record** on day 1
- They review casework and decision-making
- They go **beyond document checks** to evaluate safeguarding culture
- School website safeguarding policy is reviewed pre-inspection

## 10. Admissions Information (Detailed)

### Key Deadlines

| Deadline        | Requirement                                           |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **15 March**    | Normal-round admission arrangements must be published |
| **28 February** | Appeals timetable must be published                   |
| **31 August**   | In-year admissions procedures must be published       |

### Must Include (Foundation & Voluntary-Aided / Academies)

- Application process
- Published Admission Number (PAN)
- How places are allocated
- Oversubscription criteria
- Selection criteria (if selective)
- Deferral/delayed entry processes
- Sixth form external applicant numbers (if applicable)
- Appeals process with specific deadlines:
  - 20 school days to lodge appeal
  - 10 days' notice of hearing
  - 5 days for decision letter

### Community & Voluntary-Controlled Schools

- Must link to the **local authority's website** for admission arrangements
- Must be updated **not later than 6 weeks** before the preference expression deadline

## 14. Accessibility Requirements

### Legal Basis

- **Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018** (SI 2018/952)
- Implements EU Directive 2016/2102

### Standard Required

- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** compliance

### Schools: Partial Exemption

Schools (primary and secondary) are **partially exempt** - they must still:

- Make accessible all content people need to **use school services** (e.g. meal preference forms, admissions forms, parent communication)
- Publish an **accessibility statement**
- Fix accessibility barriers where possible

### Accessibility Statement Must Include

- How accessible the website is
- Contact details for raising accessibility issues
- Details of content exempted from requirements
- Any "disproportionate burden" claims with justification
- Plans for future accessibility improvements

### Practical Requirements for School Websites

- Screen-reader friendly content
- HTML format preferred over PDFs (especially for PE & Sport Premium reporting)
- Clear navigation and logical grouping
- Information findable from homepage or "Key Information" section
- Paper copies available free on request

## 15. Ofsted Pre-Inspection Website Checks

Source: [School inspection operating guide for inspectors (November 2025)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/school-inspection-toolkit-operating-guide-and-information/school-inspection-operating-guide-for-inspectors-for-use-from-november-2025)

### What Inspectors Review Before Visiting

**Pre-inspection information gathering:**

1. **School/trust website** - specifically checking:
   - Governance arrangements
   - Academy trust delegation schemes
   - Interim executive board (IEB) status
   - General school context and ethos

2. **Inspection Data Summary Report (IDSR)** - performance data

3. **Previous inspection reports** and section 48 reports (religious character)

4. **Complaints made to Ofsted** about the school

5. **Warning notices** from "Find information about a provider"

6. **Ofsted Parent View** survey responses

7. **Press/online information** about the school

8. **Local authority children's services** inspection reports

9. **Local area SEND** inspection findings

10. **DfE, LA, and police** publications about the school

### What Inspectors Look For on the Website

While the operating guide does not prescribe a formal website compliance audit, inspectors in practice check for:

| Check                                    | Why                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Safeguarding/child protection policy** | Core safeguarding assessment                 |
| **Governance structure**                 | Understanding leadership and accountability  |
| **Pupil premium strategy**               | Assessing provision for disadvantaged pupils |
| **SEND Information Report**              | Understanding SEND provision                 |
| **Curriculum information**               | Pre-inspection curriculum understanding      |
| **Performance data**                     | Cross-referencing with IDSR                  |
| **Admission arrangements**               | Understanding school intake                  |
| **Behaviour policy**                     | Pre-visit context                            |

### Planning Call (Day Before Inspection)

Inspectors discuss:

- School context and demographics
- Leaders' priorities and successes
- Inclusion approach and disadvantaged pupil support
- Inspection timetable planning
- Request for specific documents (listed in "inspection information for state-funded schools")

### Day 1 Priorities

- Single Central Record review "as soon as possible"
- Meeting with Designated Safeguarding Lead
- Safeguarding casework sampling

### Key Point for Compliance Checker

Inspectors do NOT run a formal website compliance audit, but they DO review the website as part of pre-inspection preparation. **Missing or outdated statutory information is a red flag** that may prompt deeper scrutiny of governance and leadership.

## 18. Data Model for Compliance Checker

### Suggested Schema

```typescript
interface WebsiteRequirement {
  id: string; // e.g. "contact_details"
  category: RequirementCategory; // grouping
  title: string; // human-readable name
  description: string; // what must be published
  detail_items: string[]; // specific sub-items to check

  // Legal basis
  legislation: LegislationRef[]; // primary legislation
  schedule_4_para?: number; // Schedule 4 paragraph (maintained)
  ath_para?: string; // Academy Trust Handbook paragraph

  // Applicability
  applies_to: SchoolType[]; // maintained, academy, free_school, mat
  phase?: SchoolPhase[]; // primary, secondary, all_through, 16_19
  conditional?: string; // e.g. "if receiving pupil premium"

  // Timing
  update_frequency: UpdateFrequency; // annually, as_needed, by_deadline
  deadline?: string; // e.g. "31 December"

  // Compliance checking
  mandatory: boolean; // must vs should
  ofsted_checked: boolean; // known to be reviewed by inspectors
  severity: "critical" | "high" | "medium" | "low";

  // Detection hints for automated scanning
  keywords: string[]; // terms to search for on website
  expected_page_titles: string[]; // likely page names
  document_formats: string[]; // pdf, html, etc.
}

type RequirementCategory =
  | "contact"
  | "admissions"
  | "curriculum"
  | "performance_data"
  | "send"
  | "pupil_premium"
  | "pe_sport_premium"

  | "policies" // behaviour, complaints, charging, safeguarding, RSE, uniform

type SchoolType =

  | "maintained_vc"
  | "academy"
  | "free_school"
  | "mat"
  | "studio_school"
  | "utc";

type SchoolPhase =
  | "primary"
  | "secondary"
  | "all_through"
  | "special"
  | "nursery"
  | "sixth_form"
  | "16_19";

type UpdateFrequency =
  | "as_needed"
  | "annually"
  | "by_deadline"
  | "every_4_years"
  | "when_published"
  | "termly";

interface LegislationRef {
  name: string; // e.g. "Education Act 1996"
```

### Severity Classification

| Severity     | Criteria                                          | Examples                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Critical** | Legal breach; Ofsted safeguarding concern         | Safeguarding policy missing; SCR issues                                   |
| **High**     | Statutory "must publish" missing; deadline passed | Pupil premium not published by 31 Dec; admissions not published by 15 Mar |
| **Medium**   | Statutory "must" but less time-sensitive          | Curriculum not updated for current year; governance details incomplete    |
| **Low**      | "Should publish" items; best practice             | Ethos statement missing; opening hours not shown                          |

### Automated Detection Approach

For each requirement, the checker should:

1. **Crawl** the school website starting from homepage
2. **Identify** key information pages (look for: "Key Information", "Policies", "About Us", "Statutory Information", "Governance", "SEND", "Pupil Premium")
3. **Search** for keywords and document links matching each requirement
4. **Check currency** - look for dates, academic year references, version numbers
5. **Flag** missing items, outdated items, or items with no detectable date
6. **Score** compliance as a percentage with breakdown by category
7. **Generate** actionable report with specific remediation steps
