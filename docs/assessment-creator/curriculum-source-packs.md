# Assessment Creator Curriculum Source Packs

## Purpose

Assessment Creator needs curriculum source packs so it can generate papers against what a school says it has taught.

The source pack is not a copied commercial scheme. It is Schoolgle's structured representation of:

- the school's taught objectives
- the timing of those objectives
- the assessment purpose
- the school's permitted source references
- national curriculum mapping

## Legal And Product Boundary

Do not use this feature to evade a provider's licensing terms.

Rules:

- Do not ingest proprietary commercial scheme content unless the school has the right to provide it and the licence allows this use.
- Do not reproduce proprietary questions, worksheets, lesson slides, schemes, images, wording or assessment packs.
- Do not market an output as aligned to any named commercial scheme unless there is a formal licence or clear permission.
- Do allow a school to say: "Our Year 5 Spring 1 maths unit covered fractions, decimals and percentages in this sequence."
- Do map school-provided coverage to the public National Curriculum.
- Do generate original assessment questions from Schoolgle's own question rules, misconception tags and mark schemes.

Safe wording:

> Aligned to your school's taught curriculum.

Avoid wording:

> Named-scheme paper generator.
> Commercial-scheme style assessment.
> Officially aligned to a named commercial scheme.

## Sample Pack Strategy

For development and demos, create neutral sample source packs:

- `schoolgle-primary-maths-sample`
- `schoolgle-primary-reading-sample`
- `schoolgle-primary-spag-sample`
- `schoolgle-primary-science-sample`

Each pack should include:

- subject
- year group
- half term
- topic
- public National Curriculum objectives
- school-taught objective phrasing written by Schoolgle
- retrieval links to prior year groups
- misconception tags
- assessment mode suitability

## Common UK Source Types To Support As School Inputs

Schools may use or reference a mixture of:

- commercial maths schemes
- commercial assessment banks
- commercial curriculum platforms
- trust-created curriculum documents
- local authority resources
- public curriculum frameworks
- public lesson resources where reuse is permitted
- bespoke trust curriculum documents

These are integration/source labels, not content sources for copying.

## What We Need From A School

Minimum useful input:

- subject
- year group
- term or half term
- topics taught
- objectives covered
- known concerns or retrieval priorities

Better input:

- the school’s chosen curriculum source or internal map
- school medium-term plan
- sequence of lessons or blocks
- teacher notes on common gaps
- previous assessment outcomes
- any internal progression document

Best input:

- anonymised completed papers
- mark scheme or moderation notes
- class-level outcomes
- curriculum map
- school-approved vocabulary and methods

## First Sample Pack

Build first:

```text
Subject: Maths
Year group: Year 5
Term: Spring 1
Topic: Fractions, decimals and percentages
Assessment modes: Quick Check, Unit Check, Retention Check
Retrieval links: Year 3 fractions, Year 4 decimals, times tables
Statutory pressure: Low for Pilot 1
```

This gives enough structure to test the app without relying on proprietary content.

## Sources Checked

- National Curriculum in England: mathematics programmes of study, GOV.UK.
- Public curriculum and assessment provider pages, checked only to understand licensing risk and common school workflows.
- Public school curriculum pages showing that UK primary schools commonly blend school-created plans, public frameworks and commercial resources.
- Commercial provider copyright notices, confirming proprietary content must not be copied or reused outside licence.
