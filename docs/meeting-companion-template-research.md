# Meeting Companion Template Research

Research date: 27 April 2026  
Scope: England-focused school and academy trust meeting templates. Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland should use separate packs because legal and governance frameworks differ.

## Product Decision

Meeting Companion should become a cross-module app, not just an HR app. Each template should show its basis in the product, so users can see whether a meeting is statutory, policy-required, or good practice.

Recommended template metadata:

- `basis_type`: `statutory`, `regulatory`, `statutory_guidance`, `policy_required`, `good_practice`
- `basis_summary`: short human-readable explanation shown in the app
- `source_urls`: official source links
- `source_reviewed_at`: date sources were last reviewed
- `jurisdiction`: initially `England`
- `required_attendees`: expected roles or optional roles
- `evidence_pack`: documents the user should upload or review before the meeting
- `decision_items`: decisions that must be captured in minutes
- `follow_up_actions`: likely actions created from the template
- `retention_note`: high-level data protection note for recordings/transcripts/minutes

## Cross-Template Compliance Prompts

- Confirm purpose, meeting type, attendees, role, and authority to chair.
- Record declarations/conflicts where decision-making is involved.
- Confirm evidence pack received and policy/source version used.
- Ask whether reasonable adjustments are needed.
- Capture decisions, rationale, action owner, deadline, and review date.
- For HR formal meetings, confirm right to be accompanied where applicable.
- For safeguarding, SEND, health, HR and finance matters, apply data minimisation.
- Confirm minutes/outcome letter, appeal/review route, and next steps.

## App Implementation Notes

1. Extend `meeting_templates` to store basis/source metadata.
2. Add a visible "Why this template exists" panel to template cards and the live meeting screen.
3. Add source chips in the template selector, for example: `ACAS`, `DfE`, `KCSIE`, `HSE`, `SFVS`.
4. Keep official source URLs available in generated minutes as an appendix or metadata block.
5. Add a "source last reviewed" timestamp and a future admin review workflow.
6. Seed global templates from versioned TypeScript/JSON, not one-off SQL only.
7. Add local-authority/school-policy override fields because official guidance is not enough for every school.
8. Treat recording retention separately from template logic: recordings should be transient unless the organisation explicitly opts in to retain them.

## Immediate Template Pack Recommendation

Build the first template pack in this order:

1. HR formal and absence templates because they are immediately usable and high-risk.
2. Safeguarding/SEND templates because they need strong source labelling and data protection warnings.
3. Governance templates because board minutes benefit from structured decision capture.
4. Estates templates because contractor/project meetings are frequent and operationally useful.
5. Finance templates because they overlap with governance approvals and audit trail.
