## Per-Module Competitor Landscape

### Safeguarding: CPOMS (20,000+ schools, ~£530/yr primary)

- Schoolgle advantage: Cross-module intelligence (attendance + behaviour + SEND correlation), included in platform price

### Behaviour: Class Charts (Tes), Bromcom built-in

- Schoolgle advantage: AI pattern detection, consequence ladder, exclusion tracking, Bradford Factor integration

### SEND: Provision Map (Tes/EduKey, from £795/yr)

- Schoolgle advantage: EHCP lifecycle, LA funding reconciliation, tribunal evidence bundles, EEF matching

### Finance: IRIS Financials, Access FMS

- Schoolgle advantage: No finance middleware exists — Schoolgle fills this gap. CFR mapping, budget confidence scoring, supplier intelligence

### HR: Every HR (IRIS), CIPHR

- Schoolgle advantage: Staff Connectors (statutory role tracking), cover management, cross-module HR

### Estates: Parago/Civica, Smartlog (2,500+ schools)

- Schoolgle advantage: 200+ statutory checks, AI findings classification, interactive floor plans, energy dashboard

### Governance: GovernorHub (£450/yr, The Key Group)

- Schoolgle advantage: Integrated with all school data, meeting companion, auto-generated governor reports

### Assessment: Sonar Tracker (Juniper), FFT Aspire

- Schoolgle advantage: Zero-knowledge pupil analysis, AI gap detection, EEF intervention matching

### Communications: ParentMail (6,000+ schools)

- Schoolgle advantage: Document production engine (38 templates), emergency broadcasts, Ed AI drafting

### Energy: Energy Sparks (charity/free)

- Schoolgle advantage: Integrated with estates, AI meter reading extraction, governor reports, carbon reporting

## Strategic Position

Schoolgle doesn't need to replace the MIS — it reads from it (Wonde/Groupcall) and adds intelligence. Target the SIMS exodus (thousands switching now) and school business managers who feel the pain of 10+ systems most acutely.
