## 📋 Pre-Implementation Checklist

Before you start using Schoolgle, please complete the following:

### For the Headteacher / Data Controller

- [ ] Read the Privacy Policy
- [ ] Sign the Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- [ ] Confirm whether the subscribed products require pupil data and which fields are necessary
- [ ] Designate an account administrator
- [ ] Inform staff about the new system

### For the IT Lead / DPO

- [ ] Review the Security Overview
- [ ] Add Schoolgle to your data asset register
- [ ] Update your privacy notice (template below)
- [ ] Complete Data Protection Impact Assessment (if required)
- [ ] Configure user roles appropriately

### For the Business Manager

- [ ] Review pricing and terms
- [ ] Set up subscription
- [ ] Add to software register

Add this to your school's existing privacy notice:

```
SCHOOL IMPROVEMENT SOFTWARE (SCHOOLGLE)

We use Schoolgle, a school improvement platform, to help us prepare for
inspections and track our development priorities.

What data is processed:
• Staff names and email addresses
• Job roles and responsibilities  
• Professional observations and feedback

This may include pupil or staff data where it is required by the subscribed product. For example, Class Builder may use a named pupil roll, class/year group, relevant pupil characteristics and pupil preferences to help staff create explainable draft class groups.

More information:
See Schoolgle's privacy policy at schoolgle.co.uk/privacy
```

## 📝 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) Screening

**Do you need a full DPIA?**

Answer these questions:

| Question | Yes | No |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Does the subscribed product process pupil data? | Complete product schedule/DPIA screening and minimise fields | ✅ Continue |
| Does it involve new technology? | Consider DPIA | ✅ Low risk |
| Does it involve profiling or automated decisions about individuals? | Consider DPIA | ✅ No |
| Does it process special category data (health, religion, etc.)? | Consider DPIA | ✅ No |
| Is data combined from multiple sources? | Consider DPIA | ✅ Minimal |

**If all answers are in the "No" column:** A full DPIA is likely not required, but you should document this screening decision.

**If any answers are "Yes" or "Consider DPIA":** Contact us at dpo@schoolgle.co.uk for support.

## 🚫 What NOT to Upload

**DO NOT upload documents containing unnecessary data outside the product scope:**
- ❌ Pupil names except where required by Class Builder, pupil passes, consent management or another agreed product
- ❌ Pupil photos except where explicitly required by an agreed product
- ❌ Medical information unless explicitly agreed and covered by the product schedule/DPIA
- ❌ SEND details about specific pupils unless required by the product purpose and minimised
- ❌ Safeguarding case notes
- ❌ Parent contact details
- ❌ Financial records (payroll, etc.)

**Safe to upload:**
- ✅ Anonymised data summaries
- ✅ Policy documents
- ✅ Curriculum documents
- ✅ School Development Plan
- ✅ SEF (without pupil names)
- ✅ Anonymised observation feedback

## ⚙️ First-Time Setup Guide

### Step 1: Create Your Organisation
1. Sign in with Google or Microsoft
2. Click "Create New Organisation"
3. Enter your school name and URN
4. Select school type and phase

### Step 2: Invite Your Team
1. Go to Settings → Team
2. Click "Invite Member"
3. Enter email address and select role
4. They'll receive an invitation email

### Step 3: Configure Settings
1. Enable/disable Church School features (SIAMS)
2. Set notification preferences
3. Review privacy settings

### Step 4: Start Using
1. Complete your first self-assessment
2. Scan some evidence documents
3. Create your first action
